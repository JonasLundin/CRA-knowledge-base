# Summary

prEN 40000-1-3 is a horizontal draft European Standard developed by CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 15[^m606][^stan4cra-jtc13]. It establishes normative requirements for manufacturer vulnerability handling processes, machine-readable Software Bills of Materials (SBOM), Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD), and security update distribution under CRA Annex I Part II[^cen-wi-81344].

# Legal effect

This document is a draft European Standard (prEN). It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27.

# Applicability

Applies horizontally to all economic operators manufacturing products with digital elements in scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

prEN 40000-1-3 addresses:
- **Point 1**: Continuous identification and documentation of vulnerabilities and components, including creation and maintenance of a machine-readable SBOM (CycloneDX / SPDX);
- **Point 2**: Procedures to investigate and remediate vulnerabilities without delay;
- **Point 3**: Regular security testing, code audits, and automated static/dynamic analysis;
- **Point 4**: Public disclosure of resolved vulnerabilities through standard security advisories (CSAF / Common Vulnerability Advisory Framework);
- **Point 5**: Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) policies, secure intake channels (security.txt), and reporter communication workflows;
- **Point 6**: Mechanisms for secure, prompt, and tamper-evident delivery of security updates;
- **Point 7**: Dissemination of clear vulnerability remediation instructions and user guidance;
- **Point 8**: Provision of separate security updates that do not bundle unwanted feature modifications.
