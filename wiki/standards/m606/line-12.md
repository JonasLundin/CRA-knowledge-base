# Summary

M/606 Line 12 requests horizontal technical specifications for recording, monitoring, and auditing security-related events, access attempts, and anomalies in products with digital elements[^m606].

# Legal effect

This request entry creates no direct presumption of conformity until standard adoption and OJEU citation.

# Requested scope and mandate

- **Legal basis**: CRA Annex I, Part I, point 2(k).
- **Mandate objective**: Define requirements for secure tamper-resistant audit logs, log formatting, timestamp accuracy, security event generation, and log retention.

# Responsible committee and deliverables

- **Technical Committee**: CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9[^stan4cra-jtc13].
- **Deliverable**: `prEN 40000-1-2` (Work Item 81344).
