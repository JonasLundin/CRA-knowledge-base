# Summary

Annex VII itemises the necessary technical documents and records that manufacturers must assemble to demonstrate compliance with Annex I.

# Legal effect

Establishes mandatory content requirements for technical files subject to market surveillance scrutiny and notified body review.

# Applicability

Applies to all manufacturers of products with digital elements.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex VII requires technical documentation to contain:
1. General description of the product, intended purpose, and versions;
2. Cybersecurity risk assessment (threat model, risk evaluation, and countermeasures);
3. Design specifications, system architecture, flow diagrams, and external interface descriptions;
4. Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) listing top-level software and hardware dependencies and third-party components;
5. List of harmonised standards, common specifications, or certification schemes applied;
6. Test reports, verification records, and vulnerability assessment results;
7. Documented vulnerability handling processes conforming to Annex I Part II;
8. Copy of the EU declaration of conformity and Annex II user instructions.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
