# Summary

Annex VI provides the template for a simplified EU declaration of conformity, allowing physical products to display a short-form statement referencing a web address.

# Legal effect

Allows manufacturers to satisfy declaration disclosure duties via a standardized short statement and direct URL.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers opting for simplified declarations in user documentation.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex VI establishes the simplified model:
- Short-form declaration: 'Hereby, [Manufacturer Name] declares that the product with digital elements [Product Type/Identifier] is in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.'
- Direct and exact internet address where the full EU declaration of conformity (Annex V) can be accessed.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
