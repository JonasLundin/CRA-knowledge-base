# Summary

Article 53 grants market surveillance authorities full access to technical documentation and, under strictly defined conditions, source code to assess CRA conformity.

# Legal effect

Obligates economic operators to provide documentation and data upon request, with stringent confidentiality protections for trade secrets and source code.

# Applicability

Applies to market surveillance authorities and economic operators possessing relevant technical data.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 53 provides:
- Market surveillance authorities have the right to require economic operators to provide all necessary technical documentation, data, and information.
- Access to source code or internal design specifications may be requested only where strictly necessary to verify compliance with Annex I essential requirements and where other means are insufficient.
- Authorities must treat obtained source code, trade secrets, and proprietary information with strict confidentiality under Article 63.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
