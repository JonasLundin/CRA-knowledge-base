# Summary

Under Article 71(2)(b) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Chapter IV (Articles 35 to 51) concerning the notification of conformity assessment bodies applies from 11 June 2026 (18 months post-entry into force).[^cra-art-71] [^cra-art-35]

# Legal effect

Enables Member States to officially designate notifying authorities, evaluate applicant conformity assessment bodies, and formally notify them to the European Commission via the NANDO database ahead of full product application.[^cra-art-35]

# Applicability

Applies to Member State notifying authorities, national accreditation bodies, and conformity assessment organizations applying to become CRA notified bodies.[^cra-art-35]

# Requirements or coverage

From 11 June 2026:
1. National notifying authorities may accept and process applications from conformity assessment bodies;
2. Candidate bodies are assessed against Annex IV / Article 39 competence, independence, and technical criteria;
3. Notifications are published in NANDO, granting bodies legal authority to contract with manufacturers for Module B and Module H assessments;
4. The Notified Bodies Coordination Group is established under Article 51.[^cra-art-35]

# Dates and transitions

- **11 June 2026:** Chapter IV becomes applicable.
