# Summary

Annex II specifies the mandatory technical information, security instructions, and support duration that manufacturers must supply to users with each product.

# Legal effect

Imposes mandatory user-facing disclosure obligations accompanying the product in clear and accessible language.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers supplying products with digital elements to consumers or professional users.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex II requires user instructions to include:
- Manufacturer identification, contact details, and single point of contact for cybersecurity inquiries and vulnerability reporting;
- Clear specification of the intended use and foreseeable security environment;
- Exact end date of the support period (month and year) during which security updates will be provided;
- Instructions on secure commissioning, default configuration, account credentials, and secure decommissioning;
- Guidance on how security updates are received, applied, and verified, including how to configure automatic updates;
- Information on risks associated with end-of-support and continued product operation.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
