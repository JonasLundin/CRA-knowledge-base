# Summary

Under Article 3(19) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, an **authorised representative** means any natural or legal person established within the Union who has received a written mandate from a manufacturer to act on its behalf in relation to specified tasks.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Acts as the designated EU liaison for market surveillance authorities on behalf of non-EU manufacturers under Article 15.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Must be formally established in the EU and hold an explicit written mandate specifying duties such as technical documentation retention and authority cooperation.[^cra-art-3]
