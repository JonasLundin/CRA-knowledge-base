# Summary

Article 4 secures internal market free movement by barring Member States from creating national technical barriers for compliant products with digital elements.

# Legal effect

Directly prohibits national restrictions, bans, or obstacles to making available products complying with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Binds all EU Member States, market surveillance authorities, and regional bodies.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 4 guarantees:
- Member States shall not prohibit, restrict, or impede the making available on the market of products with digital elements that comply with the CRA.
- At trade fairs, exhibitions, and demonstrations, Member States shall not prevent the showing or use of non-compliant products, provided a visible sign clearly indicates that they do not comply and cannot be placed on the market until brought into conformity.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
