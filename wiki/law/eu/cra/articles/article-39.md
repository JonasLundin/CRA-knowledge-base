# Summary

Article 39 establishes the detailed legal, organisational, technical, and ethical requirements that a conformity assessment body must satisfy to become and remain a notified body.

# Legal effect

Imposes mandatory qualification, impartiality, resource, and governance criteria on notified bodies.

# Applicability

Applies to all conformity assessment bodies applying for or holding notification under the CRA.

# Requirements or coverage

Key requirements under Article 39 include:
- **Legal personality**: Must be established under the national law of a Member State and have legal personality.
- **Third-party independence**: Must be an independent third-party body completely separate from the organisation or product it assesses.
- **No commercial conflict**: Body, its top-level management, and assessment personnel shall not be the designer, manufacturer, supplier, installer, purchaser, owner, user, or maintainer of assessed products, nor represent such parties.
- **Technical competence**: Possess necessary personnel with sound technical knowledge, experience in cybersecurity testing, vulnerability analysis, and conformity assessment.
- **Liability insurance**: Must take out liability insurance unless liability is assumed by the Member State.
- **Professional secrecy**: Personnel must observe professional secrecy regarding all information obtained in carrying out tasks.
- **Participation in coordination**: Participate in or ensure assessment personnel are informed of standardisation activities and the notified body coordination group (Article 51).

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
