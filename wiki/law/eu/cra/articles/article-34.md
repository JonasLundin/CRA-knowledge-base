# Summary

Article 34 regulates international conformity assessment under bilateral or multilateral Mutual Recognition Agreements between the EU and third countries.

# Legal effect

Enables recognition of conformity assessment certificates issued by third-country bodies designated under international agreements.

# Applicability

Applies to third-country conformity assessment bodies and manufacturers operating under international trade agreements with the Union.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 34 provides:
- Conformity assessment certificates, declarations, and technical documentation issued in third countries under valid Union Mutual Recognition Agreements are recognised as equivalent to Union procedures.
- Third-country bodies must meet competence and independence requirements equivalent to CRA notified bodies.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
