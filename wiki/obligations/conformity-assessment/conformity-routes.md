# Summary

Article 32 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 specifies the conformity assessment routes that manufacturers must follow to demonstrate compliance with Annex I essential requirements before placing a product with digital elements on the EU market.[^cra-art-32]

# Legal effect

Manufacturers must complete the specific assessment route mandated for their product classification. Affixing a CE mark without completing the correct route renders the product non-compliant and subject to immediate withdrawal or recall.[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Determined by the product's classification under Annex III and Annex IV:

### 1. Default Products with Digital Elements (Article 32(1))
- **Route:** **Module A** (Internal Control). Self-assessment by the manufacturer.

### 2. Important Products with Digital Elements — Class I (Article 32(2))
- **Route A (Self-assessment):** **Module A**, provided that harmonised standards cited in the OJEU or common specifications are applied in full;
- **Route B (Third-party assessment):** **Module B + C** (EU-Type Examination + Conformity to Type) or **Module H** (Full Quality Assurance) if harmonised standards do not exist, are not applied, or only partially applied;
- **Route C (Certification):** A European cybersecurity certificate issued under a scheme under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act) at assurance level at least 'substantial'.

### 3. Important Products with Digital Elements — Class II (Article 32(3))
- **Route A (Third-party assessment):** Mandatory third-party assessment via **Module B + C** or **Module H**;
- **Route B (Certification):** A European cybersecurity certificate issued under a scheme under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 at assurance level at least 'substantial'.

### 4. Critical Products with Digital Elements (Article 32(4))
- **Route:** Mandatory European cybersecurity certificate under a European cybersecurity certification scheme at assurance level 'high', or specific assessment routes specified by Commission delegated acts.[^cra-art-32] [^cra-annex-6]

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
