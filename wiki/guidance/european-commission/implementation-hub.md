# Summary

The *Cyber Resilience Act Implementation Hub* serves as the European Commission's central portal coordinating all preparatory activities, delegated acts, implementing decisions, standardisation roadmaps, and stakeholder guidance leading up to full CRA application [^ec-cra-hub].

# Legal effect

The Implementation Hub is an official informational resource and non-binding guidance portal [^ec-cra-hub]. Summaries, roadmaps, and explanatory articles hosted on the hub do not carry binding legal force.

# Applicability

Horizontal reference point for all market participants, EU Member States, standardisation bodies (CEN, CENELEC, ETSI), notified bodies, and international partners.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The Hub aggregates and structures official implementation workflows [^ec-cra-hub]:

- **Secondary Legislation Tracker:** Status of delegated acts (e.g., exclusions under Regulation (EU) 2025/1535, notification dissemination delays under Regulation (EU) 2026/881) and implementing acts (e.g., technical definitions of important/critical products under Regulation (EU) 2025/2392).
- **Standardisation Request M/606:** Tracking the progress of harmonised European standards across CEN, CENELEC, and ETSI.
- **Reporting Infrastructure:** Information on the operationalisation of the ENISA Single Reporting Platform (SRP) under Article 16.
- **Support Measures & Funding:** Information on EU-level funding instruments (Digital Europe Programme) to support SMEs, microenterprises, and open-source communities.
- **National Implementation Coordination:** Overview of Member State designations of notifying authorities and market surveillance authorities.

# Dates and transitions

- **Portal Launch:** January 2025.
- **Regular Refresh:** Updated continuously to reflect newly adopted secondary legislation, consultation calls, and guidance releases.
