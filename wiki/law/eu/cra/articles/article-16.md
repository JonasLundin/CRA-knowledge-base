# Summary

Article 16 mandates the creation and secure administration of the electronic Single Reporting Platform (SRP) by ENISA for centralising CRA notifications.

# Legal effect

Establishes ENISA's operational obligation to build, host, and maintain the SRP with end-to-end encryption and cybersecurity safeguards.

# Applicability

Applies to ENISA, designated CSIRT coordinators, market surveillance authorities, and notifying manufacturers.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 16 establishes:
- **Platform creation**: ENISA establishes, develops, and maintains a secure electronic Single Reporting Platform.
- **Security standards**: Platform must use end-to-end encryption and strict access controls to protect sensitive vulnerability and incident data.
- **Dissemination and routing**: Automatically transmits notifications to the relevant designated CSIRT coordinators and market surveillance authorities.
- **Dissemination delay**: Designated CSIRT coordinators may delay notification dissemination to other CSIRTs on justified cybersecurity-related grounds specified in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 September 2026.
