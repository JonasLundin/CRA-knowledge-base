# Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) Legal Corpus

This section contains the structured legal reference for Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act) and its supplementing and implementing secondary acts.

## Structure

- [Articles](articles/index.md): All 71 articles of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 covering general provisions, economic operator obligations, conformity assessment, notified bodies, market surveillance, penalties, and final provisions.
- [Annexes](annexes/index.md): All eight annexes establishing essential cybersecurity requirements, user instructions, product classifications, declarations of conformity, technical documentation, and conformity assessment modules.
- [Secondary Legislation](secondary-legislation/index.md): Delegated and implementing regulations adopted under CRA empowerments, alongside the future secondary legislation register.
