# Summary

Articles 52 and 53 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 integrate CRA enforcement into the Union market surveillance framework established by Regulation (EU) 2019/1020. Member States must designate competent national Market Surveillance Authorities (MSAs) responsible for verifying product cybersecurity compliance.[^cra-art-52] [^cra-art-53] [^reg-2019-1020]

# Legal effect

Designated MSAs possess binding investigative and enforcement powers, including the authority to inspect technical documentation, audit source code where strictly necessary, order corrective actions, ban non-compliant products, and impose administrative fines.[^cra-art-52]

# Applicability

Applies to all economic operators (manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, and distributors) and products with digital elements made available on the EU market.[^cra-art-52]

# Requirements or coverage

Under Articles 52–53 and Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, MSAs:

1. **Conduct compliance checks:** Carry out document reviews, laboratory testing, reverse-engineering, and vulnerability assessments of products on the market;
2. **Access technical data:** Require manufacturers to provide technical documentation, risk assessments, and SBOMs within specified deadlines;
3. **Cross-border cooperation:** Cooperate with MSAs in other Member States through the Information and Communication System for Market Surveillance (ICSMS) and EU ADCO (Administrative Cooperation Group) on CRA;
4. **Coordination with CSIRTs:** Establish continuous coordination mechanisms with national CSIRTs and ENISA to ensure alignment between incident handling and market enforcement.[^cra-art-52] [^cra-art-53]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
