# Summary

Article 24 establishes tailored, light-touch obligations for open-source software stewards supporting free and open-source software development for commercial use.

# Legal effect

Imposes governance and coordinated vulnerability disclosure duties on stewards without subjecting them to CE marking, declarations of conformity, or third-party audits.

# Applicability

Applies to legal entities qualifying as open-source software stewards under Article 3(16).

# Requirements or coverage

Key obligations for open-source software stewards include:
- **Cybersecurity policy**: Establish and document a cybersecurity policy to foster the development of secure open-source software.
- **Coordinated vulnerability disclosure**: Establish a coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD) process and provide a point of contact for vulnerability reporting.
- **Information sharing**: Cooperate with market surveillance authorities and facilitate information sharing within the developer community.
- **Exclusion of CE/audit burden**: Open-source software stewards are not subject to conformity assessment procedures, CE marking rules, or technical documentation requirements applicable to manufacturers.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
