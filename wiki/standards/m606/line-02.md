# Summary

M/606 Line 2 requests horizontal standardisation specifications ensuring products with digital elements are made available on the market with an appropriate level of cybersecurity and without known exploitable vulnerabilities[^m606].

# Legal effect

This request line sets the standardisation mandate; it does not confer legal presumption of conformity until a harmonised standard is adopted and cited in the OJEU.

# Requested scope and mandate

- **Legal basis**: CRA Annex I, Part I, point 2(a).
- **Mandate objective**: Define engineering practices, baseline validation, and testing criteria to verify the absence of known exploitable vulnerabilities prior to market placement.

# Responsible committee and deliverables

- **Technical Committee**: CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9[^stan4cra-jtc13].
- **Deliverable**: `prEN 40000-1-2` (Work Item 81344).
