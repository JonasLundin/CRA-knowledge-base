# Summary

M/606 Line 6 requests horizontal technical standards for protecting the integrity of data, commands, programs, configuration settings, and execution flow in products with digital elements against unauthorised modification or corruption[^m606].

# Legal effect

This request entry creates no direct presumption of conformity until standard adoption and OJEU citation.

# Requested scope and mandate

- **Legal basis**: CRA Annex I, Part I, point 2(e).
- **Mandate objective**: Specify mechanisms for cryptographic integrity verification (signatures, hashes, MACs), secure boot chains, and configuration validation.

# Responsible committee and deliverables

- **Technical Committee**: CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9[^stan4cra-jtc13].
- **Deliverable**: `prEN 40000-1-2` (Work Item 81344).
