# Summary

Article 48 guarantees due process for manufacturers by mandating that Member States provide transparent appeal mechanisms against notified body decisions.

# Legal effect

Obligates Member States to establish legal appeal procedures against negative conformity assessment decisions.

# Applicability

Applies to Member States, notified bodies, and applicant manufacturers.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 48 establishes:
- Member States shall ensure that an appeals procedure against decisions of notified bodies is available to affected manufacturers and applicants.
- The procedure must be transparent, independent, and accessible.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
