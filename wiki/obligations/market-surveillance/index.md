# Market Surveillance & Enforcement

Overview of market surveillance powers, corrective actions, product recalls, and administrative fines under Chapter V and Article 64 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

## Concepts

- [Market Surveillance Framework](market-surveillance-framework.md) — Authority powers, coordination, and interaction with Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 (Articles 52–53)
- [Corrective Action, Restriction & Recall](corrective-action-and-recall.md) — Procedures for addressing non-compliant products and cybersecurity risks (Articles 54, 57, 58)
- [Penalties & Administrative Fines](penalties-and-administrative-fines.md) — Statutory fine ceilings up to €15M / 2.5% of global turnover under Article 64
