# Summary

Under Article 3(32) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **substantial modification** means a change to the product with digital elements following its placing on the market, which affects the compliance of the product with digital elements with the essential requirements set out in Section 1 of Annex I or results in a modification of the intended purpose for which the product with digital elements has been assessed.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Triggers manufacturer obligations under Article 18 for any person performing the modification and terminates legacy grandfathering protections under Article 69(2).[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Distinguished from routine security updates and maintenance patches which remediate vulnerabilities without altering assessed functionality or introducing new security risks.[^cra-art-3]
