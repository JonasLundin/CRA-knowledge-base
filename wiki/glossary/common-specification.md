# Summary

Under Article 3(29) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **common specification** means a technical specification as defined in Article 2(4) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 adopted by the Commission by means of implementing acts where harmonised standards are absent, delayed, or insufficient.[^cra-art-3] [^cra-art-27]

# Legal effect

Products complying with common specifications enjoy the same presumption of conformity with Annex I essential requirements as products complying with OJEU-cited harmonised standards (Article 27(2)).[^cra-art-27]

# Requirements or coverage

Adopted as fallback regulatory benchmarks when European Standardisation Organisations fail to deliver requested standards within M/606 timelines.[^cra-art-27]
