# Summary

Point 16 of Annex III Class I covers internet-connected toys with interactive features (microphones, cameras, location tracking, or interactive communication capabilities).[^cra-annex-3] [^reg-2025-2392]

# Legal effect

Subject to Class I conformity assessment under Article 32(2) to provide heightened protections for minors against eavesdropping, unauthenticated pairing, tracking, and unauthorized remote control.[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Applies to products covered by Toy Safety legislation (Directive 2009/48/EC or its successor) that include:
- Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular data connections;
- Microphones, cameras, or biometric speech-recognition sensors;
- GPS or positioning tracking modules.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Conformity assessment route:** Module A (with full application of cited harmonised standards) or third-party Module B+C / Module H (Article 32(2));[^cra-art-32]
- **Relevant vertical standards:** M/606 line 33 (ETSI EN 304-633);
- **Child privacy & safety:** Mandatory pairing authentication, clear visual indicators when audio/video recording is active, encryption in transit and at rest, and parental control options.

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full product conformity applies from 11 December 2027.
