# Summary

Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2 Directive) establishes horizontal cybersecurity risk-management and reporting obligations for essential and important entities across critical sectors, creates national Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs), and institutionalises the CSIRTs network and Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) across the Union.[^dir-2022-2555] The Cyber Resilience Act relies directly on the NIS2 CSIRT infrastructure for routing mandatory product vulnerability and incident reports under CRA Article 14 and aligns its vulnerability handling requirements with NIS2 CVD frameworks.[^cra-art-14][^cra-annex-1]

# Interaction and scope relationship

The relationship between NIS2 and the CRA is complementary and distinct in scope:
- **NIS2:** Regulates cybersecurity at the **entity and organizational level** (operators of critical infrastructure, cloud providers, managed service providers, public administration, etc.), focusing on operational resilience and risk management.[^dir-2022-2555]
- **CRA:** Regulates cybersecurity at the **product level** across the entire supply chain and lifecycle of products with digital elements made available on the EU market.[^cra-annex-1]

# When the instrument applies

NIS2 applies whenever:
- An entity qualifying as an essential or important entity operates within the sectors defined in Annexes I and II of Directive (EU) 2022/2555;[^dir-2022-2555]
- National CSIRTs receive incident notifications, coordinate cross-border incident response, or act as trusted coordinators for coordinated vulnerability disclosure under Article 12;[^dir-2022-2555]
- ENISA maintains the European vulnerability database pursuant to NIS2 Article 12(2).[^dir-2022-2555]

# Exclusion or concurrency with CRA

NIS2 and CRA apply concurrently without mutual exclusion. An entity subject to NIS2 (e.g., a digital service provider or energy operator) that also manufactures or markets products with digital elements must comply with NIS2 for its operational security and with CRA for the products it places on the market.[^dir-2022-2555][^cra-art-14]

# Technical or procedural satisfaction of CRA elements

1. **CSIRT reporting integration:** Under CRA Article 14, manufacturers must notify actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents affecting products with digital elements to the Single Reporting Platform (SRP). The SRP automatically and simultaneously transmits notifications to the relevant national CSIRTs designated under NIS2 Article 10 in the affected Member States and to ENISA.[^cra-art-14]
2. **Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD):** CRA Annex I Part II point 5 requires manufacturers to implement a coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy. This obligation directly interfaces with the national CVD frameworks established under NIS2 Article 12, allowing manufacturers and security researchers to interact with designated CSIRTs as neutral intermediaries.[^dir-2022-2555][^cra-annex-1]
3. **Supply chain security due diligence:** Under NIS2 Article 21(2)(d), regulated entities must manage cybersecurity risks in their supply chains. Procuring products with digital elements carrying the CE mark under CRA (and obtaining the accompanying software bill of materials and EU declaration of conformity) provides verifiable assurance that the product meets EU baseline cybersecurity requirements.[^dir-2022-2555]
4. **European vulnerability database:** Actively exploited vulnerabilities reported under CRA Article 14 and remediated with security patches may be registered in the European vulnerability database managed by ENISA under NIS2 Article 12(2).[^dir-2022-2555][^cra-art-14]

# Relevant provisions

- **Directive (EU) 2022/2555:** Articles 10 & 11 (CSIRTs and CSIRT tasks), Article 12 (Coordinated vulnerability disclosure and European vulnerability database), Article 15 (CSIRTs network), Article 21 (Cybersecurity risk-management measures), Article 23 (Reporting obligations).[^dir-2022-2555]
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/2847:** Article 14 (Notification obligations of manufacturers), Article 15 (Single Reporting Platform), Article 16 (Voluntary notifications), Annex I Part II (Vulnerability handling requirements), Recitals 29, 30, 48, 70–74.[^cra-art-14][^cra-annex-1]

# Dates and transition

- Directive (EU) 2022/2555 entered into force on 16 January 2023; Member States were required to transpose it by 17 October 2024.[^dir-2022-2555]
- CRA Article 14 reporting mechanisms through the Single Reporting Platform to CSIRTs take effect on 11 September 2026 (18 months after entry into force).[^cra-art-14]
