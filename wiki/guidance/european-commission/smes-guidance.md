# Summary

The European Commission's *Guidance for SMEs and Start-ups* outlines dedicated support mechanisms, simplified administrative expectations, and financial and technical resources available under Article 25 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 [^ec-cra-smes].

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and explanatory [^ec-cra-smes]. It illustrates how proportionality and support mechanisms operate under the CRA but does not exempt SMEs from fulfilling Annex I essential requirements.

# Applicability

Applies to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), microenterprises, and technology start-ups manufacturing or distributing products with digital elements in the Union.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The guidance highlights the specific accommodations and support measures available to smaller economic operators [^ec-cra-smes]:

- **Proportionality and Simplified Documentation (Article 25(1)):** Encourages simplified technical documentation formats tailored to the size and resources of microenterprises and SMEs without compromising cybersecurity integrity.
- **Regulatory Sandboxes and Testbeds (Article 25(4)):** Guidance on participating in national and EU-supported cybersecurity sandboxes, allowing SMEs to test novel products and verify compliance in a supervised environment before market launch.
- **Financial and Technical Assistance:** Overview of EU funding schemes (including Digital Europe Programme grants) supporting vulnerability testing, conformity assessment subsidies, and cybersecurity training for SME staff.
- **Guidelines and Tools:** Standardised templates for risk assessments, user instructions, and declarations of conformity.
- **Proportionality in Enforcement (Article 64):** Principles guiding market surveillance authorities to take into account enterprise size, market share, and economic capacity when evaluating non-compliance and determining administrative fines.

# Dates and transitions

- **Support Rollout:** Ongoing throughout 2025–2027 via European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIHs) and national SME contact points.
