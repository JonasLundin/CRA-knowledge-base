# Summary

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 establishes technical descriptions and objective scoping criteria for each product category listed in Annex III (Important Products Class I and Class II) and Annex IV (Critical Products) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Legal effect

Provides legally binding technical definitions establishing whether a specific hardware or software product falls under Class I, Class II, or Critical product classifications, thereby determining the mandatory conformity assessment procedure.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers, notified bodies, and market surveillance authorities classifying products under Annexes III and IV.

# Requirements or coverage

- **Article 1 (Definitions)**: Sets out technical definitions including Common Criteria (CC) and Common Evaluation Methodology (CEM) aligned with Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/482 (EUCC).
- **Article 2 (Technical Descriptions)**: Sets out granular technical boundaries, functional characteristics, and exclusions for:
  - Annex III Class I categories (identity management, browsers, password managers, antivirus, VPNs, network management, SIEM, boot managers, PKI software, firewalls, routers, smart home security devices, smart toys, wearables);
  - Annex III Class II categories (operating systems, hypervisors, industrial firewalls/IDS/IPS, microprocessors, HSMs, smartcards, IACS, telecommunications routers/modems);
  - Annex IV Critical categories (security boxes, smart meter gateways, secure elements, cryptographic HSMs).
- **Article 3**: Enters into force on 21 December 2025 and applies from 11 December 2027.
