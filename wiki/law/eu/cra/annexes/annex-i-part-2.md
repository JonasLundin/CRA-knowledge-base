# Summary

Annex I Part II sets out mandatory organisational and technical processes for identifying, reporting, remediating, and disclosing vulnerabilities throughout the product support period.

# Legal effect

Imposes continuous post-market lifecycle obligations on manufacturers for the duration of the support period.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers of all products with digital elements throughout their defined support period.

# Requirements or coverage

Key obligations under Annex I Part II include:
1. **Vulnerability identification**: Continuously identify and document vulnerabilities and components, including drawing up a software bill of materials (SBOM) covering top-level dependencies.
2. **Prompt remediation**: Address and remediate vulnerabilities without delay, providing security updates free of charge.
3. **Security testing**: Perform regular security testing and vulnerability assessments throughout the support period.
4. **Coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD)**: Establish and publish a CVD policy and provide a contact point for security researchers.
5. **Secure update distribution**: Distribute security updates securely, providing cryptographic validation, authenticity checks, and clear advisory information.
6. **Information sharing**: Share vulnerability information and mitigation guidance with relevant CSIRTs and users.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027 (with Article 14 reporting applying from 11 September 2026).
