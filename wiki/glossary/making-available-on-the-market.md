# Summary

Under Article 3(10) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, **making available on the market** means any supply of a product with digital elements for distribution or use on the Union market in the course of a commercial activity, whether in return for payment or free of charge.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Triggers the downstream obligations of distributors and importers across the distribution chain and sets the geographic boundary for EU market surveillance enforcement.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Requires commercial supply into the EU, whether physical delivery, online distribution, or digital download.[^cra-art-3]
