# Summary

Article 19 defines the due diligence and verification obligations of importers placing products with digital elements from third countries on the Union market.

# Legal effect

Prohibits importers from placing non-compliant products on the market and establishes direct supply chain responsibility.

# Applicability

Applies to all importers established in the Union placing third-country products with digital elements on the market.

# Requirements or coverage

Key obligations under Article 19 include:
- **Verification**: Ensure the manufacturer has carried out conformity assessment, drawn up technical documentation, affixed CE marking, and provided Annex II user instructions.
- **Prohibition on non-compliant placing**: Refrain from placing a product on the market if it presents a significant cybersecurity risk or fails essential requirements, and inform the manufacturer and market surveillance authorities.
- **Identification and contact details**: Indicate importer name, registered trade name, and contact address on the product, packaging, or accompanying documentation.
- **Storage and transport**: Ensure storage or transport conditions do not jeopardize compliance with Annex I essential requirements.
- **Retention of documentation**: Keep a copy of the EU declaration of conformity at the disposal of authorities for 10 years or the support period.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
