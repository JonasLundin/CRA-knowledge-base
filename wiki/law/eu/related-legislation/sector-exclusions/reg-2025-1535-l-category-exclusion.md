# Summary

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 is a delegated act adopted under Cyber Resilience Act Article 2(5).[^reg-2025-1535] It formally excludes specified categories of two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles (and their type-approved systems, components, and separate technical units) governed by Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 from the scope of the CRA, where those products are subject to equivalent type-approval cybersecurity requirements.[^reg-2025-1535][^cra-art-2]

# Interaction and scope relationship

The act operationalises the dynamic sectoral exclusion mechanism established in CRA Article 2(5):
- Products specifically listed in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 are excluded from all CRA obligations (including CRA Annex I essential requirements, Article 14 reporting, and CRA CE marking).[^reg-2025-1535]
- Manufacturers of excluded L-category vehicles follow the dedicated type-approval cybersecurity standards under the Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 framework.[^reg-168-2013]

# When the instrument applies

Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 applies whenever:
- An L-category vehicle (or an in-vehicle electronic system/component) subject to Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 is evaluated to determine whether it is exempt from the CRA;[^reg-2025-1535]
- The vehicle or system meets the specific type-approval cybersecurity criteria (e.g., equivalent CSMS and software update management compliance) set out in the delegated act.[^reg-2025-1535]

# Exclusion or concurrency with CRA

The exclusion is specific and legally binding for the defined vehicle classes:[^reg-2025-1535]
- **Excluded:** L-category vehicles (such as high-speed motorcycles and quadricycles) that hold type-approval under rules incorporating equivalent cybersecurity provisions (e.g. alignment with UN Regulation No 155/156 principles).[^reg-2025-1535]
- **Non-excluded:** L-category subcategories not subject to equivalent cybersecurity type-approval, standalone portable consumer electronics used on two-wheelers (e.g., aftermarket handlebar GPS navigators or generic bluetooth intercoms), remain subject to the CRA.[^cra-art-2]

# Technical or procedural satisfaction of CRA elements

1. **Equivalence threshold:** The exclusion is predicated on the legal finding that the sectoral type-approval regime provides a level of cybersecurity protection at least equivalent to that ensured by CRA Annex I.[^cra-art-2]
2. **Conformity and reporting route:** For excluded vehicles, conformity is verified by national vehicle approval authorities and technical services, and security incidents are managed under vehicle safety recall and type-approval procedures rather than the CRA Single Reporting Platform.[^reg-2025-1535]

# Relevant provisions

- **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535:** Article 1 (Subject matter and scope of excluded L-category products), Article 2 (Conditions for exclusion).[^reg-2025-1535]
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/2847:** Article 2(5) (Delegation empowerment for L-category vehicle exclusion), Recital 23.[^cra-art-2]
- **Regulation (EU) No 168/2013:** Type-approval requirements for categories L1e–L7e.[^reg-168-2013]

# Dates and transition

- Adopted by the Commission in 2025 following the entry into force of the CRA.[^reg-2025-1535]
- Applies in synchronization with the CRA entry into application on 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-2]
