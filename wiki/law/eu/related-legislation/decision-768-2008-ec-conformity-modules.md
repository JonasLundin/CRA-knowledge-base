# Summary

Decision No 768/2008/EC provides the model legislative blueprint for the Union's New Legislative Framework (NLF), establishing standardized definitions of economic operators, core supply-chain obligations, and modular conformity assessment procedures (Modules A through H).[^decision-768-2008] The Cyber Resilience Act directly transposes and adapts these NLF reference modules into CRA Annex VI to regulate conformity assessment for products with digital elements.[^cra-art-32][^cra-annex-6]

# Interaction and scope relationship

Decision No 768/2008/EC is a sui generis act of general application addressed to Union institutions to ensure consistency across internal market legislation. It does not impose obligations directly on economic operators; rather, its standard modules and definitions are given direct binding force through their enactment in Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.[^decision-768-2008]

# When the instrument applies

The NLF provisions articulated in Decision No 768/2008/EC apply under the CRA whenever:
- A manufacturer determines the applicable conformity assessment procedure under CRA Article 32;[^cra-art-32]
- Economic operators (manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, distributors) fulfill traceability, marking, and supply-chain due diligence duties under CRA Chapter III;[^decision-768-2008]
- A notified body conducts type-examination or quality assurance audits pursuant to CRA Annex VI.[^cra-annex-6]

# Exclusion or concurrency with CRA

Decision No 768/2008/EC provides the structural architectural baseline for CRA. CRA incorporates NLF modules while customizing them for digital products (e.g., adding software bill of materials (SBOM), secure development lifecycles, and ongoing vulnerability handling to technical documentation).[^cra-annex-6]

# Technical or procedural satisfaction of CRA elements

CRA Annex VI establishes three conformity assessment procedures adapted from Decision No 768/2008/EC Annex II:[^cra-annex-6]

1. **Module A (Internal Production Control):** Adapted in Annex VI Part I. The manufacturer self-assesses conformity, maintains technical documentation, and signs the EU declaration of conformity. Available for default (unclassified) products with digital elements and important Class I products where harmonised standards or certified schemes are applied in full.[^cra-art-32][^cra-annex-6]
2. **Module B + Module C (EU-Type Examination + Conformity to Type):** Adapted in Annex VI Parts II & III. A notified body assesses technical design and examines a representative product specimen (Module B), followed by internal production control ensuring conformity to the approved type (Module C). Applied to important and critical products.[^cra-art-32][^cra-annex-6]
3. **Module H (Full Quality Assurance):** Adapted in Annex VI Part IV. A notified body approves and audits the manufacturer's quality management and secure development lifecycle systems. Applicable as an alternative third-party route for important Class I, Class II, and critical products.[^cra-art-32][^cra-annex-6]

# Relevant provisions

- **Decision No 768/2008/EC:** Annex I (General principles and reference provisions for economic operators), Annex II (Conformity assessment modules A, B, C, H).[^decision-768-2008]
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/2847:** Articles 13–24 (Obligations of economic operators), Article 32 (Conformity assessment procedures), Annex VI (Conformity assessment procedures), Recitals 46, 59, 63.[^cra-art-32][^cra-annex-6]

# Dates and transition

- Decision No 768/2008/EC was adopted on 9 July 2008.[^decision-768-2008]
- CRA conformity assessment modules under Annex VI become applicable on 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-32]
