# Summary

Article 69 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 establishes transitional provisions governing products placed on the market prior to the general application date, legacy certificates, and phased application milestones.[^cra-art-69]

# Legal effect

Products placed on the market before 11 December 2027 are generally grandfathered and are not required to retroactively obtain CRA CE marking or complete new conformity assessments, provided they are not substantially modified.[^cra-art-69]

# Applicability

1. **Legacy products:** Products placed on the market prior to 11 December 2027 may continue to be made available on the market without full CRA conformity certification.[^cra-art-69]
2. **Subsequent substantial modifications:** If a grandfathered product undergoes a substantial modification on or after 11 December 2027, the modified product must comply with all CRA requirements.[^cra-art-69]
3. **Reporting obligations on legacy products:** Article 14 reporting obligations apply from 11 September 2026 to all products placed on the market (including legacy products) if they are actively maintained or made available.[^cra-art-69] [^cra-art-71]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Chapter IV (Notified Bodies):** Applies from 11 June 2026 to allow Member States to designate and notify conformity assessment bodies ahead of full application.[^cra-art-71]
- **Article 14 (Vulnerability & Incident Reporting):** Applies from 11 September 2026, 15 months before the full essential product requirements.[^cra-art-71]
- **General application:** Full application of Annex I essential requirements and conformity assessment from 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-71]
- **Certificates issued under prior schemes:** Existing certificates issued under European cybersecurity certification schemes (e.g. EUCC) retain validity as specified in Article 69(4).[^cra-art-69]

# Dates and transitions

- **10 December 2024:** Entry into force.
- **11 June 2026:** Notification of conformity assessment bodies applies.
- **11 September 2026:** Article 14 mandatory reporting applies.
- **11 December 2027:** Full CRA application.
