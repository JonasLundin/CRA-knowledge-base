# Summary

Default products with digital elements encompass the broad majority (estimated over 90%) of connected hardware and software products placed on the EU market that are not specifically listed in Annex III (Important Class I / Class II) or Annex IV (Critical).[^cra-art-32]

# Legal effect

Manufacturers of default products must comply with all Annex I essential requirements and Article 14 reporting, but may demonstrate conformity entirely through internal control self-assessment (Module A) without mandatory third-party involvement.[^cra-art-32] [^cra-annex-6]

# Applicability

Applies to any connected digital product outside Annex III/IV, including:
- General consumer smart home appliances (smart fridges, washing machines, lighting);
- Standard business application software (spreadsheets, ERP client software, CRM tools);
- Video games, media streaming devices, smart TVs, and audio peripherals;
- General IoT sensors, smart garden tools, and connected office printers.[^cra-art-32]

# Requirements or coverage

Manufacturers must:
1. Conduct and document a cybersecurity risk assessment (Article 13(2));
2. Comply with Annex I Part I (security properties) and Part II (vulnerability handling) essential requirements;[^cra-annex-1]
3. Maintain technical documentation and an SBOM (Article 31, Annex VII);
4. Complete the **Module A (Internal Production Control)** conformity assessment procedure (Annex VI);
5. Draw up the EU declaration of conformity and affix the CE marking without a notified body number;
6. Declare a support period, provide free security updates, and adhere to Article 14 mandatory reporting via the SRP.[^cra-art-32]

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full Annex I compliance and Module A assessment apply from 11 December 2027.
