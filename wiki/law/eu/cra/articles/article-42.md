# Summary

Article 42 defines the formal application process for conformity assessment bodies seeking notification under the CRA.

# Legal effect

Establishes mandatory application filing requirements and evidence submission for conformity assessment bodies.

# Applicability

Applies to conformity assessment bodies submitting applications to national notifying authorities.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 42 requires:
- A conformity assessment body must submit an application for notification to the notifying authority of the Member State in which it is established.
- The application must include a description of the conformity assessment activities, modules, and product categories for which it claims competence, accompanied by an accreditation certificate issued by a national accreditation body (or equivalent documentary evidence).

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
