# Official Guidance Catalogue

This directory indexes non-binding official guidance, implementation frameworks, technical specifications, and explanatory publications supporting the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act - CRA).

Guidance documents explain operational aspects, technical methodologies, reporting interfaces, and compliance pathways. Guidance is non-binding and cannot alter or supersede the legal obligations established in the CRA or its binding implementing and delegated acts.

## Guidance Publishers

- [European Commission](./european-commission/index.md) - Commission communications, implementation guidelines (including C(2026) 5252), thematic policy pages, and official FAQs.
- [ENISA](./enisa/index.md) - European Union Agency for Cybersecurity guidance on the Single Reporting Platform (SRP), secure-by-design engineering, standards mappings, and notified body competencies.
- [National Authorities](./national-authorities/index.md) - Verified guidance published by EU Member State market surveillance authorities and cybersecurity agencies.
