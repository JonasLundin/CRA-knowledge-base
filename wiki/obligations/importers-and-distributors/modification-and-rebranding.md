# Summary

Article 18 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 establishes that an importer, distributor, or any other natural or legal person is considered a manufacturer and becomes subject to all manufacturer obligations under Article 13 if they rebrand a product or make a substantial modification.[^cra-art-18]

# Legal effect

When Article 18 triggers, the entity can no longer rely on downstream distributor/importer exemptions. It assumes full legal liability for Annex I compliance, risk assessments, technical documentation, conformity assessment, CE marking, and Article 14 vulnerability reporting.[^cra-art-18] [^cra-art-13]

# Applicability

Article 18 applies in two distinct operational scenarios:

1. **Rebranding / White-labelling:** An entity places a product with digital elements on the market under its own name or trademark (Article 18, point (a));
2. **Substantial modification:** An entity carries out a substantial modification to a product with digital elements already placed on the market (Article 18, point (b)).[^cra-art-18]

# Requirements or coverage

When becoming a deemed manufacturer under Article 18, the entity must:

- Compile or take ownership of the technical documentation and SBOM;
- Ensure that the conformity assessment procedure has been completed under its name;
- Issue a new EU declaration of conformity under Annex VIII;
- Determine and declare the support period and supply free security updates;
- Fulfill Article 14 reporting duties for any actively exploited vulnerabilities or severe incidents affecting the product.[^cra-art-18]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
