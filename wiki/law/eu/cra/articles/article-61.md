# Summary

Article 61 establishes the constitutional framework governing the European Commission's power to adopt delegated acts under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Legal effect

Confers delegated law-making powers on the Commission subject to standard 5-year renewable periods and European Parliament / Council veto powers.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission, European Parliament, and Council of the European Union.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 61 sets out:
- **Scope of delegation**: Power to adopt delegated acts is conferred on the Commission regarding Article 2(5) (exclusions), Article 7(2) and (3) (Annex III important products), Article 8(1) and (2) (Annex IV critical products and mandatory certification), Article 16(2) (SRP delay grounds), and Article 32(8) (conformity assessment amendments).
- **Duration**: Conferred for a period of five years from 11 December 2024, tacitly extendable unless opposed.
- **Revocation and objection**: The European Parliament or Council may revoke the delegation at any time or object to a delegated act within a period of two months (extendable by two months).

# Dates and transitions

Enters into force and applies from 11 December 2024.
