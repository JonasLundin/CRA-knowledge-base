# Summary

Article 23 creates supply chain traceability obligations, requiring economic operators to maintain records of suppliers and purchasers.

# Legal effect

Legally obligates economic operators to retain and disclose supply chain identity data upon request from market surveillance authorities.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, and distributors.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 23 requires economic operators to be able to identify to market surveillance authorities:
- Any economic operator who has supplied them with a product with digital elements;
- Any economic operator to whom they have supplied a product with digital elements.
- Operators must maintain this traceability information for at least 10 years after supply or for the duration of the product support period, whichever is longer.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
