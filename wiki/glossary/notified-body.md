# Summary

Under Article 3(36) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **notified body** means a conformity assessment body designated in accordance with Article 43 and other relevant Union harmonisation legislation.[^cra-art-3] [^cra-art-35]

# Legal effect

Authorized to conduct third-party conformity assessments (Module B EU-type examination and Module H quality system audits) and issue EU-type examination certificates.[^cra-art-35]

# Requirements or coverage

Must satisfy statutory independence, technical competence, and testing capacity criteria under Chapter IV and be published in NANDO.[^cra-art-35]
