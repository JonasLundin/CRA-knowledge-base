# Summary

Article 59 encourages cross-border administrative cooperation through joint market surveillance investigations, product testing, and enforcement campaigns.

# Legal effect

Provides legal authority for joint cross-border enforcement actions between authorities of different Member States.

# Applicability

Applies to Member State market surveillance authorities and the Commission.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 59 allows:
- Market surveillance authorities to set up joint activities aimed at promoting compliance, identifying non-compliance, raising awareness, and providing guidance on CRA requirements.
- Joint testing of products, shared laboratory facilities, and coordinated cross-border market inspections.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
