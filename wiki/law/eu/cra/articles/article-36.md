# Summary

Article 36 governs the designation and responsibilities of national notifying authorities in each EU Member State.

# Legal effect

Obligates Member States to establish competent notifying authorities and define their operational remit.

# Applicability

Applies to EU Member State governments and designated notifying authorities.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 36 establishes:
- Member States must designate a notifying authority responsible for setting up and carrying out assessment, notification, and monitoring procedures for conformity assessment bodies.
- Member States may decide that assessment and monitoring shall be carried out by a national accreditation body within the meaning of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
