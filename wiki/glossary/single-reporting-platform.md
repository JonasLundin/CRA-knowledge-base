# Summary

The **Single Reporting Platform (SRP)** is the centralised, secure electronic reporting platform established, maintained, and operated by ENISA pursuant to Article 14 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.[^cra-art-14] [^enisa-srp-docs]

# Legal effect

Serves as the exclusive statutory entry point for manufacturers submitting Article 14 early warnings, notifications, and final reports across the European Union.[^cra-art-14]

# Requirements or coverage

Provides end-to-end encrypted submission, automated simultaneous routing to lead national CSIRTs and ENISA, and enforces dissemination delay protocols under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881.[^cra-art-14]
