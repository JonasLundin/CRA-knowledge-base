# National Authority CRA Guidance

This section indexes verified non-binding guidance, implementation roadmaps, and technical advisories published by national cybersecurity agencies and market surveillance authorities in EU Member States regarding Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

National guidance explains domestic coordination, national CSIRT reporting interfaces, awareness initiatives, and supervisory expectations. National guidance is non-binding and does not alter the uniform application of EU law.

Note: Detailed Member State legislative acts, authority designations, and domestic penalty provisions are maintained under `wiki/jurisdictions/eu-member-states/`.
