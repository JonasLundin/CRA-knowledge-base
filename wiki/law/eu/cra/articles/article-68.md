# Summary

Article 68 amends Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 on the type-approval of two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles to align cybersecurity requirements with CRA.

# Legal effect

Integrates cybersecurity into vehicle type-approval and interfaces with Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 exclusions.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers and type-approval authorities for L-category vehicles.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 68 amends Regulation (EU) No 168/2013:
- Introduces cybersecurity requirements into the EU type-approval framework for L-category vehicles.
- Coordinates with Article 2(5) exclusions where vehicle cybersecurity is fully covered under type-approval legislation.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
