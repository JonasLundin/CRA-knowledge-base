# Sectoral Exclusions Under CRA Article 2

Overview of dedicated sector-specific Union harmonisation legislation excluded from the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act) pursuant to Article 2(2)–(5).

## Sector Instruments

- [Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (Medical Devices)](reg-2017-745-medical-devices.md) — Medical devices and standalone medical software (MDR, Article 2(2)(a))
- [Regulation (EU) 2017/746 (In Vitro Diagnostics)](reg-2017-746-in-vitro-diagnostics.md) — In vitro diagnostic medical devices and diagnostic software (IVDR, Article 2(2)(b))
- [Regulation (EU) 2019/2144 (Motor Vehicles)](reg-2019-2144-motor-vehicles.md) — Type-approved motor vehicles, trailers, systems, and ECUs under GSR / UN R155 / UN R156 (Article 2(2)(d))
- [Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (Civil Aviation)](reg-2018-1139-civil-aviation.md) — Civil aircraft, avionics, ATM/ANS systems, and certified drones under EASA / Part-IS (Article 2(2)(c))
- [Directive 2014/90/EU (Marine Equipment)](dir-2014-90-eu-marine-equipment.md) — Marine equipment placed on board EU flag ships bearing the wheel mark (Article 2(3))
- [Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 (L-Category Vehicles)](reg-168-2013-l-category-vehicles.md) — Two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles type-approval framework (Article 2(5))
- [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 (L-Category Exclusion Act)](reg-2025-1535-l-category-exclusion.md) — Delegated act specifying excluded L-category vehicle classes (Article 2(5))
