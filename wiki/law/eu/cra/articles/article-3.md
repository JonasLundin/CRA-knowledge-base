# Summary

Article 3 establishes the authoritative legal definitions used throughout the Cyber Resilience Act, defining roles, product types, lifecycle concepts, and risk parameters.

# Legal effect

Establishes legally binding interpretations for terms governing compliance, liability, and enforcement across the Union.

# Applicability

Controls the interpretation of all provisions of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and secondary acts adopted thereunder.

# Requirements or coverage

Key statutory definitions include:
- **Product with digital elements**: Any software or hardware product and its remote data processing solutions, including software or hardware components placed on the market separately.
- **Remote data processing solution**: Any data processing at a distance designed and developed by the manufacturer or under its responsibility, without which the product cannot perform one of its functions.
- **Economic operators**: Manufacturer, authorised representative, importer, and distributor.
- **Open-source software steward**: Any legal entity, other than a manufacturer, whose purpose or objective is to systematically provide support for the sustained development of free and open-source software qualifying as products with digital elements intended for commercial activities.
- **Actively exploited vulnerability**: A vulnerability for which there is reliable evidence that an execution of malicious code has occurred in a system without permission of the system owner.
- **Severe incident**: Any incident that causes or is capable of causing severe operational disruption or material damage.
- **Substantial modification**: A change following placing on the market which affects the compliance of the product with essential requirements or modifies its intended purpose.
- **Support period**: The period during which the manufacturer is required to ensure that vulnerabilities of the product are handled effectively.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027; definitions applied earlier for Chapter IV (11 June 2026) and Article 14 (11 September 2026).
