# Summary

Under Article 3(34) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the **support period** means the period during which the manufacturer is required to ensure that vulnerabilities of the product with digital elements are handled effectively and in accordance with the essential requirements set out in Section 2 of Annex I.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Establishes a legally enforceable duration during which the manufacturer must provide free security updates and active vulnerability management pursuant to Article 13(8)–(10).[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Must be determined based on reasonably expected product lifetime and clearly communicated to consumers before purchase.[^cra-art-3]
