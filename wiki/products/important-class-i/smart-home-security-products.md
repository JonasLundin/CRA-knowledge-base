# Summary

Point 15 of Annex III Class I designates smart home products with security functionality—such as smart door locks, connected video doorbells, domestic security cameras, and intrusion alarm systems—as Important Products (Class I).[^cra-annex-3] [^reg-2025-2392]

# Legal effect

Mandates Class I conformity assessment under Article 32(2) to protect physical domestic entry points, video feeds, and alarm signaling against unauthorized bypass and cyber tampering.[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Smart electronic door locks and access control systems;
- IP cameras and video doorbells designed for residential security;
- Connected home burglar alarms, motion sensors, and security hub controllers.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Conformity assessment route:** Module A (with full application of cited harmonised standards) or third-party Module B+C / Module H (Article 32(2));[^cra-art-32]
- **Relevant vertical standards:** M/606 line 32 (ETSI EN 304-632);
- **Physical & logical protection:** Resistance against replay attacks, encrypted video transmission, secure pairing protocols, and local bypass controls during network outages.

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full product conformity applies from 11 December 2027.
