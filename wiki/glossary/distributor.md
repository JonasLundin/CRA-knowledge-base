# Summary

Under Article 3(21) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **distributor** means any natural or legal person in the supply chain, other than the manufacturer or the importer, that makes a product with digital elements available on the Union market.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Obliged under Article 17 to exercise due care, verify CE marking and accompanying documentation, and cooperate in corrective actions.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Encompasses wholesalers, retailers, retail websites, and platform resellers operating in the EU distribution chain.[^cra-art-3]
