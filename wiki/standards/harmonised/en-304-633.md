# Summary

EN 304 633 is an ETSI draft European Standard under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 33[^m606], establishing essential cybersecurity requirements for internet-connected toys under CRA Annex III Class I point 18[^etsi-docbox-633].

# Legal effect

This document is an enquiry draft. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** presumption of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Interactive toys with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular connectivity;
- Toys equipped with cameras, microphones, location tracking, or biometric sensors;
- Connected companion applications controlling toy hardware.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

EN 304 633 addresses:
- Child data protection, privacy by design, and parental consent telemetry management;
- Prohibition of unauthenticated Bluetooth or open Wi-Fi connections that allow nearby strangers to connect;
- Strict mutual authentication between the toy and authorized companion devices;
- Visual indicators whenever camera, microphone, or tracking sensors are active;
- Factory reset and permanent data wiping mechanisms for audio/video recordings.
