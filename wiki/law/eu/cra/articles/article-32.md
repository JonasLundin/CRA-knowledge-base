# Summary

Article 32 assigns mandatory conformity assessment modules based on product classification: default products, Important Class I, Important Class II, and Critical products.

# Legal effect

Binds manufacturers to execute the designated conformity assessment procedure prior to CE marking and market placement.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers of all products with digital elements.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 32 establishes a four-tiered conformity assessment framework:
1. **Default products (unlisted)**: Internal control procedure (Module A) set out in Annex VIII Part I.
2. **Important products Class I (Annex III List 1)**:
   - Module A (Internal control) IF harmonised standards or common specifications covering all essential requirements are applied in full;
   - EU-type examination (Module B) followed by conformity to EU-type (Module C); or
   - Full quality assurance (Module H).
3. **Important products Class II (Annex III List 2)**:
   - EU-type examination (Module B) + conformity to type (Module C); or
   - Full quality assurance (Module H). (Self-assessment / Module A is prohibited).
4. **Critical products (Annex IV)**:
   - Mandatory European cybersecurity certificate under an adopted scheme (Article 8(1)); or
   - Module B+C or Module H where no applicable certification scheme exists.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
