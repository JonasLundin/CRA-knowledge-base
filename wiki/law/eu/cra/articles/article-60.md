# Summary

Article 60 empowers market surveillance authorities to conduct simultaneous coordinated market surveillance actions ('sweeps') across online and offline marketplaces.

# Legal effect

Authorises simultaneous Union-wide coordinated inspections to detect systemic CRA non-compliance.

# Applicability

Applies to market surveillance authorities, online marketplaces, and economic operators.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 60 establishes:
- Market surveillance authorities may decide to conduct simultaneous coordinated control actions ('sweeps') of particular product categories or online interfaces to check CRA compliance.
- Sweeps may cover online sales platforms, product listings, and digital distribution channels.
- Participating authorities may publish sweep results in an aggregated and transparent manner.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
