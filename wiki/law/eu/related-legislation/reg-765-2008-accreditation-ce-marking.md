# Summary

Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 forms the cornerstone of the Union's New Legislative Framework (NLF) by establishing rules on the operation and organization of accreditation of conformity assessment bodies across Member States and laying down the horizontal legal principles and graphic format governing the CE marking.[^reg-765-2008] Under the Cyber Resilience Act, it governs the accreditation baseline for CRA notified bodies and the fundamental rules for affixing the CE marking to products with digital elements.[^cra-art-28][^cra-art-39]

# Interaction and scope relationship

The relationship between Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 is structural and concurrent. Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 provides the Union-wide infrastructure for accreditation and CE marking rules, while the CRA specifies product-specific cybersecurity requirements, conformity routes, and notified body criteria.[^cra-art-28]

# When the instrument applies

Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 applies whenever:
- A National Accreditation Body (NAB) evaluates, accredits, or monitors conformity assessment bodies seeking designation as CRA notified bodies under Chapter IV;[^cra-art-39]
- A manufacturer affixes the CE marking to a product with digital elements, its packaging, or accompanying documentation following successful CRA conformity assessment;[^cra-art-28]
- Peer evaluation of national accreditation bodies is conducted under Article 10 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.[^reg-765-2008]

# Exclusion or concurrency with CRA

The two instruments apply concurrently. Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 does not contain cybersecurity product rules, and the CRA explicitly references and incorporates Article 30 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 without derogation.[^cra-art-28]

# Technical or procedural satisfaction of CRA elements

1. **Accreditation of notified bodies:** Under CRA Article 39 and Article 43, obtaining an accreditation certificate issued by a national accreditation body pursuant to Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 provides statutory presumption that a conformity assessment body meets the competence, independence, and impartiality criteria required for CRA notification in NANDO.[^cra-art-39]
2. **General principles of CE marking:** CRA Article 28(1) mandates that the CE marking affixed to products with digital elements must strictly respect the general principles set out in Article 30 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 (including exclusive indication of Union conformity, prohibition of misleading marks, and graphic specifications in Annex II).[^cra-art-28]
3. **Identification of notified body:** Where a notified body is involved in the conformity assessment (Modules B+C or Module H), the notified body identification number is affixed alongside the CE marking pursuant to CRA Article 29(3) and Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 Article 30.[^cra-art-28]

# Relevant provisions

- **Regulation (EC) No 765/2008:** Articles 1–12 (Accreditation framework and national accreditation bodies), Article 30 and Annex II (General principles and graphic specifications of the CE marking).[^reg-765-2008]
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/2847:** Article 28 (General principles of the CE marking), Article 29 (Rules and conditions for affixing the CE marking), Article 39 (Requirements relating to notified bodies), Recitals 60, 68.[^cra-art-28][^cra-art-39]

# Dates and transition

- Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 has been fully applicable across the EU since 1 January 2010.[^reg-765-2008]
- CRA provisions on CE marking and notified body accreditation apply in alignment with the CRA conformity assessment architecture from 11 December 2027 (with notified body designation provisions applying earlier from 11 June 2026 under CRA Article 71).[^cra-art-39]
