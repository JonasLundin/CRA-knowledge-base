# Summary

Article 54 establishes the national evaluation procedure where a market surveillance authority has sufficient reason to believe a product presents a significant cybersecurity risk.

# Legal effect

Authorises national authorities to mandate corrective actions, product withdrawals, or recalls, and triggers Union-wide notifications.

# Applicability

Applies to national market surveillance authorities and relevant economic operators.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 54 prescribes:
- Where an authority evaluates a product and finds it presents a significant cybersecurity risk or non-compliance with Annex I, it shall require the economic operator to take all appropriate corrective actions within a prescribed reasonable period.
- Corrective actions include bringing the product into compliance, withdrawing it from the market, or recalling it.
- If the operator fails to take adequate corrective action, the authority adopts provisional measures prohibiting or restricting availability.
- The authority immediately informs the Commission and all other Member States of the results and ordered measures.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
