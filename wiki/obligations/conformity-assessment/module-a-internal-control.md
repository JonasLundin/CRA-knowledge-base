# Summary

Module A (Internal Control) is the self-assessment conformity assessment procedure set out in Annex VI of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. The manufacturer ensures and declares on its sole responsibility that the product satisfies all applicable Annex I requirements.[^cra-annex-6]

# Legal effect

Permits placing products on the EU market without prior inspection, testing, or certification by a third-party notified body, provided all internal documentation and process controls are complete.[^cra-annex-6]

# Applicability

- Mandatory default route for **Default Products with Digital Elements** (Article 32(1));
- Permitted route for **Important Products with Digital Elements — Class I** *only if* the manufacturer has fully applied harmonised standards cited in the OJEU or common specifications (Article 32(2)).[^cra-art-32]

# Requirements or coverage

Under Annex VI Module A, the manufacturer must:

1. **Draw up technical documentation:** Establish technical documentation containing the cybersecurity risk assessment, SBOM, test results, and vulnerability handling processes (Annex VII);
2. **Implement development & manufacturing controls:** Take all measures necessary so that the development, production, and vulnerability handling processes ensure compliance of the manufactured/released products with Annex I;
3. **Affix CE marking & issue DoC:** Affix the CE mark without any notified body number and draw up the written EU declaration of conformity (Annex VIII);
4. **Document retention:** Maintain the technical documentation and EU declaration of conformity for 10 years or the declared support period, whichever is longer.[^cra-annex-6]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
