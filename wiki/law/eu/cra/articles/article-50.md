# Summary

Article 50 mandates the Commission to establish and facilitate exchange-of-experience forums between Member State notifying authorities.

# Legal effect

Obligates the European Commission to facilitate administrative cooperation and consistent notification practices.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission and Member State notifying authorities.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 50 provides:
- The Commission shall provide for the organisation of exchange of experience between Member States' national authorities responsible for notification policy and monitoring of notified bodies.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
