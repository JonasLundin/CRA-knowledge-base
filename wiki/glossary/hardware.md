# Summary

Under Article 3(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, **hardware** means a physical electronic information system, or parts thereof, capable of processing, storing, or transmitting digital data.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Establishes the physical scope of connected physical devices, semiconductor integrated circuits, computing appliances, and IoT equipment subject to CRA essential cybersecurity requirements.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Covers complete devices as well as physical sub-assemblies and integrated circuits intended to handle digital signals.[^cra-art-3]
