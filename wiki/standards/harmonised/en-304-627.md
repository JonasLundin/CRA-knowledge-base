# Summary

EN 304 627 is an ETSI draft European Standard under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 27[^m606], specifying essential cybersecurity requirements for routers, modems intended for internet connection, and network switches under CRA Annex III Class I point 12[^etsi-docbox-627].

# Legal effect

This document is an enquiry draft. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** presumption of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Consumer and enterprise routers and residential gateways;
- DSL, cable, fiber (ONT), and cellular (5G/4G) modems;
- Managed and unmanaged Ethernet switches.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

EN 304 627 addresses:
- Prohibition of universal default administrative credentials;
- Administrative management interface encryption (HTTPS/SSH mandatory; insecure HTTP/Telnet disabled by default);
- DNS rebinding defenses and UPnP exposure restrictions on WAN interfaces;
- Automated and cryptographically verified firmware update mechanisms;
- Physical port security, VLAN segmentation integrity, and Denial-of-Service resilience.
