# Critical Products with Digital Elements

Overview of critical product categories listed in Annex IV of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and technical descriptions under Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.

## Mandatory Certification Route

Under Article 32(4), critical products are subject to the highest level of regulatory scrutiny and must obtain:
- A European cybersecurity certificate issued under a European cybersecurity certification scheme (e.g. EUCC) pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2019/881 at assurance level **'high'**; or
- Specific conformity assessment routes laid down in Commission delegated acts.
