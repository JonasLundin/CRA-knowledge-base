# Summary

Annex V prescribes the mandatory elements and structure required in the formal EU declaration of conformity.

# Legal effect

Establishes the legal template that manufacturers must complete to attest conformity under Article 28.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers issuing EU declarations of conformity.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex V mandates the following elements:
1. Unique identification of the product with digital elements (name, type, batch, version, or serial number);
2. Name and address of the manufacturer and, where applicable, its authorised representative;
3. Statement that the declaration is issued under the sole responsibility of the manufacturer;
4. Object of the declaration (identification allowing traceability, including image where appropriate);
5. Statement of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and other relevant Union harmonisation legislation;
6. References to relevant harmonised standards, European cybersecurity certification schemes, or common specifications used;
7. Where applicable, name, identification number, and description of tasks performed by the notified body, and certificate issued;
8. Place and date of issue, name, function, and signature of the authorised person.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
