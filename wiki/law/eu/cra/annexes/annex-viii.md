# Summary

Annex VIII defines the operative rules and manufacturer/notified body tasks for the conformity assessment procedures established under Decision No 768/2008/EC.

# Legal effect

Binds manufacturers and notified bodies to specific procedural steps to establish conformity before market entry.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers and notified bodies carrying out CRA conformity assessments.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex VIII details three modules:
- **Module A (Internal control)**:
  - Manufacturer compiles technical documentation (Annex VII), ensures manufacturing compliance, conducts internal verification, affixes CE marking, and draws up EU declaration of conformity. No notified body involved.
- **Module B (EU-type examination) + Module C (Conformity to type)**:
  - Module B: Notified body examines technical documentation and tests representative product specimen, issuing an EU-type examination certificate.
  - Module C: Manufacturer ensures manufacturing conformity with the approved type and essential requirements.
- **Module H (Conformity based on full quality assurance)**:
  - Notified body assesses and audits manufacturer's comprehensive cybersecurity quality management system (design, development, vulnerability handling, production). Notified body performs ongoing surveillance audits.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
