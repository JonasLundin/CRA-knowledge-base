# Summary

A manufacturer is any natural or legal person who develops or manufactures products with digital elements or has products with digital elements designed, developed, or manufactured, and markets them under their name or trademark, whether for payment or free of charge.[^cra-art-3] Article 13 sets out their primary lifecycle obligations.[^cra-art-13]

# Legal effect

Manufacturers bear the primary responsibility for ensuring that products with digital elements comply with Annex I essential cybersecurity requirements throughout the product's design, development, production, and declared support period.[^cra-art-13]

# Applicability

Applies to all entities placing products with digital elements on the EU market under their name or trademark, as well as entities that carry out substantial modifications under Article 18.[^cra-art-13]

# Requirements or coverage

Manufacturers must fulfill the following cumulative duties:

1. **Design and manufacture:** Ensure products meet Annex I Part I (security properties) and Part II (vulnerability handling) essential requirements (Article 13(1));
2. **Cybersecurity risk assessment:** Carry out and document an assessment of risks and threats (Article 13(2));
3. **Due diligence on components:** Vet third-party hardware and software components (Article 13(5)–(6));
4. **Technical documentation:** Compile and maintain technical documentation and SBOMs for 10 years or the support period (Article 13(3), (4));
5. **Conformity assessment:** Perform the applicable conformity assessment procedure, draw up the EU declaration of conformity, and affix the CE marking (Article 13(1));
6. **Support period and security updates:** Determine the support period, provide security updates free of charge, and separate security updates from feature updates (Article 13(8)–(10), (14));
7. **Coordinated vulnerability disclosure:** Establish a public contact point and vulnerability disclosure policy (Article 13(11));
8. **Mandatory reporting:** Report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents via the Single Reporting Platform under Article 14.[^cra-art-13]

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- All other Article 13 manufacturer obligations apply from 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-13]
