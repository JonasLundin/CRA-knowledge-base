# Summary

`prEN 40000-1-3` (*Cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements — Part 1-3: Vulnerability handling*) is a Type B horizontal draft European Standard developed by CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 (PT 2) under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 15[^m606][^stan4cra-jtc13]. It establishes normative requirements for manufacturer vulnerability handling processes, machine-readable Software Bills of Materials (SBOM), Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD), and security update distribution under CRA Annex I Part II[^cen-wi-81344].

# Legal effect

This document is a draft European Standard (`prEN`) advancing through formal approval following its CEN-CENELEC Public Enquiry (which closed on 9 February 2026)[^cen-wi-81344]. Unlike foundational Parts `1-1` (*Vocabulary*) and `1-2` (*Principles for cyber resilience*)—for which CEN-CENELEC project records indicate no standalone OJEU citation is expected—`prEN 40000-1-3` **is targeted for citation in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU)** to confer a legal presumption of conformity with CRA Annex I Part II under Article 27(1) once formally adopted and assessed by the European Commission[^cen-wi-81344]. Until OJEU citation occurs, it confers **no** legal presumption of conformity.

# Applicability

Applies horizontally to all economic operators manufacturing products with digital elements in scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

`prEN 40000-1-3` addresses all eight points of CRA Annex I Part II:
- **Point 1**: Continuous identification and documentation of vulnerabilities and components, including creation and maintenance of a machine-readable SBOM (such as CycloneDX or SPDX);
- **Point 2**: Procedures to investigate, triage, and remediate vulnerabilities without delay in relation to the risks;
- **Point 3**: Regular and effective security testing, reviews, code audits, and automated static/dynamic analysis;
- **Point 4**: Public disclosure of resolved vulnerabilities once a security update is available, using structured advisories (such as CSAF);
- **Point 5**: Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) policies, secure intake channels (`security.txt`), and reporter communication workflows;
- **Point 6**: Mechanisms for secure, prompt, and tamper-evident distribution of security updates;
- **Point 7**: Free-of-charge dissemination of security updates accompanied by clear user advisory guidance;
- **Point 8**: Provision of security updates separately from functional/feature updates where technically feasible.
