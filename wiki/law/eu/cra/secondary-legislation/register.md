# Summary

This register provides a complete inventory of all legislative empowerments conferred on the European Commission under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act), categorised by legal status: adopted acts, planned/in-progress acts, and standby empowerments.

# Legal effect

Documents the legal basis, procedural requirements (delegated vs. implementing), committee procedures, and status for all CRA secondary instruments.

# Applicability

Serves as the central tracking register for regulatory developments, secondary rules, and official guidance impacting economic operators and authorities.

- Delegation period: 5 years from 11 December 2024 (tacitly renewable).
