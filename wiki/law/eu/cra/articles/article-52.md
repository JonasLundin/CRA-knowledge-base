# Summary

Article 52 establishes the market surveillance architecture for products with digital elements, integrating Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 and designating national market surveillance authorities.

# Legal effect

Confers enforcement powers on national market surveillance authorities to investigate, test, and sanction non-compliant products with digital elements.

# Applicability

Applies to Member State market surveillance authorities, economic operators, and CSIRTs.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 52 establishes:
- Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 applies to products with digital elements falling within the scope of the CRA.
- Member States must designate one or more market surveillance authorities responsible for CRA enforcement.
- Market surveillance authorities must cooperate closely with CSIRTs designated under Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2), data protection authorities, and other sector regulators.
- Market surveillance authorities may agree with CSIRTs to exchange information regarding actively exploited vulnerabilities and incidents.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
