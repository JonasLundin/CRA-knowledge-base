# Related EU Legislation and Interactions

Overview of Union acts interacting with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act), including standardisation, market surveillance, New Legislative Framework (NLF) modules, European certification schemes, horizontal cybersecurity directives, AI systems, general product safety, radio equipment transition, collective redress, product liability, and sectoral exclusions.

## Standardisation & Market Infrastructure

- [Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 (European Standardisation)](reg-1025-2012-standardisation.md) — Standardisation requests (M/606), ESOs, and presumption of conformity (CRA Article 27)
- [Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 (Market Surveillance)](reg-2019-1020-market-surveillance.md) — Horizontal market surveillance framework, ICSMS, and customs controls (CRA Chapter V, Article 67)
- [Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 (Accreditation & CE Marking)](reg-765-2008-accreditation-ce-marking.md) — Notified body accreditation rules and CE marking principles (CRA Articles 28, 29, 39)
- [Decision No 768/2008/EC (Conformity Modules)](decision-768-2008-ec-conformity-modules.md) — Reference NLF conformity assessment modules (Modules A, B+C, H) and operator obligations (CRA Chapter III, Annex VI)

## Cybersecurity Architecture & Schemes

- [Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act)](reg-2019-881-cybersecurity-act.md) — ENISA mandate, Single Reporting Platform (SRP), and European certification framework (CRA Articles 14, 27, 32)
- [Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/482 (EUCC Scheme)](reg-2024-482-eucc.md) — Common Criteria European cybersecurity scheme and CRA third-party assessment recognition (CRA Articles 27, 32)
- [Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2 Directive)](dir-2022-2555-nis2.md) — Entity-level security, CSIRTs routing, and Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) (CRA Articles 14, 15, Annex I)

## Product Safety, AI & Sector Interfaces

- [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)](reg-2024-1689-ai-act.md) — High-risk AI systems, deemed cybersecurity compliance, and single conformity assessment (CRA Article 12)
- [Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (General Product Safety Regulation)](reg-2023-988-general-product-safety.md) — Lex generalis safety net and cybersecurity risk carve-outs (CRA Article 11)
- [Directive 2014/53/EU (Radio Equipment Directive)](dir-2014-53-eu-radio-equipment.md) — Sectoral spectrum and safety rules amended to carve out CRA products (CRA Article 68)
- [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 (RED Cybersecurity & EN 18031)](reg-2022-30-red-cybersecurity.md) — Transitional wireless cybersecurity regime superseded by CRA on 11 December 2027

## Enforcement, Redress & Liability

- [Directive (EU) 2020/1828 (Representative Actions Directive)](dir-2020-1828-representative-actions.md) — Collective consumer redress for CRA infringements (CRA Article 69)
- [Directive (EU) 2024/2853 (Product Liability Directive Recast)](dir-2024-2853-product-liability.md) — Strict civil liability for defective digital products, software, and lack of security updates

## Sector Exclusions

- [Sectoral Exclusions (CRA Article 2)](sector-exclusions/index.md) — Dedicated regimes for medical devices (MDR/IVDR), motor vehicles (GSR), civil aviation (EASA), marine equipment (MED), and L-category vehicles
