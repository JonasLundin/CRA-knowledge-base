# Summary

Under Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the manufacturer must draw up an EU Declaration of Conformity (DoC) stating that the product with digital elements complies with the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in Annex I.[^cra-art-28]

# Legal effect

By drawing up the EU Declaration of Conformity, the manufacturer assumes full legal responsibility for the product's compliance with all applicable Union harmonisation legislation.[^cra-art-28]

# Applicability

Mandatory for every product with digital elements placed on the EU market, across default, important, and critical categories.[^cra-art-28]

# Requirements or coverage

Under Annex VIII, the EU Declaration of Conformity must contain:

1. **Product identification:** Name, type, batch, or serial number;
2. **Manufacturer identity:** Name, registered trade name/trademark, and contact address;
3. **Sole responsibility statement:** Explicit declaration that the DoC is issued under the sole responsibility of the manufacturer;
4. **Object of declaration:** Identification of the product allowing traceability;
5. **Legislation conformity:** Reference to Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and any other applicable EU acts (e.g. RED, AI Act);
6. **Standards & specifications:** References to relevant harmonised standards or common specifications used;
7. **Notified body details (where applicable):** Name, identification number, and conformity assessment certificate details for Module B, H, or European certification schemes;
8. **Language requirements:** Translated into the official languages required by the Member States where the product is placed on the market.[^cra-art-28] [^cra-annex-8]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
