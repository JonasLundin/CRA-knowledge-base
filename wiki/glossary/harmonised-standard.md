# Summary

Under Article 3(27) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **harmonised standard** means a European standard adopted on the basis of a request made by the Commission for the application of Union harmonisation legislation, as defined in Article 2(1)(c) of Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012.[^cra-art-3] [^reg-1025-2012]

# Legal effect

When a reference to a harmonised standard is officially published in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU), compliance with its clauses confers a legal **presumption of conformity** with the corresponding essential requirements of Annex I (Article 27(1)).[^cra-art-27]

# Requirements or coverage

A standard only confers presumption of conformity once cited in the OJEU, within the precise scope and any restrictions published by the Commission.[^cra-art-27]
