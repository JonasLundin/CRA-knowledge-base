# Summary

Point 1 of Annex III Class II covers general-purpose operating systems designed for servers, desktop computers, laptops, and mobile smartphones or tablets.[^cra-annex-3] [^reg-2025-2392]

# Legal effect

Classified under Class II due to their central role as foundational platforms. Manufacturers **cannot** self-assess under Module A; they must undergo mandatory third-party assessment or obtain an EU cybersecurity certificate under Article 32(3).[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Applies to:
- General-purpose server operating systems;
- Desktop and laptop operating systems;
- Smartphone and consumer mobile operating systems;
- Enterprise virtualisation hypervisor management OSs.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Mandatory conformity assessment route:** **Module B + C** (EU-Type Examination + Conformity to Type) or **Module H** (Full Quality Assurance) evaluated by a notified body, or European cybersecurity certificate (Article 32(3));[^cra-art-32]
- **Relevant vertical standards:** M/606 line 26 (ETSI EN 304-626);
- **Core security requirements:** Hardware-enforced memory safety, mandatory access controls (MAC), kernel integrity verification, secure enclave isolation, and encrypted storage.

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full product conformity applies from 11 December 2027.
