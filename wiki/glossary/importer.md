# Summary

Under Article 3(20) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, an **importer** means any natural or legal person established in the Union who places on the market a product with digital elements that bears the name or trademark of a person established outside the Union.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Assumes legal responsibility under Article 16 for verifying that third-country manufacturers have completed conformity assessments and compiled technical documentation prior to EU market entry.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Must be established within the EU and is identifiable on the product packaging/documentation.[^cra-art-3]
