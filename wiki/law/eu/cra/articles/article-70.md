# Summary

Article 70 requires the European Commission to periodically evaluate and review the application, effectiveness, and impact of the Cyber Resilience Act.

# Legal effect

Imposes a recurring reporting duty on the Commission towards the European Parliament, Council, and EESC.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission, Member States, and European Parliament.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 70 establishes:
- By **11 December 2030** and every 36 months thereafter, the Commission shall submit a comprehensive evaluation report on the application of the CRA to the European Parliament and the Council.
- The evaluation must specifically examine:
  - The functioning and effectiveness of Article 14 reporting obligations and the Single Reporting Platform;
  - The impact on SMEs, microenterprises, and open-source software development;
  - The coverage and effectiveness of product classifications in Annexes III and IV;
  - The availability and competence of notified bodies across the Union.
- Reports must be accompanied by legislative proposals where appropriate.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027 (first formal report due by 11 December 2030).
