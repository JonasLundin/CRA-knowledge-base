# Summary

Article 17 governs reporting interactions with national security, law enforcement, and cross-border cooperation within the CSIRTs Network.

# Legal effect

Protects national security prerogatives and regulates information exchange between CSIRTs, ENISA, and market surveillance authorities.

# Applicability

Applies to Member States, CSIRTs, market surveillance authorities, and the CSIRTs Network.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 17 establishes:
- Notification obligations under Article 14 do not require disclosure of information contrary to essential national security, defence, or public security interests.
- Information shared via the SRP may be exchanged with the CSIRTs Network and national CSIRTs in accordance with Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2).
- Market surveillance authorities receive summary information to perform regulatory enforcement without compromising technical vulnerability confidentiality.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 September 2026.
