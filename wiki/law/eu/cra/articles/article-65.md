# Summary

Article 65 integrates the CRA into the European representative actions framework under Directive (EU) 2020/1828 for collective consumer protection.

# Legal effect

Grants qualified entities legal standing to initiate collective representative actions against economic operators for CRA breaches affecting consumer interests.

# Applicability

Applies to qualified consumer protection entities, economic operators, and EU consumers.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 65 establishes:
- Directive (EU) 2020/1828 applies to representative actions brought against infringements by economic operators of CRA provisions that harm or may harm the collective interests of consumers.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
