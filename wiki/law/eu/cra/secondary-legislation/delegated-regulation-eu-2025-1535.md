# Summary

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 supplements Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 by excluding products with digital elements that fall within the scope of Regulation (EU) No 168/2013 (type-approval of two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles) from the Cyber Resilience Act.

# Legal effect

Establishes a statutory exclusion from CRA essential requirements and conformity assessment for L-category vehicles and components whose cybersecurity is comprehensively governed by vehicle type-approval legislation.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers, importers, and distributors of two- or three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles subject to Regulation (EU) No 168/2013.

# Requirements or coverage

- **Article 1**: Excludes the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 for products with digital elements falling within the scope of Regulation (EU) No 168/2013.
- **Article 2**: Enters into force on the twentieth day following publication in the Official Journal (18 November 2025).

# Dates and transitions

- Adopted by the Commission on 29 July 2025.
- Published in the Official Journal (OJ L, 2025/1535) on 29 October 2025.
- Enters into force on 18 November 2025; aligns application with the general CRA date of 11 December 2027.
