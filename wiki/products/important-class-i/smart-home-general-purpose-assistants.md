# Summary

Point 14 of Annex III Class I covers smart home general-purpose virtual assistants, including connected smart speakers, displays, and virtual assistant software controlling home devices.[^cra-annex-3] [^reg-2025-2392]

# Legal effect

Subject to Class I conformity assessment under Article 32(2), focusing on audio/video stream privacy, voice-command authentication, and companion cloud backend security.[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Smart speakers with integrated voice assistants;
- Smart home hubs featuring voice, natural language, or visual interaction;
- Virtual assistant software installed on consumer devices to control home environments.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Conformity assessment route:** Module A (with full application of cited harmonised standards) or third-party Module B+C / Module H (Article 32(2));[^cra-art-32]
- **Relevant vertical standards:** M/606 line 31 (ETSI EN 304-631);
- **Remote processing integration:** Cloud speech-processing engines under manufacturer control are remote data processing solutions under Article 3(2) and must comply with Annex I essential requirements.

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full product conformity applies from 11 December 2027.
