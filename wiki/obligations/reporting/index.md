# Article 14 Reporting Obligations

Overview of mandatory reporting workflows, timelines, Single Reporting Platform (SRP) routing, and user notification rules under Article 14 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

## Concepts

- [Article 14 Reporting Overview](article-14-reporting.md) — Core reporting triggers for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents (Article 14)
- [Early Warning (24h)](early-warning-24h.md) — First mandatory report stage within 24 hours of becoming aware (Article 14(2)(a), (4)(a))
- [Vulnerability Notification (72h)](vulnerability-notification-72h.md) — Second stage vulnerability report within 72 hours (Article 14(2)(b))
- [Severe Incident Notification (72h)](severe-incident-notification-72h.md) — Second stage severe incident report within 72 hours (Article 14(4)(b))
- [Intermediate Report (14d)](intermediate-report-14d.md) — Status update upon request or within 14 days of corrective measure availability (Article 14(2)(c))
- [Final Report (1 Month)](final-report-one-month.md) — Comprehensive technical report within one month of remediation or incident closure (Article 14(2)(e), (4)(e))
- [SRP Routing & Dissemination](srp-routing-and-dissemination.md) — ENISA Single Reporting Platform architecture and dissemination delay rules under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881
- [User Notifications](user-notifications.md) — Obligations to notify users of incidents and security mitigations (Article 14(8))
