# Summary

Article 46 authorizes the European Commission to investigate cases where there is doubt concerning the competence or compliance of a notified body.

# Legal effect

Grants investigatory powers to the Commission and enables binding implementing acts requesting notification withdrawal.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission, Member States, notifying authorities, and investigated notified bodies.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 46 provides:
- The Commission investigates all cases where it doubts, or doubt is brought to its attention regarding, the competence of a notified body or its continued fulfillment of requirements.
- The notifying authority must provide the Commission with all relevant information.
- Where the Commission determines a notified body does not meet requirements, it adopts an implementing act requesting the notifying authority to take necessary corrective measures, including withdrawal of notification if necessary.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
