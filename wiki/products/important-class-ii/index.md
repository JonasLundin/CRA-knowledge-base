# Important Products with Digital Elements — Class II

Overview of high-risk product categories listed in Annex III Class II of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and technical descriptions under Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.

## Mandatory Third-Party Conformity Route

Under Article 32(3), Class II products CANNOT use Module A self-assessment. They must undergo mandatory third-party assessment via:
- **Module B + C** (EU-Type Examination followed by Conformity to Type based on Internal Production Control); or
- **Module H** (Full Quality Assurance audited by a Notified Body); or
- A European cybersecurity certificate at assurance level at least 'substantial'.
