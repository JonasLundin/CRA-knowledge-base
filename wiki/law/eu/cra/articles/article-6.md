# Summary

Article 6 creates the core substantive market condition: products with digital elements may only be placed on the market if they meet the essential requirements set out in Annex I.

# Legal effect

Imposes a mandatory legal condition precedent for placing products with digital elements on the Union market.

# Applicability

Applies to all products with digital elements within scope and their manufacturers.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 6 establishes:
- Products with digital elements shall only be made available on the market where they meet the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in Annex I Part I (security properties) and Part II (vulnerability handling).
- Compliance must be based on a cybersecurity risk assessment conducted by the manufacturer pursuant to Article 13(2).
- Products must be delivered without known exploitable vulnerabilities and designed according to security-by-design and security-by-default principles.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
