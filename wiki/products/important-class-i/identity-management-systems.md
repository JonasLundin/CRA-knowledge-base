# Summary

Point 1 of Annex III Class I covers identity management systems software and privileged access management (PAM) software and hardware, as further specified in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.[^cra-annex-3] [^reg-2025-2392]

# Legal effect

Classified as an Important Product with Digital Elements (Class I). Products must undergo the conformity assessment procedures laid down in Article 32(2).[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Covers software and hardware whose core function is:
- Authentication, single sign-on (SSO), and identity federation;
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) server software;
- Privileged access management, credential vaulting, and session recording for administrative access.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Conformity assessment route:** Module A (if harmonised standards are applied in full), or third-party Module B+C / Module H, or European cybersecurity certification (Article 32(2));[^cra-art-32]
- **Relevant vertical standards:** M/606 line 16 (CEN/TC 224 / CEN-CLC JTC 13);
- **Lifecycle & reporting:** Full Annex I essential requirements and Article 14 mandatory reporting via the SRP.

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full product conformity applies from 11 December 2027.
