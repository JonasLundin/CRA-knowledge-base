# Summary

Article 62 establishes the Cyber Resilience Committee under the standard Union comitology rules to assist the Commission in adopting implementing acts.

# Legal effect

Establishes Member State oversight via the examination procedure for CRA implementing acts.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission and Member State representatives on the Cyber Resilience Committee.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 62 provides:
- The Commission is assisted by a committee (the Cyber Resilience Committee) within the meaning of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011.
- Where reference is made to the examination procedure, Article 5 of Regulation (EU) No 182/2011 applies.
- Covers implementing acts for common specifications (Article 27), technical descriptions of categories (Articles 7 and 8), SRP technical formats (Article 16), and safeguard decisions (Article 55).

# Dates and transitions

Enters into force and applies from 11 December 2024.
