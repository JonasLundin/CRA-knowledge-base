# Summary

Article 43 details the notification procedure via the European Commission's electronic tool (NANDO) and sets objection periods.

# Legal effect

Governs the official conferral of notified body status following Commission and Member State scrutiny.

# Applicability

Applies to notifying authorities, the European Commission, and Member States.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 43 establishes:
- Notifying authorities notify conformity assessment bodies using the electronic notification tool developed and managed by the Commission (NANDO).
- Notification must include full details of assessment activities, modules, product categories, and attestation of competence.
- The body may perform notified body activities only where no objections are raised by the Commission or other Member States within **two weeks** of notification (where an accreditation certificate is used) or within **two months** (where accreditation is not used).

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
