# Summary

To operationalise the Cyber Resilience Act, the European Commission is empowered under Article 65 to adopt delegated and implementing acts specifying technical scopes, exclusion rules, and platform procedures.[^reg-2025-1535] [^reg-2025-2392] [^reg-2026-881]

# Legal effect

Delegated and implementing acts have direct binding legal effect across all Member States upon their entry into force, completing the primary legislative architecture of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Applicability

Applies to economic operators, conformity assessment bodies, national authorities, ENISA, and CSIRTs in their respective operational areas.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

Key adopted secondary acts and official guidance include:

1. **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 (24 July 2025):** Exclusions for two- and three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles under Regulation (EU) No 168/2013;[^reg-2025-1535]
2. **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392 (15 November 2025):** Technical descriptions and boundary definitions for Important (Annex III Class I/II) and Critical (Annex IV) products with digital elements;[^reg-2025-2392]
3. **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881 (15 April 2026):** Procedural rules on delaying the dissemination of Article 14 notifications across the Single Reporting Platform (SRP);[^reg-2026-881]
4. **Commission Guidance C(2026) 5252 (30 June 2026):** Official non-binding guidelines clarifying substantial modification, commercial activity boundaries, and support period calculation.[^ec-guidance-5252]

# Dates and transitions

- Secondary acts take effect in accordance with their respective publication dates ahead of general CRA application.
