# Summary

Article 28 regulates the EU declaration of conformity, requiring manufacturers to declare that fulfillment of Annex I essential requirements has been demonstrated.

# Legal effect

Legally binds the manufacturer to assume full responsibility for product conformity with CRA essential requirements.

# Applicability

Applies to all manufacturers placing products with digital elements on the Union market.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 28 requires:
- The EU declaration of conformity must state that fulfillment of Annex I essential requirements has been demonstrated.
- Must follow the model structure in Annex V or simplified structure in Annex VI (containing a direct internet link to the full declaration).
- Must be continuously updated, translated into the language(s) required by Member States where the product is marketed, and retained for 10 years or the support period.
- Where a product is subject to multiple Union acts requiring a declaration (e.g. Radio Equipment Directive, Machinery Regulation), a single EU declaration of conformity must be drawn up.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
