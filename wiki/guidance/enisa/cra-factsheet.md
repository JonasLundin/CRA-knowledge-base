# Summary

The *ENISA Factsheet on the Cyber Resilience Act* is an executive visual and narrative publication summarizing the regulatory architecture of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, ENISA's statutory responsibilities, and key compliance milestones [^enisa-cra-factsheet].

# Legal effect

This factsheet is a non-binding informational publication [^enisa-cra-factsheet]. It provides a visual and conceptual summary without altering statutory obligations.

# Applicability

Broad reference material for corporate decision-makers, product security teams, public administrations, and cybersecurity practitioners.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The factsheet highlights the structural pillars of the CRA [^enisa-cra-factsheet]:

- **Core Legislative Objective:** Establishing mandatory baseline cybersecurity requirements for hardware and software products placed on the EU market throughout their entire lifecycle.
- **Product Classification Hierarchy:** Overview of default products (self-assessment), Important Class I products, Important Class II products, and Critical products with digital elements.
- **ENISA's Key Responsibilities:**
  1. Operating and maintaining the Single Reporting Platform (SRP) under Article 16.
  2. Synthesizing periodic EU-wide vulnerability and incident trend reports (Article 16(7)).
  3. Supporting standardisation and technical mapping activities.
  4. Participating in the CRA cooperation group and advising the European Commission.
- **Timeline and Milestones:** Clear infographic timeline detailing the progressive rollout dates (entry into force, notified body rules, Article 14 reporting, and full application).

# Dates and transitions

- **Publication Date:** April 2025.
- **Baseline Alignment:** Aligned with the final adopted text of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.
