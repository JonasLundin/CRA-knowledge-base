# Summary

Commission Communication C(2026) 5252 final provides official guidelines on the application of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act - CRA) [^ec-c-2026-5252]. Adopted under the Commission's mandate in Article 26, the guidance clarifies central concepts necessary for economic operators, open-source stewards, and national authorities to prepare for the progressive application of the regulation.

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding [^ec-c-2026-5252]. It does not create new legal rights or obligations, nor does it alter the text of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. Only the Court of Justice of the European Union possesses the competence to authoritatively interpret European Union law.

# Applicability

The guidance applies horizontally across all economic operators placing products with digital elements on the Union market, open-source software stewards, conformity assessment bodies, and Member State notifying and market surveillance authorities.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The communication and its annexed guidance cover several critical areas [^ec-c-2026-5252]:

- **Scope and Boundaries (Article 2 & 3):** Clarifies what constitutes a "product with digital elements" (PDE), the distinction between direct and indirect data connections, and the exclusion criteria for products developed exclusively for national security or defence, or regulated under specific sector legislation (e.g., medical devices, civil aviation, vehicles).
- **Substantial Modification (Article 3(32) & Article 18):** Defines criteria for determining when a software update or hardware alteration affects compliance with Annex I essential requirements, triggering manufacturer responsibilities for the modified entity.
- **Support Period Determination (Article 13(8)):** Explains how manufacturers must assess expected product lifetime, user expectations, and component availability to determine and explicitly declare the cybersecurity support period (minimum expected duration of security updates).
- **Open-Source Software Stewards (Article 24):** Details the light-touch regulatory regime applicable to non-profit entities and foundations facilitating open-source development without acting as commercial manufacturers.
- **Reporting Obligations (Article 14):** Provides guidance in Section 9.1 regarding awareness triggers for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents, reporting on legacy products placed on the market before general application, and handling vulnerabilities in third-party components.
- **Technical Documentation & SBOM (Annex VII & Annex I):** Practical guidance on structuring technical files, maintaining Software Bills of Materials (SBOMs), and retaining documentation for ten years.

# Dates and transitions

- **Publication Date:** 27 July 2026.
- **Application Context:** Designed to support compliance ahead of the reporting deadline on 11 September 2026 (Article 14) and general application on 11 December 2027.
