# Summary

Article 49 requires notified bodies to exchange information regarding certificate issuances, refusals, restrictions, and suspensions with notifying authorities and peer bodies.

# Legal effect

Imposes statutory reporting obligations on notified bodies to prevent certificate shopping and ensure peer transparency.

# Applicability

Applies to all CRA notified bodies and national notifying authorities.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 49 mandates notified bodies to inform:
- Their notifying authority of any refusal, restriction, suspension, or withdrawal of a certificate;
- Their notifying authority of any circumstances affecting the scope or conditions for notification;
- Market surveillance authorities, upon request, of conformity assessment activities performed;
- Other notified bodies performing similar tasks of negative and, on request, positive conformity assessment results.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
