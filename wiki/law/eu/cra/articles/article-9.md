# Summary

Article 9 mandates structured stakeholder and expert consultation prior to the adoption of secondary acts classifying important or critical products.

# Legal effect

Imposes a procedural legal obligation on the European Commission to consult the CRA Expert Group, ENISA, and industry stakeholders.

# Applicability

Binds the European Commission when preparing delegated and implementing acts under Articles 7 and 8.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 9 requires:
- The Commission shall consult the Cyber Resilience Act Expert Group established pursuant to Article 26 and relevant stakeholders.
- Consultation must occur before adopting delegated acts amending Annex III or Annex IV or implementing acts specifying technical descriptions.
- Consultation must assess cybersecurity risk, market prevalence, impact on SMEs, and technical feasibility.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2024 (empowerment and preparation processes).
