# Summary

ETSI EN 303 645 is a widely adopted European Standard establishing a cybersecurity baseline for consumer Internet of Things (IoT) devices[^etsi-en-303-645]. It served as the primary technical foundation for subsequent vertical CRA smart-home and IoT standardisation (M/606 Lines 31–34)[^enisa-mapping].

# Legal effect

Use of ETSI EN 303 645 is voluntary. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and does **not** confer a legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27.

# Applicability

Applies to consumer IoT devices (smart home appliances, connected toys, wearables, smart TVs, home gateways).

# Requirements and high-level coverage

ETSI EN 303 645 defines 13 baseline cybersecurity provisions:
1. No universal default passwords;
2. Implement a means to manage reports of vulnerabilities;
3. Keep software updated;
4. Securely store sensitive security parameters;
5. Communicate securely;
6. Minimise exposed attack surfaces;
7. Ensure software integrity;
8. Ensure that personal data is protected;
9. Make systems resilient to outages;
10. Examine system telemetry data;
11. Make it easy for consumers to delete user data;
12. Make installation and maintenance of devices easy;
13. Validate input data.
