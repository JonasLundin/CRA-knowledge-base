# Summary

Module H (Full Quality Assurance) is the conformity assessment procedure set out in Annex VI of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 whereby a notified body audits and approves the manufacturer's quality management system for design, development, production, and vulnerability handling.[^cra-annex-6]

# Legal effect

Provides full third-party conformity approval for a manufacturer's continuous product lifecycle, allowing ongoing product iterations and updates to be released without submitting every individual version for separate type examination.[^cra-annex-6]

# Applicability

- Permitted third-party route for **Important Products with Digital Elements — Class I** (Article 32(2));
- Permitted third-party route for **Important Products with Digital Elements — Class II** (Article 32(3)).[^cra-art-32]

# Requirements or coverage

Under Annex VI Module H, the manufacturer must:

1. **Quality system implementation:** Operate an approved quality management system covering cybersecurity design, development processes, testing methodologies, vulnerability monitoring, and patch distribution;
2. **Notified body audit:** Submit an application to a notified body of choice, including technical documentation for product families and quality system records;
3. **Audit assessment:** The notified body audits the quality system, reviews technical design documentation, and conducts on-site or remote process evaluations;
4. **Periodic surveillance:** The notified body carries out periodic audits and unannounced inspections to ensure continuous adherence;
5. **CE marking:** The manufacturer affixes the CE marking followed by the specific four-digit identification number of the auditing notified body.[^cra-annex-6]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
