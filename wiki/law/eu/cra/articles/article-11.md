# Summary

Article 11 clarifies the interplay between the Cyber Resilience Act and Regulation (EU) 2023/988 on general product safety regarding cybersecurity risks.

# Legal effect

Establishes that products compliant with CRA essential requirements are deemed to meet general product safety requirements for cybersecurity-related hazards.

# Applicability

Applies to consumer products with digital elements subject to Regulation (EU) 2023/988 and the CRA.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 11 coordinates safety regimes:
- Products with digital elements that conform to the essential requirements of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 are deemed to comply with the general safety requirements of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 with respect to the cybersecurity risks covered by the CRA.
- Preserves the application of Regulation (EU) 2023/988 for risks and product aspects not covered by the CRA.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
