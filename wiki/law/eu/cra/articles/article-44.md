# Summary

Article 44 mandates the Commission to allocate single identification numbers to notified bodies and maintain the publicly accessible NANDO register.

# Legal effect

Creates the authentic public registry of authorised CRA notified bodies.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission and all CRA notified bodies.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 44 establishes:
- The Commission assigns a single identification number to each notified body.
- The Commission makes publicly available and continuously updates the list of notified bodies on NANDO, including the identification numbers, scope of notification, and tasks for which they have been notified.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
