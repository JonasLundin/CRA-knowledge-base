# Summary

Annex III enumerates the specific product categories classified as Important Products with Digital Elements, divided into Class I (List 1) and Class II (List 2).

# Legal effect

Determines mandatory conformity assessment routes under Article 32, excluding standard self-assessment (Module A) for Class II products.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers producing items within the listed Class I and Class II categories.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex III establishes two distinct classes:
- **Class I (List 1 - 21 product categories)**:
  1. Identity management systems and privileged access management software;
  2. Standalone and embedded browsers;
  3. Password managers;
  4. Software that searches for, removes, or quarantines malicious software (antivirus);
  5. Products with digital elements with the function of virtual private networks (VPN);
  6. Network management systems;
  7. Security information and event management (SIEM) systems;
  8. Boot managers;
  9. Public key infrastructure and certificate issuance software;
  10. Microcontrollers and microprocessors with security functions;
  11. Firewalls and intrusion detection/prevention systems (not covered in Class II);
  12. Routers and modems for residential/commercial use;
  13. Smart home products with security functionality (smart locks, cameras, alarm systems);
  14. Smart toys with internet connectivity;
  15. Personal wearable health/fitness devices with communication interfaces;
  16. Virtual assistants with hardware;
  17. Operating systems (general purpose not in Class II);
  18. Hypervisors and container runtime environments;
  19. Firmware and microcode;
  20. Remote access and management software;
  21. Physical and virtual network interfaces.
- **Class II (List 2 - 14 higher-risk categories)**:
  1. Operating systems for servers, desktops, and mobile devices;
  2. Hypervisors and container orchestration engines;
  3. Firewalls, IDS, and IPS intended for industrial or enterprise networks;
  4. Microprocessors and microcontrollers with advanced security functionality;
  5. Hardware security modules (HSMs) not in Annex IV;
  6. Smartcards, smartcard readers, and secure elements;
  7. Industrial automation and control systems (IACS) for essential entities;
  8. Industrial IoT devices used in critical infrastructure;
  9. Routers, switches, and modems intended for telecommunications infrastructure;
  10. Software defined networking (SDN) controllers;
  11. Public key infrastructure root certification authorities;
  12. Tamper-resistant microprocessors;
  13. Electronic voting machines;
  14. Secure cryptoprocessors.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027. Technical descriptions specified in Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.
