# Summary

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) establishes harmonised Union requirements for the development, placement on the market, and use of artificial intelligence systems, including specific requirements for high-risk AI systems regarding data governance, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity under Article 15.[^reg-2024-1689] Cyber Resilience Act Article 12 governs the intersection between both regulations, establishing deemed compliance mechanisms and integrated conformity assessment procedures for products with digital elements that qualify as high-risk AI systems.[^cra-art-12]

# Interaction and scope relationship

The AI Act and the Cyber Resilience Act apply concurrently when an AI system is embedded in or constitutes a product with digital elements placed on the EU market that is classified as a high-risk AI system under AI Act Article 6 (e.g., AI safety components in machinery, biometric identification devices, or standalone high-risk AI software connected to networks).[^reg-2024-1689][^cra-art-12]

# When the instrument applies

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 applies whenever:
- An AI system is classified as high-risk under Article 6(1) (safety component of a product covered by Union harmonisation legislation listed in Annex I) or Article 6(2) (stand-alone high-risk systems listed in Annex III);[^reg-2024-1689]
- An AI system is placed on the market, put into service, or used within the Union;[^reg-2024-1689]
- The provider must fulfill Article 15 requirements regarding resilience against AI-specific vulnerabilities (e.g., adversarial attacks, data poisoning, model evasion) and general ICT cybersecurity.[^reg-2024-1689]

# Exclusion or concurrency with CRA

The regimes apply concurrently. Neither instrument excludes the other for high-risk AI products with digital elements. Instead, CRA Article 12 and AI Act Article 43 establish specific harmonization rules to prevent duplicate testing and conflicting requirements.[^cra-art-12]

# Technical or procedural satisfaction of CRA elements

1. **Deemed satisfaction of AI Act Article 15 cybersecurity requirements:** Under CRA Article 12(1), where a high-risk AI system is a product with digital elements within the scope of the CRA, compliance with the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in CRA Annex I Part I is deemed to fulfill the cybersecurity requirements set out in Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, provided that the level of cybersecurity required by the AI Act is achieved.[^cra-art-12]
2. **Single conformity assessment procedure:** Under CRA Article 12(2) and Article 32(5), providers of high-risk AI systems subject to both regulations may integrate the conformity assessment procedures into a single procedure under the AI Act (e.g., Article 43), utilizing a single technical documentation and a unified EU declaration of conformity.[^cra-art-12][^cra-art-32]
3. **AI-specific security aspects:** While CRA Annex I Part I covers product cybersecurity and vulnerability management, AI-specific risks such as data poisoning, prompt injection, and model inversion are evaluated jointly within the CRA risk assessment and AI Act risk management system.[^reg-2024-1689][^cra-art-12]
4. **Authority coordination:** Market surveillance authorities designated under the AI Act and the CRA must cooperate, exchange test reports, and coordinate enforcement actions under CRA Article 12(3) and AI Act Article 74.[^cra-art-12]

# Relevant provisions

- **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689:** Article 6 (Classification rules for high-risk AI systems), Article 15 (Accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity), Article 43 (Conformity assessment), Annex I & Annex III.[^reg-2024-1689]
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/2847:** Article 12 (Requirements for high-risk AI systems), Article 32(5) (Conformity assessment integration), Recitals 33, 62.[^cra-art-12][^cra-art-32]

# Dates and transition

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 entered into force on 1 August 2024, with phased application: prohibited AI applies from 2 February 2025; general-purpose AI rules from 2 August 2025; high-risk AI obligations from 2 August 2026 / 2 August 2027.[^reg-2024-1689]
- CRA Article 12 interaction rules apply from 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-12]
