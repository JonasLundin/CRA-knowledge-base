# Manufacturer Obligations

Overview of essential requirements, duties, and compliance procedures applicable to manufacturers and authorised representatives under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Concepts

- [Manufacturer Role & Duties](manufacturer.md) — Core responsibilities, placing on the market, and lifecycle compliance (Article 13)
- [Authorised Representative](authorised-representative.md) — Mandated economic operator representing non-EU manufacturers (Article 15)
- [Cybersecurity Risk Assessment](cybersecurity-risk-assessment.md) — Mandatory assessment of risks, threats, and intended environment (Article 13(2), Annex I Part I)
- [Third-Party Components Due Diligence](third-party-components.md) — Integration, vetting, and vulnerability monitoring of third-party software (Article 13(5)–(6))
- [Support Period Determination](support-period.md) — Criteria for determining and declaring the security support period (Article 13(8)–(10))
- [Security Updates](security-updates.md) — Free security patches, automatic update mechanisms, and disaggregation from feature updates (Article 13(9), (14))
- [Technical Documentation](technical-documentation.md) — Design, risk assessment, and SBOM contents required under Annex VII (Article 31)
- [EU Declaration of Conformity](eu-declaration-of-conformity.md) — Drawing up, updating, and translating the DoC under Annex VIII (Article 28)
- [CE Marking Rules](ce-marking.md) — Conditions, visibility, and rules for affixing the CE mark (Articles 29–30)
