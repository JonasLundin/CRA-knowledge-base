# Summary

Article 10 establishes cooperative measures between the Union and Member States to address cybersecurity workforce shortages and foster skills development.

# Legal effect

Obligates the Commission and Member States to support cybersecurity skills development, training programs, and awareness initiatives.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission, ENISA, and Member State authorities.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 10 provides:
- The Commission, ENISA, and Member States shall promote measures to enhance cybersecurity skills and education.
- Support development of training programs for manufacturers, developers, microenterprises, and SMEs.
- Encourage gender balance and diversity in cybersecurity education and workforce initiatives.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
