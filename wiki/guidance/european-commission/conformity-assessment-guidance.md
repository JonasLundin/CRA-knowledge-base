# Summary

The European Commission's *Guidance on Conformity Assessment Procedures and Notified Bodies* provides technical and procedural guidance on demonstrating conformity with CRA Annex I essential requirements [^ec-cra-conformity]. It explains the modular conformity architecture based on Decision No 768/2008/EC and maps procedures according to product risk tiers.

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and explanatory [^ec-cra-conformity]. It interprets the mandatory conformity assessment pathways established in Article 32 and Annex VI of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers, authorized representatives, conformity assessment bodies (notified bodies), and market surveillance authorities.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The guidance outlines the compliance pathways based on product categorization [^ec-cra-conformity]:

- **Default (Standard) Products with Digital Elements:** Manufacturers may apply **Module A (Internal Production Control)** (Annex VI), conducting a self-assessment of cybersecurity risk and Annex I compliance.
- **Important Products - Class I (Annex III):**
  - If the manufacturer applies harmonised standards (Article 27) or common specifications (Article 28) covering all essential requirements, **Module A** may be used.
  - If harmonised standards/common specifications are not applied, partially applied, or absent, the manufacturer must apply **Module B + C** (EU-type examination + conformity to type) or **Module H** (full quality assurance) involving a notified body.
- **Important Products - Class II (Annex III):** Mandatory third-party conformity assessment via **Module B + C** or **Module H**, regardless of whether harmonised standards exist.
- **Critical Products with Digital Elements (Annex IV):** Mandatory third-party conformity assessment via **Module B + C**, **Module H**, or a mandatory European cybersecurity certification scheme at assurance level 'substantial' or 'high' under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Article 8 & 32(4)).
- **Role and Accreditation of Notified Bodies (Articles 29–42):** Designation requirements, technical competence criteria, independence, and operational oversight by national notifying authorities.

# Dates and transitions

- **Notified Body Framework:** Rules concerning notifying authorities and notified bodies applied earlier on 11 June 2026 to enable timely designation.
- **Mandatory Assessment Rules:** Full application for products placed on the market from 11 December 2027.
