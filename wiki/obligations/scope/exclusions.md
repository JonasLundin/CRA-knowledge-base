# Summary

Article 2 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 excludes specific categories of products with digital elements that are already covered by sector-specific Union harmonisation legislation providing an equivalent or higher level of cybersecurity protection.[^cra-art-2]

# Legal effect

Products falling entirely under these sectoral exclusions are exempt from CRA requirements, including Annex I essential requirements, conformity assessment, and Article 14 reporting (unless specific provisions cross-refer). Manufacturers follow the dedicated sectoral cybersecurity and safety frameworks.[^cra-art-2]

# Applicability

The exclusions apply strictly to products within the scope of the following Union acts:

1. **Medical devices:** Products subject to Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (Medical Device Regulation);[^cra-art-2]
2. **In vitro diagnostic medical devices:** Products subject to Regulation (EU) 2017/746 (IVDR);[^cra-art-2]
3. **Civil aviation:** Products with digital elements certified, declared, or approved pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2018/1139 (EASA Basic Regulation);[^cra-art-2]
4. **Motor vehicles:** Vehicles, systems, components, and separate technical units approved under Regulation (EU) 2019/2144 (General Safety Regulation);[^cra-art-2]
5. **Marine equipment:** Products within the scope of Directive 2014/90/EU (Marine Equipment Directive);[^cra-art-2]
6. **Two- and three-wheel vehicles:** Vehicles and systems excluded pursuant to Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535 adopted under Regulation (EU) No 168/2013.[^reg-2025-1535]

# Requirements or coverage

- The exclusion applies only insofar as the sectoral product meets the definition and scope of the corresponding sector regulation.
- General IT systems, non-embedded aftermarket accessories, or non-integral software not covered by type-approval or medical certification remain within the horizontal scope of the CRA.
- National security, defence, and classified systems are governed by separate exclusions under Article 2(7)–(9).[^cra-art-2]

# Dates and transitions

- Effective from 11 December 2027, coinciding with full CRA application.
- Sectoral legislation continues to apply under its own transition regimes.[^cra-art-2]
