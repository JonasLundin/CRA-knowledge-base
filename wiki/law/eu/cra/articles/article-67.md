# Summary

Article 67 formally amends Annex I of Directive (EU) 2020/1828 on representative actions, listing the Cyber Resilience Act among covered consumer protection laws.

# Legal effect

Directly amends Directive (EU) 2020/1828 to include CRA provisions.

# Applicability

Applies to qualified entities and national courts handling collective consumer redress.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 67 provides:
- In Annex I to Directive (EU) 2020/1828, the following point is added:
  'Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 of the European Parliament and of the Council on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements (Cyber Resilience Act).'

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
