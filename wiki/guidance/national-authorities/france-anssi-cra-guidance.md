# Summary

The French National Cybersecurity Agency (*Agence nationale de la sécurité des systèmes d'information* - ANSSI) provides official guidance on the structure, obligations, and technical assessment frameworks introduced by Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 [^anssi-cra-guide].

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and explanatory under French and EU law [^anssi-cra-guide]. It clarifies the relationship between the CRA and existing French cybersecurity frameworks without modifying the direct applicability of Union regulation.

# Applicability

Applies to digital product manufacturers, software editors, industrial system developers, and security evaluation laboratories operating in France.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The ANSSI publication provides national technical perspectives on CRA adoption [^anssi-cra-guide]:

- **Conformity Assessment and Certification Alignment (Articles 8 & 32):** Explains how existing French evaluation schemes (e.g., CSPN - *Certification de Sécurité de Premier Niveau*) and the European Cybersecurity Certification Scheme on Common Criteria (EUCC) relate to CRA conformity requirements for Important and Critical products.
- **Reporting and CERT-FR Coordination (Article 14):** Clarifies notification channels for French manufacturers, emphasizing the dual role of the ENISA Single Reporting Platform and CERT-FR's national advisory mandate.
- **Integration with NIS2 Directive:** Guidance for essential and important entities on evaluating CRA-compliant products within their secure procurement and supply chain risk management strategies.
- **Support for Open Source and Start-ups:** Guidance on participating in French and European collaborative cybersecurity initiatives.

# Dates and transitions

- **Publication Timeline:** Published in August 2025 and updated in April 2026.
