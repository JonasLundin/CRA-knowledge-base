# Summary

Article 71 defines the official entry into force and the phased timeline of application for Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Legal effect

Establishes the binding timeline of direct applicability across all EU Member States without national transposition.

# Applicability

Applies universally across all EU Member States.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 71 establishes the phased timeline:
1. **Entry into force**: Entered into force on **11 December 2024** (the 20th day following publication in the Official Journal on 20 November 2024).
2. **General application**: Applies generally from **11 December 2027** (36 months following entry into force).
3. **Phased early application**:
   - **Chapter IV (Articles 35 to 51)** on Notifying Authorities and Notified Bodies applies from **11 June 2026** (18 months);
   - **Article 14** on Reporting Obligations of Manufacturers applies from **11 September 2026** (21 months).
4. **Immediate applicability**: Delegated and implementing empowerments (Articles 61 and 62) and preparatory administrative cooperation apply from entry into force (11 December 2024).

# Dates and transitions

Entered into force on 11 December 2024; application dates: 11 June 2026, 11 September 2026, and 11 December 2027.
