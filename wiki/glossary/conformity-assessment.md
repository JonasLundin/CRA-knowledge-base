# Summary

Under Article 3(30) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, **conformity assessment** means the process demonstrating whether the essential requirements set out in Annex I have been fulfilled.[^cra-art-3] [^cra-art-32]

# Legal effect

Mandatory prerequisite prior to placing any product with digital elements on the EU market or affixing the CE mark.[^cra-art-32]

# Requirements or coverage

Executed through Module A (internal control), Module B+C (EU-type examination + conformity to type), Module H (full quality assurance), or European cybersecurity certification schemes under the CSA.[^cra-art-32]
