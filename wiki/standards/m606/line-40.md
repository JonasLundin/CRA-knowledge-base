# Summary

M/606 Line 40 requests a vertical European Standard establishing essential cybersecurity requirements for smart meter gateways under CRA Annex IV point 3 (Critical products with digital elements)[^m606].

# Legal effect

This request entry sets the standardisation mandate; presumption of conformity requires publication in the OJEU. Critical products under Annex IV are subject to mandatory third-party conformity assessment or European cybersecurity certification under CRA Article 32.

# Requested scope and mandate

- **Legal basis**: CRA Annex IV, point 3.
- **Mandate objective**: Define requirements for metrological data integrity, wide area network (WAN) / home area network (HAN) / local metrological network (LMN) separation, hardware security modules, secure remote firmware updates, and resistance against physical tampering.

# Responsible committee and deliverables

- **Technical Committee**: CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 6 (Product Security; Rapporteur: Enrico Frumento, expert Torben Markussen)[^stan4cra-jtc13].
