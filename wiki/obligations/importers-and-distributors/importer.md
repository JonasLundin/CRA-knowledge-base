# Summary

An importer is any natural or legal person established within the European Union who places on the market a product with digital elements that bears the name or trademark of a person established outside the Union.[^cra-art-3] Article 16 establishes their verification and supply-chain obligations.[^cra-art-16]

# Legal effect

Importers must ensure that third-country manufacturers have fulfilled all CRA requirements before placing products on the EU market. Placing non-compliant products on the market exposes importers to direct administrative fines and market surveillance measures.[^cra-art-16]

# Applicability

Applies to EU-established entities importing products with digital elements into the European Union from third countries.[^cra-art-16]

# Requirements or coverage

Before placing a product on the market, importers must verify that:

1. **Conformity assessment:** The manufacturer has carried out the appropriate conformity assessment procedure (Article 16(2));
2. **Technical documentation:** The manufacturer has drawn up the technical documentation and SBOM;
3. **CE mark & documentation:** The product bears the CE marking, is accompanied by EU declaration of conformity (or internet link), and includes required user instructions (Article 16(2));
4. **Traceability:** The importer's name, registered trade name/trademark, and postal/electronic contact address are indicated on the product, packaging, or accompanying documentation (Article 16(3));
5. **Storage & transport:** Storage or transport conditions do not jeopardize compliance with Annex I requirements (Article 16(5));
6. **Prohibition on non-compliant products:** Where an importer knows or has reason to believe a product is non-compliant or presents a significant cybersecurity risk, it must refrain from placing it on the market and inform the manufacturer and market surveillance authorities (Article 16(4));
7. **Retention duty:** Keep a copy of the EU declaration of conformity at the disposal of authorities for 10 years or the support period (Article 16(8)).[^cra-art-16]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
