# Summary

Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 was published in the Official Journal of the European Union on 20 November 2024 (OJ L 2024/2847) and entered into force on 10 December 2024 (the twentieth day following its publication).[^cra-art-71]

# Legal effect

Entry into force establishes the binding legal existence of the CRA across all EU Member States, initiating the empowerment of the European Commission to adopt secondary acts, standardisation requests, and enabling Member State preparations.[^cra-art-71]

# Applicability

Applies horizontally across all 27 EU Member States and directly binds EU institutions, European Standardisation Organisations (ESOs), and national notifying authorities.[^cra-art-71]

# Requirements or coverage

Entry into force activates:
1. Commission powers to adopt delegated and implementing acts (Article 65);
2. Standardisation request mandate M/606 (C(2025) 618) to CEN, CENELEC, and ETSI;
3. Launch of ENISA development for the Single Reporting Platform (SRP);
4. The timeline countdown towards Chapter IV (18 months), Article 14 (21 months), and full application (36 months).[^cra-art-71]

# Dates and transitions

- **20 November 2024:** Publication in the OJEU.
- **10 December 2024:** Entry into force.
