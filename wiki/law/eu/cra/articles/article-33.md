# Summary

Article 33 mandates Member States and the Commission to establish dedicated financial, organizational, and technical support mechanisms for SMEs and start-ups.

# Legal effect

Obligates Member States and notified bodies to provide tailored guidance, simplified processes, and proportionate conformity assessment fees.

# Applicability

Applies to microenterprises, SMEs, and start-ups developing products with digital elements, as well as Member States, Commission, and notified bodies.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 33 provides:
- Member States and Commission shall provide dedicated financial support, cybersecurity testbeds, and regulatory sandboxes for SMEs and start-ups.
- Member States must establish SME contact points for guidance and technical assistance.
- Notified bodies must take into account the specific interests and needs of SMEs when setting conformity assessment fees and apply proportionate pricing.
- Simplified technical documentation formats and checklists to reduce administrative burden.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
