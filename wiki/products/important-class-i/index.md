# Important Products with Digital Elements — Class I

Overview of product categories listed in Annex III Class I of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and technical descriptions under Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.

## Conformity Route

Under Article 32(2), Class I products may use:
- **Module A (Internal Control)** if harmonised standards cited in the OJEU or common specifications are applied in full; or
- **Module B + C (EU-Type Examination)** or **Module H (Full Quality Assurance)** if harmonised standards are not applied or unavailable; or
- A European cybersecurity certificate at assurance level 'substantial'.
