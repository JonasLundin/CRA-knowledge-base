# Summary

Article 69 and Article 27(8) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 govern the recognition of legacy certificates, transitional support obligations, and the grandfathering of existing products already on the EU market.[^cra-art-69] [^cra-art-27]

# Legal effect

Protects pre-existing product investments and maintains the validity of certificates issued under established European cybersecurity certification schemes without requiring immediate recertification on 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-69]

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers with products placed on the EU market prior to 11 December 2027, as well as holders of certificates issued under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (Cybersecurity Act).[^cra-art-69]

# Requirements or coverage

1. **Grandfathered products (Article 69(1)):** Products placed on the EU market prior to 11 December 2027 may continue to be supplied without retroactive CRA certification, provided no substantial modification is made;
2. **Substantial modification trigger (Article 69(2)):** Any substantial modification made on or after 11 December 2027 ends grandfathering and requires full CRA compliance for the modified product;
3. **Legacy reporting (Article 69(3)):** Actively supported legacy products remain subject to Article 14 mandatory vulnerability and incident reporting starting 11 September 2026;
4. **EU cybersecurity certificates (Article 69(4), Article 27(8)):** EU Cybersecurity Act certificates (e.g. EUCC) issued prior to or during the transition confer presumption of conformity up to their expiry date, provided the covered requirements match Annex I.[^cra-art-69] [^cra-art-27]

# Dates and transitions

- **11 September 2026:** Article 14 reporting applies to legacy products.
- **11 December 2027:** Full application; substantial modifications trigger CRA.
