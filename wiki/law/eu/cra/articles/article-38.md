# Summary

Article 38 requires notifying authorities to maintain transparency by communicating national assessment and monitoring procedures to the Commission.

# Legal effect

Imposes an ongoing reporting and transparency obligation on national notifying authorities towards the European Commission.

# Applicability

Applies to national notifying authorities and the European Commission.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 38 requires:
- Member States shall inform the Commission of their national procedures for the assessment and notification of conformity assessment bodies and monitoring of notified bodies, and of any changes thereto.
- The Commission makes this information publicly available.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
