# Summary

Article 37 sets out organisational, ethical, and competence standards governing national notifying authorities.

# Legal effect

Binds Member State notifying authorities to strict standards of independence, impartiality, and professional competence.

# Applicability

Applies to designated national notifying authorities across all EU Member States.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 37 mandates that notifying authorities must:
- Be established in a manner ensuring no conflict of interest with conformity assessment bodies;
- Be organised and operated so as to safeguard objectivity and impartiality;
- Ensure that each decision relating to notification is taken by competent personnel different from those who carried out the initial assessment;
- Refrain from offering or providing activities performed by conformity assessment bodies or commercial consultancy services;
- Safeguard the confidentiality of obtained information;
- Have a sufficient number of competent personnel and adequate resources for the proper performance of their tasks.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
