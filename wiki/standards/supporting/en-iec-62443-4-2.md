# Summary

EN IEC 62443-4-2:2019 is an international and European standard specifying technical cybersecurity requirements for industrial automation and control system (IACS) components across four component types: software applications, embedded devices, host devices, and network devices[^en-iec-62443-4-2].

# Legal effect

Use of EN IEC 62443-4-2 is voluntary. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and does **not** confer a legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27.

# Applicability

Provides technical specifications for industrial products including PLCs, RTUs, DCS components, industrial PCs, SCADA software, and industrial switches/firewalls.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

EN IEC 62443-4-2 defines 7 foundational requirement areas:
1. Identification and authentication control (IAC);
2. Use control (UC);
3. System integrity (SI);
4. Data confidentiality (DC);
5. Restricted data flow (RDF);
6. Timely response to events (TRE);
7. Resource availability (RA).
