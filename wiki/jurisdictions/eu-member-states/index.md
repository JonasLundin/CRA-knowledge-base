## Common Implementation Milestones

- **2024-12-10**: Entry into force of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.
- **2026-06-11**: Application of Chapter IV (Notifying Authorities and Notified Bodies).
- **2026-09-11**: Application of Article 14 reporting obligations via the ENISA Single Reporting Platform (SRP) to designated national CSIRTs.
- **2027-12-11**: Full application of CRA requirements, market surveillance enforcement, and national penalty provisions (Article 64).
