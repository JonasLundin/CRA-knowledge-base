# Summary

The European Commission's *Cyber Resilience Act Implementation - Frequently Asked Questions* is a dynamic reference document addressing practical questions raised by manufacturers, developers, open-source communities, and national authorities [^ec-cra-faq]. The FAQ provides official clarifications on scope thresholds, economic operator roles, interaction with other EU legislation, and compliance timelines.

# Legal effect

The answers provided in the FAQ are non-binding and intended solely for informational purposes [^ec-cra-faq]. They do not substitute for or modify the legal provisions of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applicable to developers, manufacturers, importers, distributors, open-source software stewards, and notified bodies navigating the implementation phase of the CRA.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The FAQ addresses practical scenarios across several thematic clusters [^ec-cra-faq]:

- **Classification and Scope:** Boundary distinctions for SaaS/cloud solutions (distinguishing pure remote data processing from software components of a physical product), microcontrollers, development boards, and embedded systems.
- **Interaction with Intersecting Legislation:** Relationship with the NIS2 Directive ((EU) 2022/2555), General Product Safety Regulation ((EU) 2023/988), AI Act ((EU) 2024/1689), and Radio Equipment Directive (2014/53/EU).
- **Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS):** Criteria for determining when software is developed or supplied outside the course of a commercial activity, the role of bug bounty programmes, and monetary donations.
- **Reporting Thresholds:** Practical criteria for determining when a vulnerability qualifies as "actively exploited" or when an incident has a "severe impact" under Article 14.
- **Conformity Assessment and Standards:** Transition mechanisms pending the citation of harmonised standards in the Official Journal, use of common specifications, and self-assessment applicability.
