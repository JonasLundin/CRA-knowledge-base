# Summary

Under Article 3(1) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **product with digital elements** means any software or hardware product and its remote data processing solutions, including software or hardware components being placed on the market separately.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Establishes the core subject-matter definition for all obligations, conformity assessments, and market surveillance enforcement under the CRA.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

To fall under this definition, the product must:
1. Consist of software, hardware, or a combination thereof;
2. Include any companion remote data processing solutions developed by or under the control of the manufacturer;
3. Have an intended or reasonably foreseeable logical or physical data connection to a device or network (Article 2(1)).[^cra-art-3]
