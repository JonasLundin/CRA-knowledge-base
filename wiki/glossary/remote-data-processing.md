# Summary

Under Article 3(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, **remote data processing** means any data processing at a distance for which software is designed and developed by or on behalf of the manufacturer, or under the control of the manufacturer, and the absence of which would prevent the product with digital elements from performing one of its primary functions.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Brings essential cloud backends and companion online processing services into the direct scope of the product's cybersecurity compliance requirements.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Requires three cumulative elements:
1. Data processing carried out at a distance (e.g. cloud/hosted infrastructure);
2. Software designed, developed, or controlled by or on behalf of the manufacturer;
3. Direct necessity for performing at least one primary product function.[^cra-art-3]
