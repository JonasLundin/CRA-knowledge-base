# Summary

M/606 Line 11 requests horizontal standards specifying mechanisms to reduce the severity and impact of cybersecurity incidents through architectural isolation, compartmentalisation, and exploit mitigation techniques[^m606].

# Legal effect

This request entry creates no direct presumption of conformity until standard adoption and OJEU citation.

# Requested scope and mandate

- **Legal basis**: CRA Annex I, Part I, point 2(j).
- **Mandate objective**: Define technical requirements for process sandboxing, privilege separation, memory safety mitigations (ASLR, DEP, stack canaries), and containment.

# Responsible committee and deliverables

- **Technical Committee**: CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9[^stan4cra-jtc13].
- **Deliverable**: `prEN 40000-1-2` (Work Item 81344).
