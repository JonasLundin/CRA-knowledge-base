# Summary

`EN 40000-1-2` (*Cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements — Part 1-2: Principles for cyber resilience*, developed as `prEN 40000-1-2` / `FprEN 40000-1-2` and also referred to in committee work as *Principles, product risk management, and lifecycle activities*) is the core Type A horizontal European Standard developed by CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 in response to Standardisation Request M/606 Line 1[^m606][^stan4cra-jtc13]. Following approval in the CEN Formal Vote closing on 1 October 2026 and formal CEN-CENELEC ratification on 2 November 2026 (with target availability on 18 November 2026), it establishes the horizontal methodology for cybersecurity risk assessment, cybersecurity-by-design, cybersecurity-by-default, and lifecycle activities under CRA Annex I Part I point 1[^cen-wi-81335].

# Legal effect

Although formally ratified as a European Standard (`EN 40000-1-2`), CEN-CENELEC project records indicate **no standalone citation expected** in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU)[^cen-wi-81335]. Because Type A standards establish overarching risk-management principles and methodological frameworks rather than complete product-level technical specifications, `EN 40000-1-2` structures the manufacturer's technical documentation and risk assessment (CRA Article 13(2) and Annex VII) and is normatively referenced by OJEU-citable Type B (`prEN 40000-1-3`, `prEN 40000-1-4`) and Type C vertical standards, but does not confer standalone legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27 on its own.

# Applicability

Applies horizontally to all products with digital elements within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. It provides both the risk-based methodology used directly by manufacturers to determine which CRA Annex I Part I point 2 requirements apply to their product and the horizontal framework referenced by ESO technical committees drafting vertical product-specific standards (Type C) and generic security requirements (`prEN 40000-1-4`).

# Requirements and high-level coverage

`prEN 40000-1-2` specifies normative requirements and methodology for:
- **Context and Product Definition**: Defining product boundaries, intended purpose, reasonably foreseeable use, operational environment, and asset identification;
- **Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Methodology**: Threat modeling (supported by `CEN/CLC/TR 40000-1-5`), risk estimation, and determination of applicable essential cybersecurity requirements under CRA Annex I Part I;
- **Cybersecurity-by-Design and Default**: Engineering principles across design, development, production, and maintenance phases;
- **Supply Chain & Component Due Diligence**: Risk management when integrating third-party commercial and open-source software/hardware components (CRA Article 13(5));
- **Lifecycle & Support Period Governance**: Aligning risk management continuously across the product's expected support period and linking risk findings to vulnerability handling (`prEN 40000-1-3`) and generic security controls (`prEN 40000-1-4`).
