# Summary

Annex IV lists products with digital elements presenting critical cybersecurity risk, whose disruption would cause severe cross-sectoral systemic failure.

# Legal effect

Subjects products to third-party conformity assessment (Module B+C or Module H) and empowers the Commission to mandate European cybersecurity certification.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers of hardware and software products listed in Annex IV.

# Requirements or coverage

Annex IV lists four critical product categories:
1. **Hardware devices with security boxes** (e.g. smart meter gateways, secure payment boxes);
2. **Smart meter gateway devices** within smart metering systems and advanced metering infrastructure;
3. **Smartcards, smartcard readers, and secure elements** intended for high-assurance authentication and electronic identification;
4. **Hardware Security Modules (HSMs)** and secure cryptoprocessors intended for critical cryptographic operations.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
