# Summary

Article 21 sets out the legal circumstances under which an importer or distributor is deemed to be a manufacturer under the CRA.

# Legal effect

Transfers all manufacturer obligations (Articles 13 and 14, conformity assessment, documentation) to importers or distributors meeting specified triggers.

# Applicability

Applies to importers and distributors placing products on the market under their own name/trademark or making substantial modifications.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 21 provides that an importer or distributor is subject to all manufacturer obligations where they:
1. Place a product with digital elements on the market under their own name or trademark; or
2. Carry out a substantial modification to a product with digital elements already placed on the market.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
