# Summary

Article 58 sets out the market surveillance procedure for addressing administrative and formal non-compliance issues without immediate technical security failure.

# Legal effect

Obligates economic operators to rectify administrative non-compliance under penalty of product restriction or withdrawal.

# Applicability

Applies to all economic operators placing or making available products on the market.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 58 identifies formal non-compliance where:
- The CE marking has been affixed in violation of Article 29 or 30;
- The identification number of the notified body has been affixed in violation of Article 30 or omitted;
- The EU declaration of conformity has not been drawn up or is incomplete;
- Technical documentation is either not available or not complete;
- Manufacturer or importer identification or contact details are missing;
- Any other administrative requirement provided for in Article 13, 18, 19, or 20 is not fulfilled.
Authorities require the operator to end formal non-compliance; failure to do so results in product restriction or recall.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
