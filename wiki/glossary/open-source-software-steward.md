# Summary

Under Article 3(23) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, an **open-source software steward** means any legal person, other than a manufacturer, that has the purpose or objective of systematically providing support on a sustained basis for the development of specific products with digital elements qualifying as free and open-source software intended for commercial activities.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Establishes a dedicated light-touch regulatory category subject to Article 24 cybersecurity policy and CVD coordination requirements, while exempting stewards from manufacturer conformity assessment and CE marking.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Applies to open-source foundations and legal entities that systematically support and host FOSS development infrastructure.[^cra-art-3]
