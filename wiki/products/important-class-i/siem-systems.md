# Summary

Point 7 of Annex III Class I covers security information and event management (SIEM) systems, log aggregation platforms, and security analytics software.[^cra-annex-3] [^reg-2025-2392]

# Legal effect

SIEM products are subject to Class I conformity assessment under Article 32(2), given their critical role in security incident detection, forensic investigation, and audit trail integrity.[^cra-art-32]

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Centralized SIEM software engines and appliances;
- Security log collection, correlation, and event analysis platforms;
- Extended detection and response (XDR) management consoles.[^reg-2025-2392]

# Requirements or coverage

- **Conformity assessment route:** Module A (with full application of cited harmonised standards) or third-party Module B+C / Module H (Article 32(2));[^cra-art-32]
- **Relevant vertical standards:** M/606 line 22 (ETSI EN 304-622);
- **Log integrity protection:** Write-once / tamper-evident storage, cryptographic validation of log records, and secure ingestion pipelines.

# Dates and transitions

- Article 14 reporting applies from 11 September 2026.
- Full product conformity applies from 11 December 2027.
