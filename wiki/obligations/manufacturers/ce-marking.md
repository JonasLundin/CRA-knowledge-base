# Summary

Articles 29 and 30 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 define the rules and conditions for affixing the CE marking to products with digital elements, demonstrating compliance with the CRA and general Union harmonisation principles.[^cra-art-29] [^cra-art-30] [^reg-765-2008]

# Legal effect

The CE marking indicates that the product complies with all applicable Union legislation providing for its affixing. Affixing the CE mark is a prerequisite for making a product with digital elements available on the EU single market.[^cra-art-29]

# Applicability

Mandatory for all products with digital elements placed on the EU market once the relevant conformity assessment procedure is completed.[^cra-art-29]

# Requirements or coverage

1. **Affixing location:** The CE marking must be affixed visibly, legibly, and indelibly to the product with digital elements or to its data plate. Where not possible due to the nature of the product (e.g. purely digital software), it must be affixed to packaging, accompanying documentation, or a dedicated software screen/website (Article 30(1)–(2));
2. **Timing:** Must be affixed before the product is placed on the market;
3. **Notified body identification:** Where a notified body is involved under Module H, Module B+C, or a cybersecurity certification scheme, the CE marking must be followed by the identification number of the notified body (Article 30(4));
4. **Prohibition of misleading marks:** Affixing markings or inscriptions likely to mislead third parties regarding the meaning or form of the CE marking is prohibited.[^cra-art-30]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
