# Summary

M/606 Line 1 requests the development of a foundational horizontal European Standard establishing principles for cyber resilience across all products with digital elements in scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847[^m606].

# Legal effect

An M/606 request entry defines standardisation mandates given by the European Commission. It does not itself confer presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27. Presumption arises only after adoption of the resulting standard and publication of its citation in the OJEU.

# Requested scope and mandate

- **Legal basis**: CRA Annex I, Part I, point 1 (cybersecurity-by-design, cybersecurity-by-default, risk assessment framework, and product lifecycle management).
- **Mandate objective**: Provide an overarching framework guiding manufacturers through risk assessment and establishing horizontal principles applied by all downstream vertical standards.

# Responsible committee and deliverables

- **Technical Committee**: CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 (Special Working Group on CRA)[^stan4cra-jtc13].
- **Deliverable**: `prEN 40000-1-1` (Work Item 81335).
