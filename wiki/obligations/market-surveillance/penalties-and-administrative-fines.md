# Summary

Article 64 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 establishes maximum statutory ceilings for administrative fines that Member State authorities may impose on economic operators for non-compliance with CRA obligations.[^cra-art-64]

# Legal effect

Administrative fines are directly enforceable financial sanctions imposed by national market surveillance authorities or competent courts, designed to be effective, proportionate, and dissuasive.[^cra-art-64]

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, and distributors placing or making available products with digital elements on the EU market.[^cra-art-64]

# Requirements or coverage

Article 64 establishes three distinct statutory fine tiers:

### Tier 1: Core Essential Requirements & Reporting Breaches (Article 64(2))
- **Infringements:** Non-compliance with Annex I essential cybersecurity requirements, manufacturer duties under Article 13, or Article 14 mandatory reporting obligations.
- **Maximum Fine:** Up to **€15 000 000** or **2.5% of total worldwide annual turnover** for the preceding financial year, whichever is higher.

### Tier 2: General Economic Operator Obligation Breaches (Article 64(3))
- **Infringements:** Non-compliance with obligations of authorised representatives (Article 15), importers (Article 16), distributors (Article 17), CE marking rules (Articles 29–30), or technical documentation (Article 31).
- **Maximum Fine:** Up to **€10 000 000** or **2% of total worldwide annual turnover**, whichever is higher.

### Tier 3: Misleading Information (Article 64(4))
- **Infringements:** Supplying incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information to notified bodies or market surveillance authorities in response to formal requests.
- **Maximum Fine:** Up to **€5 000 000** or **1% of total worldwide annual turnover**, whichever is higher.

### Proportionality & Small Enterprises
When determining fine amounts, authorities must account for the gravity, duration, intentional or negligent character of the infringement, and the economic size of the enterprise (with specific mitigating considerations for micro-enterprises, small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), and start-ups) (Article 64(6)–(10)).[^cra-art-64]

# Dates and transitions

- Fines relating to Article 14 reporting apply from 11 September 2026.
- General fine framework applies from 11 December 2027.[^cra-art-64]
