# Summary

The European Commission's *Guidance for Member States and National Authorities* sets out expectations and coordination mechanisms for national competent authorities implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 [^ec-cra-member-states].

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and intended to ensure harmonious enforcement across the internal market [^ec-cra-member-states]. It does not modify Member State statutory competencies or the binding text of the CRA.

# Applicability

Applies to Member State notifying authorities, market surveillance authorities (MSAs), designated Computer Security Incident Response Teams (CSIRTs), single points of contact, and judicial authorities.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The guidance provides procedural recommendations across core administrative pillars [^ec-cra-member-states]:

- **Notifying Authorities (Articles 29–31):** Procedures for designating notifying authorities responsible for assessing, notifying, and monitoring conformity assessment bodies (notified bodies) under CRA Chapter IV.
- **Market Surveillance Framework (Articles 52–56):** Applying Regulation (EU) 2019/1020 market surveillance principles to digital products, conducting risk-based product checks, and using the Information and Communication System on Market Surveillance (ICSMS).
- **Administrative Cooperation Group (ADCO-CRA):** Establishing cross-border coordination mechanisms among national market surveillance authorities to ensure consistent interpretation and coordinated market interventions.
- **CSIRT Integration and SRP Routing (Articles 14 & 16):** Technical and operational coordination between designated national CSIRTs and the ENISA Single Reporting Platform for receiving early warnings and vulnerability notifications.
- **National Penalties Framework (Article 64):** Setting proportional and dissuasive administrative fines and enforcement penalties within the ceilings established by the CRA.

# Dates and transitions

- **Notifying Authority & Notification Rules:** Applied earlier from 11 June 2026 (Article 71(2)).
- **General Enforcement Framework:** Fully operational by 11 December 2027.
