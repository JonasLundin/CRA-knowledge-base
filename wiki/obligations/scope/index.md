# Scope & Applicability

Topical navigation for the scope, exclusions, and applicability criteria under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Concepts

- [Product Scope](product-scope.md) — Products with digital elements made available on the EU market (Article 2(1))
- [Sectoral Exclusions](exclusions.md) — Exclusions for medical devices, motor vehicles, aviation, and marine equipment (Article 2(2)–(6))
- [Remote Data Processing Solutions](remote-data-processing.md) — Inclusion of cloud services and backend processing designed and developed by or on behalf of the manufacturer (Article 2(1), Article 3(2))
- [Commercial Activity Test](commercial-activity.md) — Boundary between commercial supply and non-commercial open-source development (Recitals 18–23, Article 2(1))
- [Substantial Modification](substantial-modification.md) — Criteria triggering new conformity assessments and manufacturer status (Article 3(32), Article 18)
- [Transitional Rules](transitional-rules.md) — Application dates, grandfathering, and support periods (Article 69, Article 71)
- [National Security & Defence Exemptions](exemptions-national-security.md) — Specific exclusions for military, defence, and classified systems (Article 2(7)–(9))
