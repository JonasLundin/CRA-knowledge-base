# Summary

Article 15 establishes a voluntary notification channel allowing economic operators and other entities to report cybersecurity threats, incidents, and vulnerabilities.

# Legal effect

Authorises voluntary submissions via the Single Reporting Platform while ensuring confidentiality and non-discriminatory processing.

# Applicability

Open to any economic operator, open-source software steward, or other entity.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 15 allows:
- Voluntary notification of any vulnerability, severe incident, or cyber threat affecting products with digital elements.
- Processing through the Single Reporting Platform under the same confidentiality and security safeguards as mandatory notifications under Article 14.
- Voluntary reporting does not impose mandatory obligations or create liability for the reporting entity.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 September 2026.
