# Summary

Chapter IV (Articles 35–51) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 establishes the legal framework for the designation, notification, and monitoring of independent third-party conformity assessment bodies (notified bodies) authorized to carry out CRA conformity assessments.[^cra-art-35]

# Legal effect

Only bodies officially designated by national notifying authorities and published in the Commission's NANDO (New Approach Notified and Designated Organisations) information system are legally empowered to perform Module B and Module H assessments under the CRA.[^cra-art-35]

# Applicability

Applies to conformity assessment bodies seeking notification, national notifying authorities, and manufacturers submitting products for third-party assessment.[^cra-art-35]

# Requirements or coverage

Under Chapter IV, notified bodies must satisfy rigorous criteria:

1. **Independence and impartiality:** Third-party independence from the economic operators whose products they evaluate;
2. **Technical competence:** Documented expertise in software security, penetration testing, hardware security, cryptography, and vulnerability analysis;
3. **Personnel & facilities:** Dedicated qualified personnel, testing laboratories, and secure facilities to protect commercial secrets and zero-day vulnerabilities;
4. **Coordination & peer participation:** Participation in EU-wide notified body coordination groups established under Article 51;
5. **Reporting duties:** Inform notifying authorities of certificates issued, refused, suspended, or withdrawn, and share relevant conformity information with other notified bodies.[^cra-art-35]

# Dates and transitions

- **11 June 2026:** Chapter IV applies (18 months post-entry into force) to enable Member States to designate and notify bodies before full product application in December 2027 (Article 71(2)(b)).[^cra-art-71]
