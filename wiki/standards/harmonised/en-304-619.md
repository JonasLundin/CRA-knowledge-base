# Summary

EN 304 619 is an ETSI draft European Standard under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 19[^m606], defining cybersecurity requirements for software that searches for, removes, or quarantines malicious software (Antivirus / Endpoint Protection) under CRA Annex III Class I point 4[^etsi-docbox-619].

# Legal effect

This document is an enquiry draft. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** presumption of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Endpoint antivirus software and Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) agents;
- Anti-malware scanners operating on endpoints, servers, and gateways.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

EN 304 619 addresses:
- Authenticity, cryptographic verification, and tamper-resistance of detection signatures and engine updates;
- Kernel driver and hook security, ensuring antivirus drivers cannot be abused for local privilege escalation;
- Anti-tampering and self-defense mechanisms protecting the AV service from termination by malicious processes;
- Secure quarantine isolation and containment;
- Telemetry data minimisation and user privacy protection.
