# Summary

The European Commission's *Guidance for Manufacturers* outlines the core responsibilities and technical workflows required for entities designing, developing, or manufacturing products with digital elements for the EU market [^ec-cra-manufacturers].

# Legal effect

This guidance page is non-binding and explanatory [^ec-cra-manufacturers]. It outlines the structure of obligations but does not replace the binding requirements set out in Article 13, Article 14, and Annex I of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers of all products with digital elements, whether default, important (Class I or Class II), or critical. It also applies to natural or legal persons making a substantial modification to an existing product (Article 18).

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The guidance covers key compliance steps for manufacturers [^ec-cra-manufacturers]:

- **Essential Cybersecurity Requirements (Annex I Part I):** Designing, developing, and producing products to ensure an appropriate level of cybersecurity based on a cybersecurity risk assessment.
- **Vulnerability Handling Processes (Annex I Part II):** Maintaining processes to identify, document, and remediate vulnerabilities without delay, providing free security updates throughout the declared support period.
- **Technical Documentation & SBOM (Annex VII):** Compiling and maintaining technical files, risk assessments, test reports, and component Software Bills of Materials (SBOMs) in machine-readable formats.
- **CE Marking and EU Declaration of Conformity (Articles 27–32):** Affixing the CE mark, drawing up the EU declaration of conformity, and providing user instructions in clear, accessible language.
- **Support Period Declaration:** Determining and clearly communicating the support period at the point of sale, ensuring users are informed of security patch availability.

# Dates and transitions

- **Obligations Application:** General manufacturer obligations apply from 11 December 2027, with Article 14 reporting obligations applying earlier from 11 September 2026.
