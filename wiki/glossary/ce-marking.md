# Summary

Under Article 3(25) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the **CE marking** means a marking by which the manufacturer indicates that the product with digital elements and the processes put in place by the manufacturer are in conformity with the essential requirements set out in Annex I and other applicable Union harmonisation legislation providing for its affixing.[^cra-art-3] [^cra-art-29]

# Legal effect

Affixing the CE mark constitutes the manufacturer's formal assertion of full legal conformity across all applicable EU product safety and cybersecurity directives and regulations.[^cra-art-29]

# Requirements or coverage

Must be affixed before placing on the market, in accordance with the general principles of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 and Article 30.[^cra-art-29]
