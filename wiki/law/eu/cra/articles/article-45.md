# Summary

Article 45 regulates procedures for restricting, suspending, or withdrawing a body's notification when it fails to satisfy competence or operational criteria.

# Legal effect

Empowers notifying authorities to modify or revoke notification status and safeguards the validity of existing certificates.

# Applicability

Applies to notifying authorities, notified bodies, and affected certificate holders.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 45 establishes:
- Where a notifying authority finds that a notified body no longer meets Article 39 requirements or fails its obligations, it must restrict, suspend, or withdraw notification as appropriate.
- In the event of restriction, suspension, or withdrawal, the notifying authority ensures another notified body processes the files or keeps them available for responsible authorities.
- Certificates issued prior to suspension or withdrawal remain valid unless proven to present a direct cybersecurity risk.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026 (paragraph 2 on administrative cooperation applies from 11 December 2024).
