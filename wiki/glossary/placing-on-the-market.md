# Summary

Under Article 3(11) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, **placing on the market** means the first making available of a product with digital elements on the Union market.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Establishes the exact legal moment when conformity assessment must be completed, technical documentation finalised, EU declaration of conformity issued, and the CE mark affixed by the manufacturer or importer.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Occurs when an individual product unit is supplied for the first time on the EU market, determining the applicable legal version and grandfathering cutoff dates under Article 69.[^cra-art-3]
