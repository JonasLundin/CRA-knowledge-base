# Summary

ENISA's *Competence Criteria and Assessment Framework for Notified Bodies under the Cyber Resilience Act* outlines technical evaluation benchmarks, personnel competence requirements, and testing facility capabilities for conformity assessment bodies (CABs) applying for notification under Chapter IV of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 [^enisa-notified-bodies].

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and technical [^enisa-notified-bodies]. It assists national notifying authorities and accreditation bodies in evaluating candidate CABs against the statutory requirements of Article 35 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to national notifying authorities, national accreditation bodies (NABs), candidate conformity assessment bodies, and designated notified bodies operating in the CRA domain.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The guidance structures the technical competence requirements across key operational dimensions [^enisa-notified-bodies]:

- **Personnel Technical Qualifications (Article 35(6)):** Required depth of expertise for assessment personnel in vulnerability analysis, reverse engineering, cryptographic verification, source code auditing, and industrial control protocol testing.
- **Independence and Impartiality (Article 35(3)–(5)):** Strict structural separation between conformity assessment activities and consulting, product development, or commercial vulnerability research for client products.
- **Laboratory Infrastructure and Tooling:** Standards for testing hardware and software testbeds, isolated reverse-engineering environments, automated vulnerability scanners, and secure test data retention systems.
- **Assessment Methodologies:** Recommended procedures for executing Module B (EU-Type Examination) and Module H (Full Quality Assurance) assessments across Important Class I, Class II, and Critical products.
- **Coordination of Notified Bodies (Article 42):** Participation in the sectoral group of notified bodies to ensure consistent assessment practices and uniform application of conformity modules.

# Dates and transitions

- **Early Application:** The statutory framework for notifying authorities and notified bodies took effect on **11 June 2026** (Article 71(2)), enabling CABs to be evaluated and notified prior to general CRA application in December 2027.
