# Summary

Article 13 defines the comprehensive legal duties of manufacturers placing products with digital elements on the Union market.

# Legal effect

Imposes mandatory legal duties on manufacturers throughout the product lifecycle, from design and risk assessment to post-market vulnerability handling.

# Applicability

Applies directly to all manufacturers placing products with digital elements on the Union market.

# Requirements or coverage

Key obligations under Article 13 include:
- **Design and manufacturing**: Ensure products are designed, developed, and produced in accordance with Annex I Part I essential requirements.
- **Cybersecurity risk assessment**: Conduct and document a cybersecurity risk assessment considering intended and foreseeable use, taking it into account during design and vulnerability handling.
- **Due diligence on components**: Exercise due diligence when integrating third-party components, including free and open-source software.
- **Support period determination**: Determine and document the support period reflecting user expectations and product nature (minimum 5 years unless product expected lifetime is shorter), ensuring security updates are provided without delay and free of charge.
- **Vulnerability handling**: Implement vulnerability handling processes conforming to Annex I Part II.
- **Documentation and CE marking**: Draw up technical documentation (Annex VII), carry out conformity assessment, affix CE marking, and issue the EU declaration of conformity (Annex V/VI).
- **Traceability and contact info**: Indicate name, registered trade name, postal address, and a single electronic contact point on the product or packaging.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
