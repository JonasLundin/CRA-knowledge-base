# CRA Implementation Timeline

Overview of key regulatory milestones, phased application dates, standardisation deadlines, and transitional periods under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

## Key Milestones

- [Entry into Force](entry-into-force.md) — 10 December 2024 (20 days post-publication in OJEU)
- [Chapter IV Notified Bodies Application](notified-bodies-application.md) — 11 June 2026 (18 months)
- [Article 14 Mandatory Reporting Application](article-14-reporting-application.md) — 11 September 2026 (21 months)
- [Standardisation Request M/606 Milestones](standardisation-m606-deadline.md) — European standardisation deliverables and enquiry milestones
- [Secondary Legislation Adoption](secondary-legislation-adoption.md) — Commission delegated and implementing acts (IR 2025/2392, DR 2025/1535, DR 2026/881)
- [General Application Date](main-application-date.md) — 11 December 2027 (36 months; full entry into application)
- [Transitional Support & Certificate Expiry](transitional-support-and-certificate-expiry.md) — Grandfathering, legacy product rules, and CSA certificate validity
