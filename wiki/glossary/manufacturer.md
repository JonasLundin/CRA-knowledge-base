# Summary

Under Article 3(18) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **manufacturer** means any natural or legal person who develops or manufactures products with digital elements or has products with digital elements designed, developed, or manufactured, and markets them under its name or trademark, whether for payment or free of charge.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Designates the principal economic operator legally accountable for Annex I essential cybersecurity requirements, conformity assessment, support periods, and Article 14 mandatory reporting.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Includes both entities that physically build products and entities that commission software/hardware and commercialise it under their own brand.[^cra-art-3]
