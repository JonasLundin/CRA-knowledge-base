# Summary

Article 2 determines which products with digital elements fall under the Regulation, establishing key exclusions for sectors governed by equivalent Union cybersecurity frameworks.

# Legal effect

Binds products intended or reasonably foreseeable to have direct or indirect digital connection to a device or network, while carving out excluded product regimes and non-commercial open source.

# Applicability

Governs all hardware and software products made available on the EU market, excluding national security/defence, spare parts, and specific regulated sectors.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 2 defines positive scope and statutory exclusions:
- **Positive scope**: Any software or hardware product and its remote data processing solutions whose intended or reasonably foreseeable use includes a direct or indirect logical or physical data connection to a device or network.
- **Sector exclusions**:
  - Medical devices and in vitro diagnostic medical devices covered by Regulations (EU) 2017/745 and (EU) 2017/746;
  - Civil aviation equipment falling within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2018/1139;
  - Motor vehicles and systems covered by Regulation (EU) 2019/2144, subject to Article 68 amendments;
  - Marine equipment falling within Directive 2014/90/EU where equivalent cybersecurity requirements apply.
- **Defence and national security**: Excludes products developed or modified exclusively for national security or defence purposes.
- **Open-source boundary**: Free and open-source software developed or supplied outside the course of a commercial activity is excluded from manufacturer obligations.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027. Commission empowerment under Article 2(5) to adopt delegated acts on exclusions applies from 11 December 2024.
