# Summary

Article 35 creates the statutory framework for Member States to notify third-party conformity assessment bodies to the Commission and other Member States.

# Legal effect

Imposes an obligation on Member States to establish notification mechanisms for conformity assessment bodies under the CRA.

# Applicability

Applies to EU Member States, national notifying authorities, and applicant conformity assessment bodies.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 35 provides:
- Member States shall notify to the Commission and to the other Member States the conformity assessment bodies authorised to carry out third-party conformity assessment tasks under the CRA.
- Only notified bodies meeting Article 39 requirements may perform conformity assessment under Modules B, C, and H.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026 pursuant to Article 71(2).
