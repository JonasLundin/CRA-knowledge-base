# Summary

prEN 40000-1-1 is the foundational horizontal European Standard developed by CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 in response to Standardisation Request M/606 Line 1[^m606][^stan4cra-jtc13]. It establishes the overarching principles for cyber resilience, cybersecurity-by-design, cybersecurity-by-default, and risk assessment methodologies across the entire lifecycle of products with digital elements[^cen-wi-81335].

# Legal effect

This document is a draft European Standard (prEN). It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27.

# Applicability

Applies horizontally to all products with digital elements within the scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847. It provides the architectural framework and methodology utilized both directly by manufacturers of default-class products and as a reference by standardizers developing vertical standards.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

prEN 40000-1-1 addresses:
- Cybersecurity risk assessment methodologies tailored to product intended purpose and reasonably foreseeable use;
- Cybersecurity-by-design and cybersecurity-by-default engineering lifecycle management;
- Threat modeling, security objective definition, and risk mitigation strategies;
- Supply chain security assessment and third-party component integration criteria;
- Coordinated governance between product engineering, manufacturing, and operational maintenance.
