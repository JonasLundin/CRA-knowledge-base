# Summary

Under Article 3(22) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, an **economic operator** means the manufacturer, the authorised representative, the importer, the distributor, or any other natural or legal person who is subject to obligations in relation to the manufacture of products with digital elements or making them available on the market under the CRA.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Identifies the regulated entities subject to market surveillance enforcement, inspection powers, and administrative penalties.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Collective category encompassing all commercial market participants in the lifecycle of a product with digital elements.[^cra-art-3]
