# Summary

Article 12 governs the intersection between the Cyber Resilience Act and Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act) for high-risk artificial intelligence systems.

# Legal effect

Integrates CRA essential requirements into AI Act compliance and enables a single, coordinated conformity assessment procedure.

# Applicability

Applies to products with digital elements qualifying as high-risk AI systems under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 12 establishes:
- High-risk AI systems falling within the scope of the CRA must fulfill CRA essential requirements set out in Annex I.
- Compliance with CRA essential requirements fulfills the cybersecurity requirements of Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
- A single conformity assessment procedure under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 may be used, incorporating CRA requirements, to avoid redundant assessments and administrative burden.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
