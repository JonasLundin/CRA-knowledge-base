# Summary

Article 47 governs the day-to-day operations of notified bodies, requiring proportionate assessments and setting criteria for issuing, refusing, or suspending certificates.

# Legal effect

Binds notified bodies to conduct assessments proportionally while strictly enforcing Annex I essential cybersecurity requirements.

# Applicability

Applies to notified bodies and manufacturers undergoing third-party conformity assessment.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 47 mandates:
- Notified bodies shall carry out conformity assessments in proportion to the size of economic operators and product complexity, avoiding unnecessary burdens for SMEs.
- If a notified body finds that Annex I essential requirements, harmonised standards, or common specifications have not been met, it shall require the manufacturer to take appropriate corrective measures and shall not issue a conformity certificate.
- Where compliance issues arise after certificate issuance during monitoring, the notified body shall suspend or withdraw the certificate.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
