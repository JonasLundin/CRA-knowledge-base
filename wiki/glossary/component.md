# Summary

Under Article 3(5) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **component** means software or hardware intended to be integrated into a product with digital elements.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Components placed on the market separately as independent commercial products are themselves products with digital elements. When integrated by downstream manufacturers, components are subject to due diligence under Article 13(5) and SBOM inclusion under Annex I.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Includes third-party software libraries, SDKs, open-source modules, microcontrollers, communication chipsets, and physical sub-modules.[^cra-art-3]
