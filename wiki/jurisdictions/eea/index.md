# EEA EFTA States

Status and jurisdictional application of the Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) in European Economic Area (EEA) EFTA States.

## EEA EFTA Jurisdictions

- [Iceland](iceland.md) (IS)
- [Liechtenstein](liechtenstein.md) (LI)
- [Norway](norway.md) (NO)

## EEA Incorporation Mechanism

The CRA is marked as a "Text with EEA relevance". Under the EEA Agreement:
1. **Scrutiny & Assessment**: The EEA EFTA States assess the act for EEA relevance and identify any necessary adaptations.
2. **Joint Committee Decision**: The EEA Joint Committee must adopt a decision incorporating the CRA into Annex II (Technical regulations, standards, testing and certification) or Annex XI of the EEA Agreement (Article 102).
3. **Constitutional Requirements**: If an EEA EFTA State indicates that constitutional requirements must be met (e.g. parliamentary approval under Norwegian Constitution Article 26 or Icelandic constitutional procedure), the decision enters into force pursuant to EEA Agreement Article 103 upon notification that all constitutional requirements are fulfilled.
4. **National Transposition**: Statutory enactments are passed in the respective national official gazettes.

As of 2026-08-21, incorporation into the EEA Agreement remains in progress and the CRA has not yet entered into force in Iceland, Liechtenstein, or Norway.
