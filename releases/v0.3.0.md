## 2. Executive Summary & Regulatory Context

Following the **31 October 2026** European Commission Standardisation Request (`M/606` / `C(2025) 618`) delivery milestone for foundational horizontal standards and the first two months of live Article 14 reporting on the ENISA Single Reporting Platform (SRP), several major normative developments occurred across European standardisation bodies, ENISA, and EU cybersecurity certification frameworks:

1. **CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 Ratification & Availability of `EN 40000-1-1` and `EN 40000-1-2` + Horizontal Series Re-Alignment:** Under the official CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 (PT1–PT4) structure, `EN 40000-1-1` is the horizontal *Vocabulary*, `EN 40000-1-2` covers *Principles for cyber resilience* (M/606 Line 01), `prEN 40000-1-3` covers *Vulnerability handling* (M/606 Line 15), `prEN 40000-1-4` covers *Generic security requirements* (M/606 Lines 02–14), and `CEN/CLC/TR 40000-1-5` provides supporting guidance on *Threats and security objectives*. Following CEN Formal Vote closure on **1 October 2026**, `EN 40000-1-1` and `EN 40000-1-2` were formally **ratified on 2 November 2026** with target availability on **18 November 2026**. Crucially, CEN-CENELEC project records (`WI 81335`) indicate **"no OJEU citation expected"** for Parts `1-1` and `1-2` (they serve as foundational terminology and risk-management methodology frameworks referenced by citable standards, whereas `prEN 40000-1-3` *Vulnerability handling* is targeted for OJEU citation).
2. **ENISA *Technical Advisory for Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)* (22 October 2026) & SRP `v1.2.1` Update (20 October 2026):** On **22 October 2026**, ENISA published its *Technical Advisory for Software Bill of Materials* (building on its 9 June 2026 *SBOM Adoption State of Play - 2026* report), establishing a six-phase continuous SBOM lifecycle (generation in SPDX/CycloneDX, validation, cryptographic signing, secure storage, consumption, maintenance), guidance on declaring **"known unknowns"** for unresolved transitive dependencies, and a **STRIDE-based SBOM threat model**. Concurrently, on **20 October 2026**, ENISA deployed **SRP `v1.2.1`** (`portal.cra-srp.enisa.europa.eu`), updating the invitation and delegation workflow for Secondary Assigned Representatives (AR Backup Users).
3. **July 2026 M/606 Deadline Amendment & Concrete 2026 Vertical `prEN` Numbers:** Reconciled the European Commission's July 2026 amendment to M/606 Annex II shifting 2026 deadlines by two months (**31 October 2026** for Type A Principles & Type B Vulnerability Handling; **31 December 2026** for Type C Vertical Product-Specific Standards; **30 October 2027** for Type B Generic Security Requirements `prEN 40000-1-4`) and mapped all 2026 vertical draft European Standard numbers across **CEN/TC 224 WG 17** (`prEN 40000-10`, `prEN 40000-11`, `prEN 18330`), **CLC/TC 47X** (`prEN 50765:2026`, `prEN 50766:2026`, `prEN 50764:2026`), **CLC/TC 65X WG 3** (`prEN 50770-1` through `prEN 50770-6`), and **ETSI TC CYBER / WG EUSR** (`EN 304 642` for telecommunications network functions).
4. **EUCC & Cybersecurity Act Amendments + Article 27(9) Delegated Act Precision:** Integrated **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/3144** and **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2462** (adding "product series" certification and "minor/major change" assurance continuity rules to the EUCC scheme) alongside **Regulation (EU) 2025/37** (expanding the Cybersecurity Act certification framework to managed security services), and corrected CRA Article 27(8)–(9) references to reflect that qualifying European cybersecurity certification schemes are specified via **delegated acts** (in accordance with Article 61).

## 5. Implementation & Operational Guidance (Downstream Impact)

### 5.1 Standards & Conformity Assessment Teams
1. **Update Internal Standards Traceability Matrices:** Map CRA Annex I Part I point 1 (risk assessment & cybersecurity-by-design) to ratified **`EN 40000-1-2`** (noting that it provides the risk-assessment methodology for technical documentation rather than a standalone OJEU presumption of conformity), CRA Annex I Part II (vulnerability handling) to OJEU-targeted **`prEN 40000-1-3`**, and CRA Annex I Part I points 2(a)–(m) (technical security controls) to **`prEN 40000-1-4`**.
2. **Track Vertical Deliverables Ahead of 31 December 2026:** Manufacturers of IAM/PAM systems (`prEN 40000-10`), HSMs/security boxes (`prEN 40000-11`), smartcards/secure elements (`prEN 18330` and `prEN 50764:2026`), semiconductors (`prEN 50765:2026` and `prEN 50766:2026`), OT network/security appliances (`prEN 50770-1` through `-6`), and telecom network functions (`ETSI EN 304 642`) should track vertical standard finalization ahead of the **31 December 2026** M/606 milestone.

### 5.2 DevSecOps, Reporting & EUCC Certification Teams
1. **Align SBOM Pipelines with ENISA's 22 October 2026 Advisory:** Automate SPDX/CycloneDX generation, schema validation, and cryptographic signing in CI/CD, and explicitly record "known unknowns" where transitive dependency resolution is incomplete.
2. **Verify SRP `v1.2.1` Backup Delegation:** Ensure Primary Assigned Representatives verify their manufacturer association with the coordinating CSIRT so Secondary AR (backup user) invitations can be issued and accepted within the 7-day window.
