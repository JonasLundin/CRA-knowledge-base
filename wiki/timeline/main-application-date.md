# Summary

Under Article 71(2) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, the Cyber Resilience Act enters into full horizontal application across the European Union on 11 December 2027 (36 months after its entry into force).[^cra-art-71]

# Legal effect

From 11 December 2027, no product with digital elements within the scope of the CRA may be placed on the EU market unless it complies with all Annex I essential requirements, bears the CE marking, and has completed the appropriate conformity assessment procedure.[^cra-art-71] [^cra-art-13]

# Applicability

Applies to all economic operators (manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, distributors) and all new products with digital elements placed on the EU market.[^cra-art-71]

# Requirements or coverage

On and after 11 December 2027:
1. **Annex I essential requirements:** Full compliance with Part I (security by design and default) and Part II (vulnerability handling);
2. **Conformity assessment & CE mark:** Mandatory Module A, Module B+C, Module H, or Cybersecurity Certification;
3. **Technical documentation & SBOM:** Fully drawn up and retained under Annex VII;
4. **Declared support periods:** Publicly declared and supported with free security updates;
5. **Full market surveillance:** Active enforcement and administrative fine powers under Articles 52–64 take full effect.[^cra-art-71]

# Dates and transitions

- **11 December 2027:** General application date.
