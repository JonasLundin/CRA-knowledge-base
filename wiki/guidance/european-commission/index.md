# European Commission Guidance

This section catalogues official guidance, communications, explanatory pages, and frequently asked questions published by the European Commission (DG CNECT) to support the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

All guidance issued by the European Commission under Article 26 or general policy channels is non-binding and intended to facilitate uniform application across the Union.
