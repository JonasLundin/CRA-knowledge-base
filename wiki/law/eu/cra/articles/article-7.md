# Summary

Article 7 establishes the regime for important products with digital elements, categorising them into Class I and Class II based on their cybersecurity functionality and impact.

# Legal effect

Subjects listed product categories to specific conformity assessment procedures under Article 32 and empowers the Commission to amend Annex III lists and adopt technical descriptions.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers of products belonging to categories listed in Annex III.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 7 defines:
- **Classification**: Important products are classified into Class I (Annex III list 1) and Class II (Annex III list 2) based on cybersecurity-related functionality, core function, and risk of widespread disruption.
- **Delegated acts**: Commission is empowered to adopt delegated acts under Article 61 to amend Annex III by adding or removing product categories.
- **Implementing acts**: Commission must adopt implementing acts specifying technical descriptions of Annex III categories.
- **Conformity assessment link**: Triggers Article 32(2) and 32(3) conformity assessment procedures.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027. Empowerments for secondary acts apply from 11 December 2024.
