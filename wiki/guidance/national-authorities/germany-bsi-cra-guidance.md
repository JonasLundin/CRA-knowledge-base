# Summary

The German Federal Office for Information Security (*Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik* - BSI) publishes official guidance and actionable recommendations for companies developing, manufacturing, or distributing products with digital elements in Germany [^bsi-cra-guide].

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and advisory under German and EU law [^bsi-cra-guide]. It assists market participants in preparing for compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and does not supersede Union law or German national implementing legislation.

# Applicability

Applies to hardware and software manufacturers, system integrators, importers, and distributors operating in or supplying the German market.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The BSI guidance covers practical compliance preparation [^bsi-cra-guide]:

- **Cybersecurity Risk Assessment & Baselines:** Practical guidance on implementing risk-based development lifecycles in alignment with BSI standards (e.g., IT-Grundschutz) and international norms (IEC 62443).
- **Vulnerability Management & Coordinated Disclosure:** Advisories on setting up Product Security Incident Response Teams (PSIRTs), establishing RFC 9116 `security.txt` discovery files, and managing vulnerability intake.
- **Reporting Routing to CERT-Bund:** Information on the interaction between national reporting channels and the ENISA Single Reporting Platform for German manufacturers.
- **SME and Industry Preparedness:** Checklists and gap-analysis templates specifically tailored to German industrial and Mittelstand technology providers.

# Dates and transitions

- **Publication Timeline:** Published in July 2025 and updated in May 2026 to incorporate secondary legislative acts.
