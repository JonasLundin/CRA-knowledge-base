# Summary

prEN 40000-1-2 is a horizontal draft European Standard developed by CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 under Standardisation Request M/606 Lines 2 through 14[^m606][^stan4cra-jtc13]. It provides a comprehensive catalogue of generic technical security requirements and engineering controls corresponding to CRA Annex I Part I points 2(a) through 2(m)[^cen-wi-81344].

# Legal effect

This document is a draft European Standard (prEN). It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27.

# Applicability

Applies horizontally across all hardware and software products with digital elements. Manufacturers of default products can use its technical specifications directly to demonstrate compliance with Annex I Part I point 2 essential requirements.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

prEN 40000-1-2 translates CRA essential requirements into technical controls:
- **Point 2(a)**: Delivering products without known exploitable vulnerabilities;
- **Point 2(b)**: Secure default configurations and secure reset mechanisms;
- **Point 2(c)**: Access control, identification, and authentication;
- **Point 2(d)**: Confidentiality of stored, transmitted, or processed data;
- **Point 2(e)**: Data and system integrity protection;
- **Point 2(f)**: Data minimisation;
- **Point 2(g)**: Essential function availability and Denial-of-Service resilience;
- **Point 2(h)**: Limiting negative impacts on external devices and networks;
- **Point 2(i)**: Attack surface reduction and interface limitation;
- **Point 2(j)**: Exploit mitigation and incident severity reduction;
- **Point 2(k)**: Security event logging, auditing, and monitoring;
- **Point 2(l)**: Secure disposal, decommissioning, and data sanitisation;
- **Point 2(m)**: Cryptographically verified and secure update mechanisms.
