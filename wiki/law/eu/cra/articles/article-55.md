# Summary

Article 55 establishes the Union safeguard procedure where objections are raised against national provisional measures taken under Article 54.

# Legal effect

Empowers the Commission to decide whether a national restrictive measure is legally justified, binding all Member States.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission, Member States, and affected economic operators.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 55 establishes:
- Where objections are raised by a Member State or the Commission within three months of notification under Article 54, the Commission enters into consultation with the relevant parties and evaluates the national measure.
- The Commission adopts an implementing act determining whether the national measure is justified or unjustified.
- If justified, all Member States must take necessary measures to ensure the non-compliant product is withdrawn/restricted in their territory; if unjustified, the initiating Member State must withdraw its measure.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
