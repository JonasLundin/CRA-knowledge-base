# Importer & Distributor Obligations

Overview of supply-chain due diligence, verification duties, and re-branding rules for importers and distributors under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Concepts

- [Importer Obligations](importer.md) — Verification duties before placing third-country products on the EU market (Article 16)
- [Distributor Obligations](distributor.md) — Due care, CE marking verification, and supply chain duties (Article 17)
- [Modification & Rebranding](modification-and-rebranding.md) — Circumstances where importers or distributors become subject to manufacturer obligations (Article 18)
