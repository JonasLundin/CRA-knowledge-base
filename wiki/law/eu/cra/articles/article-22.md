# Summary

Article 22 addresses substantial modifications carried out by third parties other than importers or distributors.

# Legal effect

Imposes manufacturer legal status and full compliance responsibilities on any natural or legal person carrying out a substantial modification.

# Applicability

Applies to any entity modifying a product with digital elements in a manner affecting essential requirements.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 22 establishes:
- Any natural or legal person who carries out a substantial modification to a product with digital elements and makes it available on the market is considered a manufacturer for the modified part or the entire product.
- That person must ensure compliance with Annex I essential requirements and carry out the appropriate conformity assessment procedure under Article 32.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
