# Summary

ETSI Technical Committee CYBER operates Working Group EU Standardisation Requests (WG EUSR)[^etsi-tc-cyber][^stan4cra-etsi] to draft harmonised European Standards (EN) for vertical digital product categories under Commission Standardisation Request M/606[^m606]. ETSI publishes working drafts and public enquiry deliverables in an Open Consultation Area (`docbox.etsi.org/CYBER/EUSR/Open`)[^etsi-open-docbox].

# Legal effect

Work items, drafts, and committee deliverables published by ETSI TC CYBER/EUSR do not confer presumption of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 until approved as final European Standards (EN) and cited in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU).

# Responsibilities and work allocation

ETSI TC CYBER WG EUSR is responsible for 17 vertical product deliverables under the EN 304 series:

- **Line 17 (EN 304-617)**: Standalone and embedded browsers
- **Line 18 (EN 304-618)**: Password managers
- **Line 19 (EN 304-619)**: Antivirus software
- **Line 20 (EN 304-620)**: Virtual private networks (VPN)
- **Line 21 (EN 304-621)**: Network management systems (NMS)
- **Line 22 (EN 304-622)**: Security information and event management (SIEM) systems
- **Line 23 (EN 304-623)**: Boot managers
- **Line 24 (EN 304-624)**: Public-key infrastructure (PKI) and certificate issuance software
- **Line 25 (EN 304-625)**: Physical and virtual network interfaces
- **Line 26 (EN 304-626)**: Operating systems
- **Line 27 (EN 304-627)**: Routers, modems, and switches
- **Line 31 (EN 304-631)**: Smart-home general-purpose virtual assistants
- **Line 32 (EN 304-632)**: Smart-home products with security functionality
- **Line 33 (EN 304-633)**: Internet-connected toys
- **Line 34 (EN 304-634)**: Personal wearable products
- **Line 35 (EN 304-635)**: Virtualisation and container systems (hypervisors, container runtimes)
- **Line 36 (EN 304-636)**: Firewalls and intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS)

# Consultation and public drafts

ETSI TC CYBER/EUSR conducts open public consultation cycles using standard commenting spreadsheets (`ETSI Commenting Format for Open Consultation`) and publishes comments resolution matrices (`OC1`, `OC2`, `OC3`)[^etsi-open-docbox].
