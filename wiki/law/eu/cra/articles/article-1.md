# Summary

Article 1 defines the objective and scope of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, setting out horizontal cybersecurity rules for placing products with digital elements on the Union market.

# Legal effect

Sets binding harmonised Union rules covering essential cybersecurity requirements, manufacturer and supply chain obligations, conformity assessment procedures, and market surveillance mechanisms.

# Applicability

Applies across the European Union to all economic operators placing or making available products with digital elements on the Union market.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 1 sets out the three core pillars of the Cyber Resilience Act:
- Rules for placing products with digital elements on the market to ensure their cybersecurity;
- Essential requirements for the design, development, and production of products with digital elements, as well as vulnerability handling processes;
- Obligations for economic operators (manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers, distributors) and open-source software stewards;
- Rules on market surveillance, enforcement, and monitoring across EU Member States.

# Dates and transitions

Enters into force on 11 December 2024; applies generally from 11 December 2027 pursuant to Article 71.
