# Summary

Article 5 clarifies the interaction between the CRA and national public procurement, enabling contracting authorities to require CRA compliance and security properties.

# Legal effect

Empowers Member States and public authorities to condition public procurement and internal deployment on compliance with essential cybersecurity requirements.

# Applicability

Applies to Member State contracting authorities, contracting entities, and public sector bodies procuring products with digital elements.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 5 provides:
- Member States may establish requirements promoting or requiring that products with digital elements procured or used by public authorities meet essential cybersecurity requirements.
- Such measures must remain without prejudice to Directives 2014/24/EU and 2014/25/EU on public procurement.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
