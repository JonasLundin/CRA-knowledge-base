# Summary

Article 25 introduces voluntary security attestation programs enabling open-source software developers and stewards to demonstrate adherence to secure development standards.

# Legal effect

Provides a legal basis for voluntary security attestations that downstream manufacturers can use to satisfy supply chain due diligence under Article 13(5).

# Applicability

Applies voluntarily to open-source software developers, stewards, and downstream integrating manufacturers.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 25 provides:
- Open-source software stewards and developers may issue voluntary European cybersecurity attestations or third-party audit reports.
- Manufacturers integrating open-source software may rely on such attestations as part of their due diligence obligations under Article 13(5).
- Commission may adopt implementing acts specifying attestation templates and criteria.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
