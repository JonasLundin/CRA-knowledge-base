# Summary

Pursuant to Article 71(2)(a) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Article 14 reporting obligations for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents become legally binding on 11 September 2026 (21 months post-entry into force).[^cra-art-71] [^cra-art-14]

# Legal effect

From this date forward, manufacturers are legally required to report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents within 24 hours (early warning) and 72 hours (notification) via the Single Reporting Platform (SRP). Failure to notify constitutes an enforceable statutory violation.[^cra-art-14]

# Applicability

Applies across the EU to all manufacturers of products with digital elements placed on the market, including legacy products actively supported or available on the market (Article 69(3)).[^cra-art-14]

# Requirements or coverage

From 11 September 2026:
1. The Single Reporting Platform (SRP) operated by ENISA goes live for mandatory production reporting;[^enisa-srp-docs]
2. Manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities (Article 14(1)) and severe incidents (Article 14(3));
3. Designated national CSIRTs receive simultaneous encrypted feeds and initiate coordination;
4. Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881 on dissemination delay rules enters into full operational effect.

# Dates and transitions

- **11 September 2026:** Mandatory reporting takes effect.
