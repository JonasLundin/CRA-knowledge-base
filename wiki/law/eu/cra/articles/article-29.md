# Summary

Article 29 establishes that the CE marking on products with digital elements is governed by the general principles set out in Article 30 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.

# Legal effect

Establishes CE marking as the visible declaration of conformity with all applicable Union harmonisation legislation.

# Applicability

Applies to economic operators affixing or distributing CE marked products with digital elements.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 29 provides:
- The CE marking shall be subject to the general principles set out in Article 30 of Regulation (EC) No 765/2008.
- By affixing or having affixed the CE marking, the manufacturer takes full responsibility for product conformity with all applicable Union requirements.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
