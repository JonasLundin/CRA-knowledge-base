# Summary

Article 8 establishes the regime for critical products with digital elements, empowering the Commission to require European cybersecurity certification schemes under Regulation (EU) 2019/881.

# Legal effect

Imposes the highest tier of conformity assessment obligations on Annex IV products and empowers mandatory European cybersecurity certification.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers of critical products with digital elements listed in Annex IV.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 8 provides:
- **Critical category**: Products listed in Annex IV present critical cybersecurity risk due to their essential function in sensitive environments or infrastructure.
- **Mandatory certification**: Commission is empowered to adopt delegated acts requiring manufacturers of critical products to obtain a European cybersecurity certificate under an adopted EU scheme (Regulation (EU) 2019/881) at assurance level 'substantial' or 'high'.
- **Conformity assessment**: Pending certification acts, critical products must undergo third-party conformity assessment under Article 32(6) (Module B+C or Module H).
- **Delegated updates**: Commission is empowered to amend Annex IV categories via delegated acts.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027. Delegated empowerments apply from 11 December 2024.
