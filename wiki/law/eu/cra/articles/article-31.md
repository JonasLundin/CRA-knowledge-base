# Summary

Article 31 requires manufacturers to compile and maintain comprehensive technical documentation demonstrating compliance with Annex I essential requirements.

# Legal effect

Imposes a legal obligation to create, maintain, and provide technical documentation to market surveillance authorities upon request.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers and authorised representatives of products with digital elements.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 31 establishes:
- Technical documentation must contain all relevant data and details of the means used by the manufacturer to ensure compliance with Annex I, as detailed in Annex VII.
- Must include the cybersecurity risk assessment, design architecture, software bill of materials (SBOM) or component inventory, test reports, and vulnerability handling documentation.
- Must be drawn up before the product is placed on the market and kept continuously updated.
- Must be retained for at least 10 years after placing on the market or for the product support period, whichever is longer.
- Commission is empowered to adopt implementing acts specifying simplified technical documentation formats for microenterprises and SMEs.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
