# Summary

The European Commission's *Guidance on Standardisation and Harmonised Standards* outlines the European standardisation process under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 and Standardisation Request M/606 (Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 618) [^ec-cra-standardisation]. It details how harmonised European standards are developed, assessed, and cited in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU) to confer a presumption of conformity.

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and explanatory [^ec-cra-standardisation]. It explains the legal mechanism of Article 27 but does not itself confer presumption of conformity. Only the formal publication of harmonised standard references in the OJEU confers presumption of conformity under EU law.

# Applicability

Relevant for European Standardisation Organisations (CEN, CENELEC, ETSI), national standardisation bodies, technical committees, manufacturers, and conformity assessment bodies.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The guidance explains the standardisation framework and lifecycle [^ec-cra-standardisation]:

- **Standardisation Request M/606 (C(2025) 618):** Mandate issued by the European Commission under Regulation (EU) No 1025/2012 requesting CEN, CENELEC, and ETSI to draft 41 European standards covering horizontal cybersecurity requirements and vertical product categories.
- **Horizontal vs. Vertical Standards:**
  - *Horizontal standards (M/606 lines 1–15):* Generic cybersecurity and vulnerability handling requirements (e.g., the emerging EN 40000 family).
  - *Vertical standards (M/606 lines 16–41):* Specific product categories listed in Annex III and Annex IV (e.g., firewalls, routers, smart-home devices, operating systems, password managers).
- **Presumption of Conformity (Article 27):** Products that conform to harmonised standards (or parts thereof) whose references have been published in the OJEU benefit from a legal presumption of conformity with the Annex I essential requirements covered by those standards.
- **Common Specifications (Article 28):** Fallback mechanism allowing the Commission to adopt implementing acts laying down technical specifications where harmonised standards are delayed, unavailable, or insufficient.
- **Interim Reliance:** Clarifies that during the transitional period prior to OJEU citation, manufacturers may use existing international or European standards (e.g., IEC 62443, ETSI EN 303 645) as supporting technical evidence, but such use does not confer statutory presumption of conformity.

# Dates and transitions

- **M/606 Adoption:** Early 2025.
- **Target OJEU Citations:** Phased delivery of final standards by ESOs throughout 2026–2027 to enable adoption prior to the December 2027 application date.
