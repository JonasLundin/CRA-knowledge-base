# CRA Secondary Legislation

This directory contains concepts for adopted delegated and implementing regulations supplementing or implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, together with a register of all CRA empowerments.

## Adopted Acts

- [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1535](delegated-regulation-eu-2025-1535.md): Exclusion of certain two- and three-wheel vehicles and quadricycles under Regulation (EU) No 168/2013.
- [Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392](implementing-regulation-eu-2025-2392.md): Technical descriptions of categories of important and critical products with digital elements.
- [Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/881](delegated-regulation-eu-2026-881.md): Terms and conditions for delaying dissemination of notifications via the Single Reporting Platform.

## Register of Empowerments

- [Future Secondary Legislation Register](register.md): Comprehensive inventory of CRA delegated acts, implementing acts, common specifications, and official guidelines.
