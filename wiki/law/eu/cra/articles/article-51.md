# Summary

Article 51 establishes a sectoral coordination and cooperation group of notified bodies to ensure consistent application of CRA conformity assessment procedures.

# Legal effect

Mandates the establishment of a sectoral group and requires notified bodies to participate directly or via designated representatives.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission and all CRA notified bodies.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 51 establishes:
- The Commission shall ensure that appropriate coordination and cooperation between notified bodies are put in place and properly operated in the form of a sectoral group of notified bodies.
- Notified bodies shall participate in the work of that group, directly or by means of designated representatives.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
