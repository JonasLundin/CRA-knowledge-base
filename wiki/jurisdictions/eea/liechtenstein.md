# EEA legal status and incorporation

Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 is marked by the EU co-legislators as a "Text with EEA relevance".
- EEA Status: As an EEA EFTA State, EU regulations do not apply directly in Liechtenstein upon EU entry into force.
- Incorporation procedure: The act must be formally incorporated into Annex II (Technical regulations, standards, testing and certification) and/or Annex XI of the EEA Agreement by a Decision of the EEA Joint Committee pursuant to EEA Agreement Article 102[^efta-eea-lex].
- Incorporation status: Pending incorporation as of 2026-08-21.
