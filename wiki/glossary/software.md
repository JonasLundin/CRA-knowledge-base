# Summary

Under Article 3(3) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, **software** means the part of an electronic information system which consists of computer code.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Encompasses all executable code, scripts, microcode, firmware, operating systems, and standalone applications that are placed on the market or integrated into hardware products.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Includes both source and binary forms across desktop, server, mobile, cloud, and embedded implementations, whether distributed via physical media or digital downloads.[^cra-art-3]
