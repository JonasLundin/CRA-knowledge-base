# Summary

Article 20 establishes the due care obligations of distributors making products with digital elements available on the Union market.

# Legal effect

Binds distributors to verify visible compliance markers and refrain from distributing products suspected of non-compliance.

# Applicability

Applies to all distributors in the Union supply chain making products with digital elements available on the market.

# Requirements or coverage

Key obligations under Article 20 include:
- **Due care**: Act with due care in relation to CRA requirements when making products available on the market.
- **Verification**: Verify that the product bears the CE marking, and that manufacturer and importer contact details and Annex II instructions are provided.
- **Non-distribution of non-compliant products**: If a product is suspected of presenting a cybersecurity risk, withhold distribution until brought into conformity, and inform the manufacturer/importer and market surveillance authorities.
- **Corrective action**: Ensure necessary corrective actions are taken for non-compliant products already made available (bringing into conformity, withdrawal, or recall).

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
