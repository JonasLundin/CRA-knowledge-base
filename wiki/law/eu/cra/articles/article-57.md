# Summary

Article 57 establishes an emergency mechanism for cases where a product conforms formally to CRA requirements but nonetheless presents an unforeseen significant cybersecurity risk.

# Legal effect

Allows national authorities and the Commission to mandate corrective measures or withdrawals for formally compliant products that pose unexpected threats.

# Applicability

Applies to market surveillance authorities, the Commission, and economic operators.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 57 provides:
- Where an authority finds that a product complies with this Regulation but nonetheless presents a significant cybersecurity risk, it requires the operator to take all appropriate measures to eliminate the risk, withdraw the product, or recall it.
- The authority immediately informs the Commission and Member States.
- The Commission evaluates the measure and adopts an implementing act declaring the measure justified or unjustified.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
