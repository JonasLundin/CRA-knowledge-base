# Summary

EN 304 634 is an ETSI draft European Standard under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 34[^m606], defining essential cybersecurity requirements for personal wearable products under CRA Annex III Class I point 19[^etsi-docbox-634].

# Legal effect

This document is an enquiry draft. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** presumption of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Smartwatches and connected fitness wristbands;
- Wearable health monitors and activity trackers;
- Smart glasses and connected apparel.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

EN 304 634 addresses:
- Biometric, vital sign, and GPS location data encryption at rest and in transit;
- Secure pairing and bonding protocols for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and NFC;
- Prevention of BLE MAC address tracking and advertising packet profiling;
- On-device screen lock and biometric unlock protection for stored messages and health records;
- Cryptographically signed firmware update over-the-air (FOTA) mechanisms.
