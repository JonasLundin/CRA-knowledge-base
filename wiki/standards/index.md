# Cyber Resilience Act Standards

This section contains structured information on standardisation activities supporting Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Structure

- [Standards Development Process](process/) — How CRA harmonised standards are built from standardisation request M/606 to OJEU citation.
- [Technical Committees](committees/) — European Standardisation Organisation (ESO) technical committees responsible for CRA deliverables.
- [Standardisation Request M/606](m606/) — Comprehensive index of all 41 request lines in Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 618.
- [Harmonised Standards](harmonised/) — European Standards and public enquiry drafts (EN 40000 family and EN 304 series).
- [Supporting Standards](supporting/) — Relevant international and European supporting standards (EN IEC 62443, ETSI EN 303 645, ISO/IEC 29147, ISO/IEC 30111).
- [Certification Schemes](certification-schemes/) — European cybersecurity certification schemes under Regulation (EU) 2019/881 (EUCC).
