# Summary

Articles 54, 57, and 58 of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 establish escalating enforcement procedures for market surveillance authorities when products with digital elements are non-compliant or present significant cybersecurity risks.[^cra-art-54] [^cra-art-57] [^cra-art-58]

# Legal effect

Authorities may mandate immediate corrective measures, restrict or prohibit the making available of the product on the market, or order its withdrawal or recall at the operator's expense.[^cra-art-54]

# Applicability

Applies across the EU single market to any economic operator marketing non-compliant products.[^cra-art-54]

# Requirements or coverage

Enforcement follows a structured escalation path:

### 1. Formal Non-Compliance (Article 58)
Where an administrative deficiency exists (e.g. CE mark incorrectly affixed, DoC missing, technical documentation incomplete), the MSA requires the operator to end the non-compliance within a reasonable timeframe. If non-compliance persists, the MSA restricts or withdraws the product.[^cra-art-58]

### 2. Products Presenting Significant Cybersecurity Risks (Article 54)
1. **Evaluation & Notice:** The MSA conducts an evaluation and requires the economic operator to take all appropriate corrective measures within a prescribed period (e.g. deploy security updates, rectify configuration vulnerabilities);
2. **Failure to Remediate:** If the operator fails to take adequate corrective action within the timeframe, the MSA adopts provisional measures prohibiting or restricting the making available of the product, or ordering its withdrawal or recall;
3. **Union Safeguard Procedure (Article 55):** The national measure is notified to the Commission and other Member States. If no objections are raised within three months, the measure is deemed justified across all EU Member States.[^cra-art-54]

### 3. Emergency Interventions (Article 57)
In exceptional circumstances presenting imminent cybersecurity risks to critical infrastructure or public safety, MSAs may order immediate product withdrawal or blocking of connections.[^cra-art-57]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
