# Summary

Article 2(7)–(9) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 establishes explicit exemptions from the CRA for products with digital elements designed, developed, or modified exclusively for national security, defence, or classified information handling.[^cra-art-2]

# Legal effect

Products falling within these exemptions are not subject to the cybersecurity requirements, reporting duties, conformity assessment procedures, or market surveillance mechanisms of the Cyber Resilience Act.[^cra-art-2]

# Applicability

The exemptions apply strictly in the following cases:

1. **Military and defence:** Products with digital elements developed or modified exclusively for military or defence purposes (Article 2(7));[^cra-art-2]
2. **National security:** Products specifically designed to protect national security interests of Member States (Article 2(7));[^cra-art-2]
3. **Classified information:** Products specifically designed to process EU classified information (EUCI) or national classified information at an equivalent level (Article 2(8)).[^cra-art-2]

Dual-use products or commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) products that are not designed *exclusively* for defence or national security remain within the general scope of the CRA when placed on the commercial market.[^cra-art-2]

# Requirements or coverage

- Member States retain sole competence for their essential state functions, including territorial integrity and national security (Article 2(9)).[^cra-art-2]
- The exemption does not extend to commercial hardware or software merely because it is purchased by a public entity or defence contractor, unless it meets the exclusivity criteria.

# Dates and transitions

- Applicable horizontally with the full CRA regime from 11 December 2027.
