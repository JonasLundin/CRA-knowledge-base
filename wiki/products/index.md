# Products with Digital Elements

Product classification and category requirements under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act) and Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2392.

## Product Classes & Conformity Routes

- [Default Products](default/index.md) — Products with digital elements not listed in Annex III or IV; assessed via self-assessment (Module A) under Article 32(1)
- [Important Products — Class I](important-class-i/index.md) — Products listed in Annex III Class I; assessed via Module A (with harmonised standards) or third-party Module B+C / Module H under Article 32(2)
- [Important Products — Class II](important-class-ii/index.md) — High-risk products listed in Annex III Class II; subject to mandatory third-party assessment (Module B+C or Module H) under Article 32(3)
- [Critical Products](critical/index.md) — Highly critical products listed in Annex IV; subject to mandatory European cybersecurity certification at assurance level 'high' under Article 32(4)
