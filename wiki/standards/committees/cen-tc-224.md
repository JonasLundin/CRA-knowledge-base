# Summary

CEN/TC 224 (Personal identification and related personal devices with secure element, systems, operations and privacy in a multi-sectorial environment)[^cen-tc224][^stan4cra-tc224] develops vertical European Standards for identity systems, hardware security devices, and secure elements under Standardisation Request M/606[^m606].

# Legal effect

Deliverables and draft specifications produced by CEN/TC 224 do not confer presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27 until formal European Standard (EN) adoption and citation in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU).

# Responsibilities and work allocation

CEN/TC 224 Working Group 17 (WG 17) is assigned three key vertical areas in M/606:

- **Line 16**: European standard(s) on essential cybersecurity requirements for identity management systems and privileged access management (PAM) software and hardware, including authentication and access control readers, including biometric readers (CRA Annex III Class I point 1).
- **Line 39**: European standard(s) on essential cybersecurity requirements for Hardware Devices with Security Boxes (Hardware Security Modules, cryptographic hardware, CRA Annex III Class II point 5).
- **Line 41**: European standard(s) on criteria and security requirements for Smartcards, similar devices, and Secure Elements to achieve conformity with CRA essential requirements (CRA Annex III Class II point 6 / Annex IV, in coordination with CLC/TC 47X WG 3).
