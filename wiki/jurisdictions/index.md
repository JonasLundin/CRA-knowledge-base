# Jurisdictions

Catalogue of national authorities, market surveillance bodies, CSIRT routing, and legislative enforcement frameworks for Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Sections

- [EU Member States](eu-member-states/index.md): All 27 European Union Member States with national notifying authorities, market surveillance authorities, NIS2-aligned CSIRT coordinators, and penalty frameworks.
- [EEA EFTA States](eea/index.md): European Economic Area (EEA) EFTA States (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway) tracking formal incorporation status into the EEA Agreement.

## Legislative Architecture

The CRA applies directly across all 27 EU Member States. National designations of notifying authorities (Article 35), market surveillance authorities (Article 52), and penalty regimes (Article 64) are established under national legal frameworks.

For EEA EFTA States (Iceland, Liechtenstein, and Norway), the CRA constitutes a text with EEA relevance that requires formal incorporation into Annex II/Annex XI of the EEA Agreement by Decision of the EEA Joint Committee before taking legal effect in those jurisdictions.
