# Summary

Article 63 obligates all parties involved in CRA application to safeguard confidential commercial information, trade secrets, source code, and national security data.

# Legal effect

Imposes statutory confidentiality and professional secrecy obligations on authorities, notified bodies, ENISA, and Commission personnel.

# Applicability

Applies to all public authorities, notified bodies, ENISA, the Commission, and third-party experts.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 63 mandates:
- All parties must respect the confidentiality of information obtained in carrying out their tasks, protecting:
  - Intellectual property rights and trade secrets in accordance with Directive (EU) 2016/943;
  - Source code and internal technical design documentation;
  - The effective implementation of CRA requirements (especially avoiding premature vulnerability disclosure);
  - Public security and national defence interests;
  - The conduct of criminal or administrative investigations.
- Information exchanged between authorities on a confidential basis shall not be disclosed without prior agreement of the originating authority.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
