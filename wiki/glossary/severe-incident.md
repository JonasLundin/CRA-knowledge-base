# Summary

Under Article 3(44) of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, a **severe incident** means an incident having an impact on the security of the product with digital elements and causing or capable of causing:
1. Severe operational disruption to the operations of the product with digital elements or to the provision of services by an entity using that product; or
2. Material or non-material damage to a natural or legal person.[^cra-art-3]

# Legal effect

Triggers mandatory statutory reporting under Article 14(3)–(4) to national CSIRTs and ENISA via the Single Reporting Platform.[^cra-art-3]

# Requirements or coverage

Applies to operational outages, compromised control systems, and widespread data breaches originating from a vulnerability or exploit within the product.[^cra-art-3]
