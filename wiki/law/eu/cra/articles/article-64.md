# Summary

Article 64 harmonises administrative financial penalties for CRA infringements, establishing three statutory fine ceilings based on the gravity of the breach.

# Legal effect

Establishes maximum administrative fine thresholds and obligates Member States to establish effective, proportionate, and dissuasive national penalty rules.

# Applicability

Applies to economic operators and open-source software stewards infringing CRA provisions.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 64 sets three statutory penalty tiers:
1. **Essential requirements and core obligations (Annex I, Articles 13 & 14)**:
   - Administrative fines up to **EUR 15 000 000** or, if the offender is an undertaking, up to **2.5 %** of its total worldwide annual turnover for the preceding financial year, whichever is higher.
2. **Other economic operator obligations (Articles 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 28, 30, 31, 32)**:
   - Administrative fines up to **EUR 10 000 000** or up to **2 %** of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
3. **Misleading or false information to notified bodies or authorities**:
   - Administrative fines up to **EUR 5 000 000** or up to **1 %** of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
- Member States must notify their penalty rules to the Commission by **11 December 2026** (24 months from entry into force).
- For open-source software stewards and SMEs, Member States must ensure penalties are proportionate and take account of financial capacity.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027. Member State notification of penalty rules due by 11 December 2026.
