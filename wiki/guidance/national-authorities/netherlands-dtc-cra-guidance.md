# Summary

The Dutch Digital Trust Center (DTC, part of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Climate Policy) and the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC-NL) publish official guidance and practical roadmaps for businesses in the Netherlands preparing for the Cyber Resilience Act [^dtc-cra-guide].

# Legal effect

This guidance is non-binding and educational [^dtc-cra-guide]. It provides practical compliance pathways for Dutch enterprises and does not alter the mandatory obligations under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to hardware and software developers, manufacturers, importers, and distributors active in the Dutch economy, with specific guidance for SMEs and supply-chain partners.

# Key topics or guidance coverage

The Dutch guidance structures practical implementation for companies [^dtc-cra-guide]:

- **Role Clarification across the Supply Chain:** Detailed breakdown distinguishing manufacturer duties (Article 13), importer verification duties (Article 19), and distributor due diligence duties (Article 21).
- **Vulnerability Handling and CSIRT-NL Interaction:** Procedures for establishing coordinated vulnerability disclosure (CVD) channels and understanding reporting routing to CSIRT-NL via the ENISA SRP.
- **SME Step-by-Step Implementation Roadmap:** Actionable multi-phase roadmap helping smaller businesses conduct product inventories, classify connected products, establish SBOM workflows, and prepare technical documentation.
- **Supply Chain Security & Software Sourcing:** Guidance on evaluating third-party components and ensuring supplier contracts align with CRA Annex I requirements.

# Dates and transitions

- **Publication Timeline:** Published in September 2025 and updated in May 2026.
