# Summary

`prEN 40000-1-4` (*Cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements — Part 1-4: Generic security requirements*) is the Type B horizontal draft European Standard developed by CEN-CLC/JTC 13 WG 9 (PT 3) under Standardisation Request M/606 Lines 2 through 14[^m606][^stan4cra-jtc13]. It provides a comprehensive horizontal catalogue of generic technical security requirements, security capabilities, and assurance controls corresponding to CRA Annex I Part I points 2(a) through 2(m)[^cen-wi-81344].

# Legal effect

This document is a draft European Standard (`prEN`). It has **not** been cited in the Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU) and confers **no** legal presumption of conformity under CRA Article 27 until formal adoption and OJEU publication.

# Applicability

Applies horizontally across all hardware and software products with digital elements. Manufacturers of default-category products (as well as Important/Critical products where a vertical standard is not yet available or where horizontal controls are referenced) use `prEN 40000-1-4` alongside the risk assessment methodology of `prEN 40000-1-2` to select and implement technical security controls satisfying CRA Annex I Part I point 2.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

`prEN 40000-1-4` translates each of the 13 CRA Annex I Part I point 2 essential cybersecurity requirements (M/606 Lines 2–14) into verifiable technical security controls:
- **Line 02 — Point 2(a)**: Delivery without known exploitable vulnerabilities;
- **Line 03 — Point 2(b)**: Secure default configurations and secure state reset mechanisms;
- **Line 04 — Point 2(c)**: Protection from unauthorised access via appropriate authentication, identity, and access management controls;
- **Line 05 — Point 2(d)**: Protection of the confidentiality of stored, transmitted, or otherwise processed data via state-of-the-art cryptography;
- **Line 06 — Point 2(e)**: Protection of the integrity of stored, transmitted, or processed data, commands, programs, and configurations;
- **Line 07 — Point 2(f)**: Data minimisation (processing only data adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the intended purpose);
- **Line 08 — Point 2(g)**: Protection of the availability of essential and basic functions, including resilience against Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks;
- **Line 09 — Point 2(h)**: Minimisation of negative impacts on the availability of services provided by other devices or networks;
- **Line 10 — Point 2(i)**: Attack surface limitation, including reducing external interfaces;
- **Line 11 — Point 2(j)**: Exploitation mitigation and incident impact containment mechanisms;
- **Line 12 — Point 2(k)**: Security-relevant internal activity recording, logging, and monitoring;
- **Line 13 — Point 2(l)**: Clear, easy-to-use, and permanent data removal and secure data transfer mechanisms;
- **Line 14 — Point 2(m)**: Secure and cryptographically verified update delivery, automatic update options, and update notifications.
