# CRA Obligations

Overview of compliance obligations, roles, and procedures established under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

## Structure

- [Scope & Exclusions](scope/index.md) — Products covered, commercial activity, exclusions, and modifications
- [Manufacturers](manufacturers/index.md) — Essential cybersecurity requirements, risk assessments, updates, and declarations
- [Importers & Distributors](importers-and-distributors/index.md) — Supply-chain due diligence and verification obligations
- [Open Source](open-source/index.md) — Open-source software stewards and non-commercial development
- [Reporting](reporting/index.md) — Article 14 reporting timelines (24h, 72h, 14d, 1 month) and Single Reporting Platform
- [Vulnerability Handling](vulnerability-handling/index.md) — CVD policies, SBOMs, and vulnerability management
- [Conformity Assessment](conformity-assessment/index.md) — Assessment modules (A, B+C, H) and notified bodies
- [Market Surveillance](market-surveillance/index.md) — Authority powers, corrective actions, and administrative fines
