# Summary

An authorised representative is any natural or legal person established within the European Union who has received a written mandate from a manufacturer to act on its behalf in relation to specified tasks under Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.[^cra-art-3] [^cra-art-15]

# Legal effect

Where a manufacturer based outside the Union appoints an authorised representative, the representative acts as the primary EU legal contact point for market surveillance authorities and can be held liable for administrative non-compliance within the scope of its mandate.[^cra-art-15]

# Applicability

Non-EU manufacturers placing products on the EU market may designate an authorised representative. (Appointment does not relieve the manufacturer of its core technical design and cybersecurity obligations).[^cra-art-15]

# Requirements or coverage

Under Article 15, the written mandate must allow the authorised representative to perform at least the following tasks:

1. **Keep documentation available:** Maintain the EU declaration of conformity and technical documentation at the disposal of market surveillance authorities for 10 years or the support period;
2. **Cooperate with authorities:** Provide market surveillance authorities, upon reasoned request, with all necessary information and documentation demonstrating conformity;
3. **Cooperate on risk elimination:** Cooperate with authorities on any corrective action taken to eliminate the cybersecurity risks posed by covered products;
4. **Mandate restrictions:** The mandate cannot delegate the drawing up of the technical documentation or the obligation to ensure the product is designed and developed in accordance with Annex I (Article 15(2)).[^cra-art-15]

# Dates and transitions

- Applies from 11 December 2027.
