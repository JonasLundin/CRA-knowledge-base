# Summary

Article 26 mandates the European Commission to publish official non-binding guidelines assisting economic operators and authorities with CRA implementation.

# Legal effect

Imposes a legal duty on the Commission to draft and update guidelines in consultation with stakeholders and expert groups.

# Applicability

Applies to the European Commission, with guidance directed to economic operators, open-source stewards, and national authorities.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 26 requires the Commission to publish guidelines covering:
- Clarification of scope and practical application of essential cybersecurity requirements;
- Determining the support period and criteria for lifetime expectations;
- Practical implementation for open-source software developers and open-source stewards;
- Guidance on substantial modification and component integration;
- Targeted guidance for microenterprises, SMEs, and start-ups.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2024 (preparation and publication of guidance).
