# Summary

EN 304 621 is an ETSI draft European Standard under Standardisation Request M/606 Line 21[^m606], specifying essential cybersecurity requirements for network management systems (NMS) under CRA Annex III Class I point 6[^etsi-docbox-621].

# Legal effect

This document is an enquiry draft. It has **not** been cited in the OJEU and confers **no** presumption of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2024/2847.

# Applicability

Applies to:
- Centralised and distributed network management software;
- SDN controllers and automated network orchestration platforms;
- Network monitoring and configuration management tools.

# Requirements and high-level coverage

EN 304 621 addresses:
- Secure management communication channels (SSH, TLS, SNMPv3 with authPriv, NETCONF/RESTCONF over TLS);
- Strict role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (MFA), and credential vaulting for managed nodes;
- Automated configuration change auditing, backup integrity, and unauthorized change detection;
- Segregation between management networks and data payload planes;
- High availability, failover resilience, and secure automated software patching.
