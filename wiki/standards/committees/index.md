# Technical Committees

Index of European Standardisation Organisation (ESO) technical committees responsible for developing harmonised standards under Standardisation Request M/606.

## Committees

- [CEN-CLC/JTC 13](cen-clc-jtc-13.md) — CEN-CENELEC Joint Technical Committee 13 (Cybersecurity and Data Protection), responsible for horizontal standards (Lines 1–15) via WG 9 and smart meter gateways (Line 40) via WG 6.
- [ETSI TC CYBER / WG EUSR](etsi-tc-cyber-eusr.md) — ETSI Technical Committee CYBER Working Group on EU Standardisation Requests, responsible for 17 vertical software and connected device deliverables (EN 304 series, Lines 17–27 and 31–36).
- [CEN/TC 224](cen-tc-224.md) — CEN Technical Committee 224, responsible for identity management and PAM (Line 16), hardware security boxes (Line 39), and smartcards/secure elements (Line 41).
- [CLC/TC 47X](clc-tc-47x.md) — CENELEC Technical Committee 47X (Semiconductor devices and trusted chips), responsible for microprocessors, MCUs, ASICs/FPGAs, and secure element platforms (Lines 28, 29, 37, 38, 41).
- [CLC/TC 65X](clc-tc-65x.md) — CENELEC Technical Committee 65X (Industrial-process measurement, control and automation), responsible for OT and industrial cybersecurity adaptations (Lines 20–22, 25, 27, 36).
