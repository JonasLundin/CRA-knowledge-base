# Summary

Article 18 sets out the rules for manufacturers appointing authorised representatives established in the Union to carry out specified compliance tasks.

# Legal effect

Binds authorised representatives to the terms of their written mandate and establishes direct regulatory responsibility before market surveillance authorities.

# Applicability

Applies to authorised representatives and manufacturers established outside the Union who designate them.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 18 provides:
- A manufacturer may appoint by written mandate an authorised representative established in the Union.
- **Mandatory mandate tasks**:
  - Keep the EU declaration of conformity and technical documentation at the disposal of market surveillance authorities for 10 years or the support period;
  - Provide market surveillance authorities with all necessary information and documentation upon reasoned request;
  - Cooperate with market surveillance authorities on corrective actions taken to eliminate cybersecurity risks.
- **Excluded tasks**: The mandate cannot delegate obligations relating to secure design, cybersecurity risk assessment, or drawing up technical documentation.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
