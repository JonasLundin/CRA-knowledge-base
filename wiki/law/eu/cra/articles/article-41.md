# Summary

Article 41 governs conditions under which a notified body may subcontract specific conformity assessment tasks or delegate them to a subsidiary.

# Legal effect

Maintains strict notified body liability for all subcontracted work and mandates client consent and technical qualification.

# Applicability

Applies to notified bodies, their subsidiaries, subcontractors, and assessed manufacturers.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 41 mandates:
- A notified body may subcontract specific tasks or have them carried out by a subsidiary only with the agreement of the client.
- The notified body must ensure the subcontractor or subsidiary meets the requirements of Article 39.
- Notified bodies retain full responsibility for tasks performed by subcontractors or subsidiaries.
- Must keep at the disposal of the notifying authority the relevant documents concerning the assessment of subcontractor qualifications and work performed.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 June 2026.
