# Summary

Article 30 prescribes the technical and visual rules for affixing the CE marking to products with digital elements, their packaging, or accompanying documentation.

# Legal effect

Imposes mandatory physical and digital labeling requirements for products placed on the Union market.

# Applicability

Applies to manufacturers and authorised representatives affixing CE markings.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 30 requires:
- The CE marking must be affixed visibly, legibly, and indelibly to the product with digital elements. Where not possible due to nature/size, it must be affixed to packaging or accompanying documentation.
- For purely digital software products, the CE marking must be affixed to the electronic declaration of conformity or displayed on the accompanying website/software documentation.
- Must be affixed before placing on the market and followed by the identification number of the notified body where involved in production control (Module H).

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
