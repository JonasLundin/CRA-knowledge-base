# ENISA CRA Guidance

This section catalogues official operational guidance, technical interface documentation, standards mappings, and explanatory materials published by the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA) supporting the implementation of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act).

ENISA guidance is non-binding and establishes operational, technical, and engineering best practices, in particular for the Single Reporting Platform (SRP) under Article 16.
