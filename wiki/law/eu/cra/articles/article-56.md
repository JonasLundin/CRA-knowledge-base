# Summary

Article 56 provides for coordinated Union-level investigations where products with digital elements present significant cross-border cybersecurity risks across multiple Member States.

# Legal effect

Enables the Commission to adopt implementing acts requiring economic operators to take corrective actions or product withdrawals Union-wide.

# Applicability

Applies to the Commission, national market surveillance authorities, ENISA, and economic operators.

# Requirements or coverage

Article 56 provides:
- The Commission, on its own initiative or following requests from market surveillance authorities, may initiate a Union-level evaluation of products presenting significant cross-border cybersecurity risks.
- Where a product presents a significant cybersecurity risk across multiple Member States, the Commission may adopt implementing acts requiring economic operators to take corrective measures, withdraw, or recall the product across the entire Union market.

# Dates and transitions

Applies from 11 December 2027.
